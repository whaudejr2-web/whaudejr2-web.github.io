How to say "Add a spoon of red pepper paste" in Korean (고추장 한 숟가락 넣어요)

You can say this

고추장 한 숟가락 넣어요.

gochujang han sutgarak neoeoyo

Meaning: Add a spoon of red pepper paste.

Good to know

The paste is fermented from chili, glutinous rice and soybeans, traditionally aged outdoors in clay pots. Supermarket tubs are red and print the heat level on the lid, from mild to very hot.
