How to say "This is the final boarding call. Please come to the gate right now. Airport announcement" in Korean (마지막 탑승 안내입니다. 지금 바로 탑승구로 와 주세요)

You can say this

마지막 탑승 안내입니다. 지금 바로 탑승구로 와 주세요.

majimak tapseung annaeimnida. jigeum baro tapseungguro wa juseyo

Meaning: This is the final boarding call. Please come to the gate right now. Airport announcement.
