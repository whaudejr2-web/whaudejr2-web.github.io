How to say "Do you have an alien registration card? Asked at the front desk" in Korean (외국인 등록증 있으세요?)

You can say this

외국인 등록증 있으세요?

oegugin deungnokjeung isseuseyo

Meaning: Do you have an alien registration card? Asked at the front desk.

Good to know

Foreigners staying in Korea six months or more are enrolled in National Health Insurance, and clinics look you up with your registration card. Short-term visitors pay full price, which is usually still modest.
