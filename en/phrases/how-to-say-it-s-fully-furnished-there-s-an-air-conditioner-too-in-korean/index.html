What does "풀옵션이에요. 에어컨도 있어요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

풀옵션이에요. 에어컨도 있어요.

pulopsyeonieyo. eeokeondo isseoyo

Meaning: It's fully furnished. There's an air conditioner too.
