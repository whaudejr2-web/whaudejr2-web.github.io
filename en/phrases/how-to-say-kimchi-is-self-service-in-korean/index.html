How to say "Kimchi is self-service" in Korean (김치는 셀프예요)

You can say this

김치는 셀프예요.

gimchineun selpeuyeyo

Meaning: Kimchi is self-service.

Good to know

Noodle shops usually set out a big tub of freshly seasoned kimchi. Take a small dish and scoop as much as you like; it is free, and refills are expected.
