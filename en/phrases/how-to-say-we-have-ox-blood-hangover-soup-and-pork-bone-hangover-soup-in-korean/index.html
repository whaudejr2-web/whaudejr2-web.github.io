How to say "We have ox blood hangover soup and pork bone hangover soup" in Korean (선지해장국이랑 뼈해장국 있어요)

You can say this

선지해장국이랑 뼈해장국 있어요.

seonjihaejanggugirang ppyeohaejangguk isseoyo

Meaning: We have ox blood hangover soup and pork bone hangover soup.
