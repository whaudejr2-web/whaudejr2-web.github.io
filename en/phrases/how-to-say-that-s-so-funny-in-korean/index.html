How to say "That's so funny" in Korean (진짜 웃겨요)

You can say this

진짜 웃겨요.

jinjja utgyeoyo

Meaning: That's so funny.

Good to know

In chat, Koreans type laughter as a row of the Korean consonant letter for the k sound, which reads like a giggle. More letters mean a bigger laugh, and a single one can come across as cold.
