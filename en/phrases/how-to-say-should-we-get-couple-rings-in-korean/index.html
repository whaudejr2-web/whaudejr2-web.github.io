How to say "Should we get couple rings?" in Korean (커플링 맞출까요?)

You can say this

커플링 맞출까요?

keopeulling matchulkkayo

Meaning: Should we get couple rings?

Good to know

Matching couple rings are common in Korea and do not mean engagement. Couples also wear matching outfits, phone cases and sneakers.
