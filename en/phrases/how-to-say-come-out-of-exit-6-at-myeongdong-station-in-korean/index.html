How to say "Come out of exit 6 at Myeongdong Station" in Korean (명동역 6번 출구로 나오세요)

You can say this

명동역 6번 출구로 나오세요.

myeongdongnyeok yuk beon chulguro naoseyo

Meaning: Come out of exit 6 at Myeongdong Station.
