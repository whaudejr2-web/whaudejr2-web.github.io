How to say "I bought a gold ring as a first birthday gift" in Korean (돌 선물로 금반지 샀어요)

You can say this

돌 선물로 금반지 샀어요.

dol seonmullo geumbanji sasseoyo

Meaning: I bought a gold ring as a first birthday gift.

Good to know

A gold ring of one don, about 3.75 grams, is the classic first birthday gift. With gold prices high, many people now give cash or a half-don ring instead.
