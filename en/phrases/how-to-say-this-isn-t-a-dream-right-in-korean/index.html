How to say "This isn't a dream, right?" in Korean (꿈 아니죠?)

You can say this

꿈 아니죠?

kkum anijyo

Meaning: This isn't a dream, right?

Good to know

죠 at the end asks the other person to confirm, like right? at the end of an English sentence.
