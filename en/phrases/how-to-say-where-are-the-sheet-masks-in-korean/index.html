How to say "Where are the sheet masks?" in Korean (마스크팩은 어디 있어요?)

You can say this

마스크팩은 어디 있어요?

maseukeupaegeun eodi isseoyo

Meaning: Where are the sheet masks?

Good to know

In Korea sheet masks are called mask packs and are sold by the box of ten or more. Leave one on for fifteen to twenty minutes and pat in the leftover serum instead of rinsing.
