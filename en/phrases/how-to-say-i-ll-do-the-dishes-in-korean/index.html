How to say "I'll do the dishes" in Korean (설거지는 제가 할게요)

You can say this

설거지는 제가 할게요.

seolgeojineun jega halgeyo

Meaning: I'll do the dishes.

Good to know

Korean sinks have a small strainer basket in the drain for food scraps, because food waste is collected separately and residents pay by weight or by special bag.
