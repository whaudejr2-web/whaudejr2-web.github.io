How to say "Hello, keep it up. The greeting hikers exchange on the trail" in Korean (수고하십니다)

You can say this

수고하십니다.

sugohasimnida

Meaning: Hello, keep it up. The greeting hikers exchange on the trail.

Good to know

Hikers passing on Korean trails greet each other, and this phrase, literally you are working hard, is the classic one. On a weekend you will say it and hear it many times before the top.
