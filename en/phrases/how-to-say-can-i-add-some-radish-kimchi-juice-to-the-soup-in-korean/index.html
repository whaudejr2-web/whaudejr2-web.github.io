How to say "Can I add some radish kimchi juice to the soup?" in Korean (깍두기 국물 좀 넣어도 돼요?)

You can say this

깍두기 국물 좀 넣어도 돼요?

kkakdugi gungmul jom neoeodo dwaeyo

Meaning: Can I add some radish kimchi juice to the soup?

Good to know

Pouring the red juice from cubed radish kimchi into beef bone soup is a classic local move. It adds tang and a little heat to the mild broth.
