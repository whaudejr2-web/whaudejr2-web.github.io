How to say "I purple you means let's love each other for a long, long time" in Korean (보라해는 오래오래 사랑하자는 뜻이에요)

You can say this

보라해는 오래오래 사랑하자는 뜻이에요.

borahaeneun oraeorae saranghajaneun tteusieyo

Meaning: I purple you means let's love each other for a long, long time.
