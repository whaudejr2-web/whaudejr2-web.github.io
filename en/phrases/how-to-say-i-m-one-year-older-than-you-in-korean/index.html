How to say "I'm one year older than you" in Korean (제가 한 살 더 많아요)

You can say this

제가 한 살 더 많아요.

jega han sal deo manayo

Meaning: I'm one year older than you.

Good to know

Even a one-year gap matters. The older person usually switches to casual speech first, while the younger one stays polite until invited to drop it.
