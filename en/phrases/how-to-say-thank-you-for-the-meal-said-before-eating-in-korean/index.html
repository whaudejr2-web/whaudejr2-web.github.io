How to say "Thank you for the meal, said before eating" in Korean (잘 먹겠습니다)

You can say this

잘 먹겠습니다.

jal meokgetseumnida

Meaning: Thank you for the meal, said before eating.

Good to know

Literally: I will eat well. Koreans say it before a meal to the host or the cook, and after eating they say a matching phrase meaning I ate well.
