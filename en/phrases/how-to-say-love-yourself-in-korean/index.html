How to say "Love yourself" in Korean (자기 자신을 사랑하세요)

You can say this

자기 자신을 사랑하세요.

jagi jasineul saranghaseyo

Meaning: Love yourself.

Good to know

In 2018 RM spoke at the United Nations for a UNICEF youth campaign and asked young people to speak themselves, a message tied to the Love Yourself albums.
