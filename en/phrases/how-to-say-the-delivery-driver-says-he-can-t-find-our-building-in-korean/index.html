How to say "The delivery driver says he can't find our building" in Korean (배달 기사님이 우리 동을 못 찾으신대요)

You can say this

배달 기사님이 우리 동을 못 찾으신대요.

baedal gisanimi uri dongeul mot chajeusindaeyo

Meaning: The delivery driver says he can't find our building.
