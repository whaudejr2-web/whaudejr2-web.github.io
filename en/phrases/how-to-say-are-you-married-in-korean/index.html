How to say "Are you married?" in Korean (결혼하셨어요?)

You can say this

결혼하셨어요?

gyeolhonhasyeosseoyo

Meaning: Are you married?

Good to know

Koreans marry late: in 2024 the average age at first marriage was about thirty-four for men and thirty-two for women. The question is normal small talk, and a simple not yet is a complete answer.
