How to say "Really?" in Korean (진짜요?)

You can say this

진짜요?

jinjjayo

Meaning: Really?

Good to know

Korean has two common words for really. This one is the casual, everyday choice among friends, while the other sounds a bit more formal and is the one you usually see in textbooks.
