How to say "Thank you, I enjoyed it" in Korean (잘 마셨습니다)

You can say this

잘 마셨습니다.

jal masyeotseumnida

Meaning: Thank you, I enjoyed it.

Good to know

Literally: I drank well. Saying it on the way out is a small courtesy that makes staff smile.
