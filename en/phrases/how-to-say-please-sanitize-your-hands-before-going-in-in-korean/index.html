How to say "Please sanitize your hands before going in" in Korean (들어가기 전에 손 소독해 주세요)

You can say this

들어가기 전에 손 소독해 주세요.

deureogagi jeone son sodokae juseyo

Meaning: Please sanitize your hands before going in.
