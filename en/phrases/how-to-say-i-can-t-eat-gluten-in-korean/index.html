How to say "I can't eat gluten" in Korean (글루텐 못 먹어요)

You can say this

글루텐 못 먹어요.

geulluten mon meogeoyo

Meaning: I can't eat gluten.

Good to know

Korean soy sauce and most commercial soybean paste contain wheat, so gluten hides in many sauces and stews. Rice, rice cakes and glass noodles made from sweet potato starch are safer choices.
