What does "시급은 만 삼백이십 원이에요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

시급은 만 삼백이십 원이에요.

sigeubeun man sambaegisip wonieyo

Meaning: It's ten thousand three hundred twenty won an hour.

Good to know

Korea's legal minimum wage for 2026 is 10,320 won an hour, and it applies to part-timers and foreign workers alike. The figure is reset every year, so check the current rate before you agree to a wage.
