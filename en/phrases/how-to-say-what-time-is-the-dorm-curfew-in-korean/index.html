How to say "What time is the dorm curfew?" in Korean (기숙사 통금이 몇 시예요?)

You can say this

기숙사 통금이 몇 시예요?

gisuksa tonggeumi myeot siyeyo

Meaning: What time is the dorm curfew?

Good to know

Many university dorms still lock the doors around midnight or one a.m. Coming in late earns demerit points, and too many points can cost you your room the next semester.
