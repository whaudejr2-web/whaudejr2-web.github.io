How to say "Um, hold on a second" in Korean (음, 잠깐만요)

You can say this

음, 잠깐만요.

eum jamkkanmanyo

Meaning: Um, hold on a second.

Good to know

Koreans hum this sound while thinking, often with the mouth closed, instead of the English um. Stretching it longer simply signals that you need more time.
