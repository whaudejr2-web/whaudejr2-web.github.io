How to say "It's the bottom of the ninth, I'm so nervous" in Korean (구 회 말이에요. 너무 떨려요)

You can say this

gu hoe marieyo. neomu tteollyeoyo

Meaning: It's the bottom of the ninth, I'm so nervous.
