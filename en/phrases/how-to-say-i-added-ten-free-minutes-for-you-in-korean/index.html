What does "서비스로 십 분 더 넣어 드렸어요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

서비스로 십 분 더 넣어 드렸어요.

seobiseuro sip bun deo neoeo deuryeosseoyo

Meaning: I added ten free minutes for you.

Good to know

Free extra minutes are called service, and staff add them generously on quiet nights. The remaining time shows in the corner of the screen, so keep an eye on it.
