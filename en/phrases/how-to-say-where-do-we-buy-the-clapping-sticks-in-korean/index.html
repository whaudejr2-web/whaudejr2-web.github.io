How to say "Where do we buy the clapping sticks?" in Korean (막대풍선은 어디서 사요?)

You can say this

막대풍선은 어디서 사요?

makdaepungseoneun eodiseo sayo

Meaning: Where do we buy the clapping sticks?

Good to know

These long inflatable balloons, banged together in rhythm, became popular in Korean ballparks in the 1990s and later spread to stadiums around the world.
