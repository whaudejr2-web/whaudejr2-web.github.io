How to say "Don't count your chickens before they hatch. Literally, don't drink the kimchi soup first" in Korean (김칫국부터 마시지 마세요)

You can say this

김칫국부터 마시지 마세요.

gimchitgukbuteo masiji maseyo

Meaning: Don't count your chickens before they hatch. Literally, don't drink the kimchi soup first.

Good to know

From an old saying about someone who drinks cold kimchi soup, expecting rice cake that never comes. That soup was drunk with rice cake to help it go down.
