How to say "Thank you, that was a great meal" in Korean (잘 먹었습니다)

You can say this

잘 먹었습니다.

jal meogeotseumnida

Meaning: Thank you, that was a great meal.

Good to know

Literally: I ate well. Koreans say it after a meal to whoever cooked or paid, and also to restaurant staff on the way out. It is the standard thank you for food.
