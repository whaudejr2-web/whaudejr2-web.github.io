How to say "More wasabi and soy sauce, please" in Korean (와사비하고 간장 좀 더 주세요)

You can say this

와사비하고 간장 좀 더 주세요.

wasabihago ganjang jom deo juseyo

Meaning: More wasabi and soy sauce, please.
