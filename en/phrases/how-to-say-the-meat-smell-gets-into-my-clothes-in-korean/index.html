How to say "The meat smell gets into my clothes" in Korean (고기 냄새가 옷에 배요)

You can say this

고기 냄새가 옷에 배요.

gogi naemsaega ose baeyo

Meaning: The meat smell gets into my clothes.

Good to know

Barbecue places know the smell sticks to clothes. Most hand out big plastic bags for coats and keep a fabric deodorizer spray by the door for customers to use on the way out.
