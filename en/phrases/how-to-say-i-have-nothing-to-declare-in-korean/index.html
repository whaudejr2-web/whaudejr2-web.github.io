How to say "I have nothing to declare" in Korean (신고할 물건 없어요)

You can say this

신고할 물건 없어요.

singohal mulgeon eopseoyo

Meaning: I have nothing to declare.

Good to know

Since May 2023, travelers with nothing to declare no longer need to fill out the paper customs form. Just walk through the green lane marked nothing to declare.
