What does "네, 안주는 하나 꼭 시키셔야 돼요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

네, 안주는 하나 꼭 시키셔야 돼요.

ne anjuneun hana kkok sikisyeoya dwaeyo

Meaning: Yes, you have to order at least one dish.

Good to know

Korean bars expect every table to order at least one food dish with drinks. Drinking without food is seen as unhealthy, and many places refuse drinks-only orders.
