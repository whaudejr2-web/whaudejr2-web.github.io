How to say "Seoul is hectic. On the other hand, Busan is laid-back" in Korean (서울은 복잡해요. 반면에 부산은 여유로워요)

You can say this

서울은 복잡해요. 반면에 부산은 여유로워요.

seoureun bokjapaeyo. banmyeone busaneun yeoyurowoyo

Meaning: Seoul is hectic. On the other hand, Busan is laid-back.
