How to say "Is this a hot noodle dish or a cold one?" in Korean (이건 따뜻한 국수예요, 차가운 국수예요?)

You can say this

이건 따뜻한 국수예요, 차가운 국수예요?

igeon ttatteutan guksuyeyo chagaun guksuyeyo

Meaning: Is this a hot noodle dish or a cold one?
