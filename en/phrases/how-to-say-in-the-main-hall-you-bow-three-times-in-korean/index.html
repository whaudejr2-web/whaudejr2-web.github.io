What does "법당에서는 세 번 절해요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

법당에서는 세 번 절해요.

beopdangeseoneun se beon jeolhaeyo

Meaning: In the main hall, you bow three times.

Good to know

The three full bows in the main hall honor the Buddha, his teachings, and the community of monks. Knees, elbows, and forehead all touch the floor, with the palms turned up.
