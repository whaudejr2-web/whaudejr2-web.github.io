How to say "Is there a restroom nearby?" in Korean (근처에 화장실 있어요?)

You can say this

근처에 화장실 있어요?

geuncheoe hwajangsil isseoyo

Meaning: Is there a restroom nearby?

Good to know

Every Seoul subway station has free public restrooms, usually just outside the ticket gates. Big department stores and coffee chains are the other easy options when you are out walking.
