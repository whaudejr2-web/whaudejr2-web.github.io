How to say "Where do I get the tax refund?" in Korean (택스 리펀은 어디서 받아요?)

You can say this

택스 리펀은 어디서 받아요?

taekseu ripeoneun eodiseo badayo

Meaning: Where do I get the tax refund?

Good to know

Above the in-store limit you get a refund form instead. At Incheon Airport, scan it at a refund kiosk before check-in, show the goods at customs if the kiosk asks, then collect the money after immigration.
