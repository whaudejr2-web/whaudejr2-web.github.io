How to say "Get the radish kimchi and cabbage kimchi from over there" in Korean (깍두기랑 김치는 저기서 가져다 드세요)

You can say this

깍두기랑 김치는 저기서 가져다 드세요.

kkakdugirang gimchineun jeogiseo gajyeoda deuseyo

Meaning: Get the radish kimchi and cabbage kimchi from over there.
