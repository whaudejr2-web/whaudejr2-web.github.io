How to say "Sorry, I spilled the drink" in Korean (음료를 쏟아서 죄송해요)

You can say this

음료를 쏟아서 죄송해요.

eumnyoreul ssodaseo joesonghaeyo

Meaning: Sorry, I spilled the drink.
