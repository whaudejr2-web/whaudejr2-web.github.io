How to say "Will you marry me?" in Korean (저랑 결혼해 줄래요?)

You can say this

저랑 결혼해 줄래요?

jeorang gyeolhonhae jullaeyo

Meaning: Will you marry me?

Good to know

해 줄래요 asks someone to do something for you, so the proposal sounds like: will you do me the favor of marrying me?
