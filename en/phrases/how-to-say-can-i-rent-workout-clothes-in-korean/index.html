How to say "Can I rent workout clothes?" in Korean (운동복 대여 돼요?)

You can say this

운동복 대여 돼요?

undongbok daeyeo dwaeyo

Meaning: Can I rent workout clothes?

Good to know

Most gyms rent a set of workout clothes and a towel for a small monthly fee, so locals often arrive with nothing but their shoes.
