How to say "Take your penalty" in Korean (벌칙 받으세요)

You can say this

벌칙 받으세요.

beolchik badeuseyo

Meaning: Take your penalty.

Good to know

The penalty game for the loser. Classics are a forehead flick, a shot of wasabi or a bucket of cold water, and the caption counts it down. Nobody is ever really hurt, which is the point.
