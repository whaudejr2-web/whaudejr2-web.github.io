How to say "Can you see the fireworks well from here?" in Korean (여기서 불꽃놀이 잘 보여요?)

You can say this

여기서 불꽃놀이 잘 보여요?

yeogiseo bulkkonnori jal boyeoyo

Meaning: Can you see the fireworks well from here?
