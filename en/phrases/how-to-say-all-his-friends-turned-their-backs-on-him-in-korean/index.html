How to say "All his friends turned their backs on him" in Korean (친구들이 다 등을 돌렸어요)

You can say this

친구들이 다 등을 돌렸어요.

chingudeuri da deungeul dollyeosseoyo

Meaning: All his friends turned their backs on him.
