How to say "I'm from the Dominican Republic" in Korean (저는 도미니카 공화국에서 왔어요)

You can say this

저는 도미니카 공화국에서 왔어요.

jeoneun dominika gonghwagugeseo wasseoyo

Meaning: I'm from the Dominican Republic.

Good to know

공화국 means republic, so this is literally Dominica Republic.
