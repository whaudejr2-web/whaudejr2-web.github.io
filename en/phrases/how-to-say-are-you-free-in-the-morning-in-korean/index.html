How to say "Are you free in the morning?" in Korean (오전에 시간 있어요?)

You can say this

오전에 시간 있어요?

ojeone sigan isseoyo

Meaning: Are you free in the morning?

Good to know

Koreans split the day at noon into before noon and after noon, and use a twelve hour clock in conversation. Train tickets and schedules, however, are printed in twenty four hour time.
