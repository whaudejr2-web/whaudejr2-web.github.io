How to say "I'm twenty-five this year" in Korean (올해 스물다섯 살이에요)

You can say this

올해 스물다섯 살이에요.

olhae seumuldaseot sarieyo

Meaning: I'm twenty-five this year.

Good to know

Since June 2023, Korea uses international age for all legal purposes, but many people still answer with the old Korean count, which can be one or two years higher. Ask for international age if it matters.
