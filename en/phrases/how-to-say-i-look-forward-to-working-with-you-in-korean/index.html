How to say "I look forward to working with you" in Korean (앞으로 잘 부탁드립니다)

You can say this

앞으로 잘 부탁드립니다.

apeuro jal butakdeurimnida

Meaning: I look forward to working with you.

Good to know

Literally: please take good care of me from now on. It is the standard line when you join a team, and coworkers usually answer with the same words.
