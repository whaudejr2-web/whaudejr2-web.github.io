How to say "The ads are so long" in Korean (광고가 너무 길어요)

You can say this

광고가 너무 길어요.

gwanggoga neomu gireoyo

Meaning: The ads are so long.

Good to know

Korean multiplexes run about ten minutes of ads after the printed start time. Consumer groups filed complaints against the big chains over it in 2017, so arriving a few minutes late rarely matters.
