What does "안약 처방해 드릴게요. 하루 세 번 넣으세요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

안약 처방해 드릴게요. 하루 세 번 넣으세요.

anyak cheobanghae deurilgeyo. haru se beon neoeuseyo

Meaning: I'll prescribe eye drops. Use them three times a day.
