What does "전화번호만 입력하면 돼요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

전화번호만 입력하면 돼요.

jeonhwabeonhoman imnyeokamyeon dwaeyo

Meaning: Just enter your phone number.

Good to know

Most PC bangs register you by phone number at a kiosk by the door. Members pay a lower hourly rate, and any unused time stays on your account for the next visit.
