What does "알레르기성 결막염이에요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

알레르기성 결막염이에요.

allereugiseong gyeolmangnyeomieyo

Meaning: It's allergic conjunctivitis.

Good to know

Spring in Korea brings yellow dust from the Gobi and heavy fine dust days, and eye clinics fill up with itchy, red eyes. Check the air quality app and wear glasses instead of contacts on bad days.
