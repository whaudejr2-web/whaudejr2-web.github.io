What does "허리를 펴고 숨을 천천히 쉬세요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

허리를 펴고 숨을 천천히 쉬세요.

heorireul pyeogo sumeul cheoncheonhi swiseyo

Meaning: Straighten your back and breathe slowly.
