What does "신분증 보여 주세요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

신분증 보여 주세요.

sinbunjeung boyeo juseyo

Meaning: Please show me your ID.

Good to know

The name on your ID has to match the booking exactly, and a passport is what staff expect from foreign fans. Without it, the booth can refuse to hand over the ticket even if you already paid.
