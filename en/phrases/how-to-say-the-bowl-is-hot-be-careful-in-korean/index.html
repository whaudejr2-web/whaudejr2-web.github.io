How to say "The bowl is hot. Be careful" in Korean (뚝배기 뜨거워요. 조심하세요)

You can say this

뚝배기 뜨거워요. 조심하세요.

ttukbaegi tteugeowoyo josimhaseyo

Meaning: The bowl is hot. Be careful.

Good to know

Soup comes bubbling in a black earthenware bowl straight off the fire. Don't touch the rim; the thick clay keeps the soup hot down to the last spoonful.
