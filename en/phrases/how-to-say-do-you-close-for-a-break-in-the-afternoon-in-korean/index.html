How to say "Do you close for a break in the afternoon?" in Korean (브레이크 타임 있어요?)

You can say this

브레이크 타임 있어요?

beureikeu taim isseoyo

Meaning: Do you close for a break in the afternoon?

Good to know

Many Korean restaurants close between lunch and dinner, often from three to five, and put up a sign saying break time in English letters. Check before you go in the mid afternoon.
