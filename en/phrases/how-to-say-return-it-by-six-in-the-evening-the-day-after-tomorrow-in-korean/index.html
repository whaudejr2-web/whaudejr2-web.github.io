What does "모레 오후 여섯 시까지 반납하시면 돼요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

모레 오후 여섯 시까지 반납하시면 돼요.

more ohu yeoseot sikkaji bannapasimyeon dwaeyo

Meaning: Return it by six in the evening the day after tomorrow.
