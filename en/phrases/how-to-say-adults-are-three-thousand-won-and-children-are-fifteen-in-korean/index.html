What does "어른은 삼천 원, 어린이는 천오백 원이에요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

어른은 삼천 원, 어린이는 천오백 원이에요.

eoreuneun samcheon won eorinineun cheonobaek wonieyo

Meaning: Adults are three thousand won and children are fifteen hundred.
