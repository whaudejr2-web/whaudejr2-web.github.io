How to say "Can the jjamppong broth be made not spicy?" in Korean (짬뽕 국물 안 맵게 돼요?)

You can say this

짬뽕 국물 안 맵게 돼요?

jjamppong gungmul an maepge dwaeyo

Meaning: Can the jjamppong broth be made not spicy?
