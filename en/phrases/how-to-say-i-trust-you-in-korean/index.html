What does "과인이 그대를 믿소." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

과인이 그대를 믿소.

gwaini geudaereul mitso

Meaning: I trust you.

Good to know

Joseon kings referred to themselves with a humble word that literally means a person of little virtue; emperors used a grander one. Today everyone just uses the ordinary polite I, so never copy this one.
