How to say "Awesome" in Korean (대박)

You can say this

대박!

daebak

Meaning: Awesome!

Good to know

Literally a jackpot or a huge hit. Since the late 2000s it has become the all-purpose exclamation for anything amazing, and it appears in almost every drama and variety show.
