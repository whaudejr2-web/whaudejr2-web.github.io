How to say "This is so touching, I'm crying" in Korean (너무 감동이에요. 눈물 나요)

You can say this

너무 감동이에요. 눈물 나요.

neomu gamdongieyo. nunmul nayo

Meaning: This is so touching, I'm crying.

Good to know

Crying is typed with the Korean vowel that looks like two streams of tears. It covers sad, touched, jealous and simply frustrated, and goes after almost anything.
