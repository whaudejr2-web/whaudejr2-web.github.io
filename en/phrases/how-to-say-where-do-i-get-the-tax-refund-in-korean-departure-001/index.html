How to say "Where do I get the tax refund?" in Korean (세금 환급은 어디서 해요?)

You can say this

세금 환급은 어디서 해요?

segeum hwangeubeun eodiseo haeyo

Meaning: Where do I get the tax refund?

Good to know

The refund kiosks are in the check-in hall, before security. Scan your passport and the refund receipts; if a kiosk tells you to see customs, show the goods there before you check your suitcase.
