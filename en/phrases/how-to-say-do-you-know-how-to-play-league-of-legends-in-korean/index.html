How to say "Do you know how to play League of Legends?" in Korean (롤 할 줄 알아요?)

You can say this

롤 할 줄 알아요?

rol hal jul arayo

Meaning: Do you know how to play League of Legends?

Good to know

League of Legends has topped Korean PC bang play charts for most of the years since 2012. Many pro players, including Faker, grew up practicing in rooms like this.
