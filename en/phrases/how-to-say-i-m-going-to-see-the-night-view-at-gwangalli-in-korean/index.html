How to say "I'm going to see the night view at Gwangalli" in Korean (광안리 야경 보러 갈 거예요)

You can say this

광안리 야경 보러 갈 거예요.

gwangalli yagyeong boreo gal geoyeyo

Meaning: I'm going to see the night view at Gwangalli.
