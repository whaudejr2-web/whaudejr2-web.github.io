How to say "I'll set the table" in Korean (상 차릴게요)

You can say this

상 차릴게요.

sang charilgeyo

Meaning: I'll set the table.

Good to know

On a Korean table the rice bowl goes on the left, the soup on the right, and the spoon and chopsticks sit together on the right side. Side dishes go in the middle and are shared.
