How to say "Please send this letter to Mexico" in Korean (이 편지 멕시코로 보내 주세요)

You can say this

이 편지 멕시코로 보내 주세요.

i pyeonji meksikoro bonae juseyo

Meaning: Please send this letter to Mexico.

Good to know

For international mail, write the address in English with the destination country in capital letters on the last line, and your own Korean address as the sender. The clerk weighs the letter and adds the postage.
