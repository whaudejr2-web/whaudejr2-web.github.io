What does "멜젓에 찍어 드세요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

멜젓에 찍어 드세요.

meljeose jjigeo deuseyo

Meaning: Dip it in the anchovy sauce.

Good to know

Jeju black pigs are a native breed with black hair. The pork is grilled in thick slices and dipped in a salty fermented anchovy sauce that is warmed on the grill, instead of plain salt.
