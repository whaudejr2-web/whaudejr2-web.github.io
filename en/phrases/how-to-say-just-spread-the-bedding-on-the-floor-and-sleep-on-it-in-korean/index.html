How to say "Just spread the bedding on the floor and sleep on it" in Korean (이불은 바닥에 깔고 주무시면 돼요)

You can say this

이불은 바닥에 깔고 주무시면 돼요.

ibureun badage kkalgo jumusimyeon dwaeyo

Meaning: Just spread the bedding on the floor and sleep on it.

Good to know

Traditional Korean bedding is a thin padded mattress and a quilt laid directly on the heated floor at night and folded into a closet in the morning. Hosts will show you where it is kept.
