How to say "The bathroom is shared. It's across the courtyard" in Korean (화장실은 같이 쓰세요. 마당 건너편에 있어요)

You can say this

화장실은 같이 쓰세요. 마당 건너편에 있어요.

hwajangsireun gachi sseuseyo madang geonneopyeone isseoyo

Meaning: The bathroom is shared. It's across the courtyard.
