What does "네, 가리비에 치즈 올려 드릴게요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

네, 가리비에 치즈 올려 드릴게요.

ne garibie chijeu ollyeo deurilgeyo

Meaning: Sure, we'll put cheese on the scallops.
