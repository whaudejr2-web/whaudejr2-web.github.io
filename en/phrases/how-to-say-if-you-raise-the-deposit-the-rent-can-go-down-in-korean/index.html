What does "보증금을 올리면 월세를 줄일 수 있어요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

보증금을 올리면 월세를 줄일 수 있어요.

bojeunggeumeul ollimyeon wolsereul juril su isseoyo

Meaning: If you raise the deposit, the rent can go down.

Good to know

Deposit and rent trade off against each other: adding ten million won to the deposit typically cuts the rent by roughly forty to fifty thousand won a month, at a legally capped rate. Ask the agent to show both options.
