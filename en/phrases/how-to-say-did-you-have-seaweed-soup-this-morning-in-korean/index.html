How to say "Did you have seaweed soup this morning?" in Korean (아침에 미역국 먹었어요?)

You can say this

아침에 미역국 먹었어요?

achime miyeokguk meogeosseoyo

Meaning: Did you have seaweed soup this morning?

Good to know

Koreans eat seaweed soup on their birthday because mothers traditionally eat it after giving birth. Asking this is a warm, indirect way of saying happy birthday.
