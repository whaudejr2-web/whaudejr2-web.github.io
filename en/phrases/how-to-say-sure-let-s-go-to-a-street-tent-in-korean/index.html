How to say "Sure. Let's go to a street tent" in Korean (좋아요. 포장마차 가요)

You can say this

좋아요. 포장마차 가요.

joayo pojangmacha gayo

Meaning: Sure. Let's go to a street tent.

Good to know

Street tents are the orange or green plastic tents that open on sidewalks at night, serving soju with simple hot dishes. Many now operate as fixed stalls, but the name stuck.
