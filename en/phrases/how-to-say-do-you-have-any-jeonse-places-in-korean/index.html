How to say "Do you have any jeonse places?" in Korean (전세도 있어요?)

You can say this

전세도 있어요?

jeonsedo isseoyo

Meaning: Do you have any jeonse places?

Good to know

This is Korea's unique rental system: you hand the landlord a huge lump sum, often half the home's value, pay no monthly rent, and get the whole sum back when you leave. Most newcomers choose monthly rent instead.
