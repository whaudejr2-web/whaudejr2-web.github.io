How to say "We will repay you with even better music" in Korean (앞으로 더 좋은 음악으로 보답하겠습니다)

You can say this

앞으로 더 좋은 음악으로 보답하겠습니다.

apeuro deo joeun eumageuro bodapagetseumnida

Meaning: We will repay you with even better music.

Good to know

Promising to repay the fans with better music is one of the most common closing lines in K-pop acceptance speeches.
