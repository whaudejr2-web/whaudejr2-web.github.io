What does "편의점에서 충전할 수 있어요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

편의점에서 충전할 수 있어요.

pyeonuijeomeseo chungjeonhal su isseoyo

Meaning: You can top up at a convenience store.

Good to know

Prepaid top-ups are sold at convenience store counters: say the carrier name and the amount, pay, and get a voucher code to enter on your phone. The carrier app and website also take a card.
