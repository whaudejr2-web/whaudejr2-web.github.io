How to say "Don't go" in Korean (가지 마세요)

You can say this

가지 마세요.

gaji maseyo

Meaning: Don't go.

Good to know

The casual form, with the polite ending dropped, is usually said while grabbing someone's wrist. That wrist grab is such a drama cliché that Korean fans joke about it.
