How to say "Could I have scissors? I'll cut them myself" in Korean (가위 좀 주세요. 제가 자를게요)

You can say this

가위 좀 주세요. 제가 자를게요.

gawi jom juseyo jega jareulgeyo

Meaning: Could I have scissors? I'll cut them myself.
