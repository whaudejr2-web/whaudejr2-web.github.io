How to say "Hello, teacher" in Korean (안녕하세요, 선생님)

You can say this

안녕하세요, 선생님!

annyeonghaseyo seonsaengnim

Meaning: Hello, teacher!

Good to know

Students never call a teacher by name. The same title is also the polite way to address doctors, hairdressers and older colleagues, so you will hear it everywhere.
