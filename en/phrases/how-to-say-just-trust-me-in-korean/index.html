How to say "Just trust me" in Korean (저만 믿어요)

You can say this

저만 믿어요.

jeoman mideoyo

Meaning: Just trust me.

Good to know

Literally: believe only me. The hero says it right before saving the day, so it sounds confident and a little cheesy.
