What does "쓰레기는 가지고 내려가세요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

쓰레기는 가지고 내려가세요.

sseuregineun gajigo naeryeogaseyo

Meaning: Take your trash down with you.

Good to know

Korean national parks have no trash cans on the trails on purpose; you carry everything back down. Rangers do check, and littering can cost a fine.
