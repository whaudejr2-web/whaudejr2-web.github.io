How to say "I'm an ARMY" in Korean (저는 아미예요)

You can say this

저는 아미예요.

jeoneun amiyeyo

Meaning: I'm an ARMY.

Good to know

BTS announced the fan name ARMY in 2013. It stands for Adorable Representative M.C. for Youth, and the fans are also the band's army.
