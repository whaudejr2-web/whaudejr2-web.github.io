What does "밥은 말아 드릴까요, 따로 드릴까요?" mean in Korean?

You will hear this

밥은 말아 드릴까요, 따로 드릴까요?

babeun mara deurilkkayo ttaro deurilkkayo

Meaning: Rice in the soup, or on the side?

Good to know

Rice served on the side has its own name and is said to come from Daegu in the 1940s, when older customers refused to have rice dunked into their soup. Many diners now ask which you prefer.
