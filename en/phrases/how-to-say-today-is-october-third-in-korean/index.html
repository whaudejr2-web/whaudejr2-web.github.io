How to say "Today is October third" in Korean (오늘은 시월 삼 일이에요)

You can say this

오늘은 시월 삼 일이에요.

oneureun siwol sam irieyo

Meaning: Today is October third.

Good to know

October third is National Foundation Day, a public holiday that marks the legendary founding of the first Korean kingdom in 2333 BC. Shops stay open, but banks and offices close.
