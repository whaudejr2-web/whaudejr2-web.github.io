How to say "Your Korean is good" in Korean (한국어 잘하시네요)

You can say this

한국어 잘하시네요.

hangugeo jalhasineyo

Meaning: Your Korean is good.

Good to know

You will hear this compliment often, even after just a few words. A modest answer like the next phrase is the usual reply, and a simple thank you works too.
