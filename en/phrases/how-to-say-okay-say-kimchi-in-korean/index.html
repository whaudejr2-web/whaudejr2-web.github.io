How to say "Okay, say kimchi" in Korean (자, 김치)

You can say this

자, 김치!

ja gimchi

Meaning: Okay, say kimchi!

Good to know

Koreans say kimchi when posing, the same way English speakers say cheese, because the ee sound pulls the mouth into a smile. Counting one, two, three is just as common.
