How to say "I want to go to Gamcheon Culture Village" in Korean (감천문화마을에 가고 싶어요)

You can say this

감천문화마을에 가고 싶어요.

gamcheonmunhwamaeure gago sipeoyo

Meaning: I want to go to Gamcheon Culture Village.

Good to know

Gamcheon was built on a steep hillside by refugees during the Korean War. In 2009 an art project painted the houses in pastel colors, and today visitors buy a map and collect stamps as they walk around.
