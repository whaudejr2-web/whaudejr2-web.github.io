How to say "See the pharmacy? It's right next to it" in Korean (약국 보여요? 바로 그 옆이에요)

You can say this

약국 보여요? 바로 그 옆이에요.

yakguk boyeoyo baro geu yeopieyo

Meaning: See the pharmacy? It's right next to it.
