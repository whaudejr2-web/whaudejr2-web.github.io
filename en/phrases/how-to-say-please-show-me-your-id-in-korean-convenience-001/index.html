What does "신분증 좀 보여 주세요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

신분증 좀 보여 주세요.

sinbunjeung jom boyeo juseyo

Meaning: Please show me your ID.

Good to know

The legal age to buy alcohol or cigarettes in Korea is nineteen. Stores check IDs strictly because they can be shut down for selling to a minor, so keep your passport with you.
