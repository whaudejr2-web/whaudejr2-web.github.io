How to say "You have to book the shuttle in advance" in Korean (셔틀은 미리 예약해야 돼요)

You can say this

셔틀은 미리 예약해야 돼요.

syeoteureun miri yeyakaeya dwaeyo

Meaning: You have to book the shuttle in advance.
