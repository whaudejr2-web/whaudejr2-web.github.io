How to say "Half spicy, half not spicy" in Korean (매운 거랑 안 매운 거 반반이요)

You can say this

매운 거랑 안 매운 거 반반이요.

maeun georang an maeun geo banbaniyo

Meaning: Half spicy, half not spicy.
