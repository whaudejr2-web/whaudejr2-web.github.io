How to say "Yes, it's fragile. Please handle it with care" in Korean (네, 깨지기 쉬운 거예요. 조심해 주세요)

You can say this

네, 깨지기 쉬운 거예요. 조심해 주세요.

ne, kkaejigi swiun geoyeyo. josimhae juseyo

Meaning: Yes, it's fragile. Please handle it with care.
