How to say "Everyday conversation is fine, but I'm still learning" in Korean (일상 대화는 괜찮은데 아직 배우고 있어요)

You can say this

일상 대화는 괜찮은데 아직 배우고 있어요.

ilsang daehwaneun gwaenchaneunde ajik baeugo isseoyo

Meaning: Everyday conversation is fine, but I'm still learning.
