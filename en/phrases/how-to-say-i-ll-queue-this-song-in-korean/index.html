How to say "I'll queue this song" in Korean (이 노래 예약할게요)

You can say this

이 노래 예약할게요.

i norae yeyakalgeyo

Meaning: I'll queue this song.

Good to know

The remote has a reserve button that queues your song behind the one playing. Koreans queue several at once, so grab the remote early or you will wait all night.
