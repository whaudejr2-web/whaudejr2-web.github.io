How to say "Can you do a heart for me, just once?" in Korean (하트 한 번만 해 주세요)

You can say this

하트 한 번만 해 주세요.

hateu han beonman hae juseyo

Meaning: Can you do a heart for me, just once?

Good to know

The finger heart, thumb crossed over the index finger, spread from Korean celebrities in the mid 2010s. It is now the default pose an idol gives when a fan asks for a heart.
