What does "이 열쇠는 손목에 차세요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

이 열쇠는 손목에 차세요.

i yeolsoeneun sonmoge chaseyo

Meaning: Wear this key on your wrist.

Good to know

The locker key is a rubber wristband with a number on it. Snacks, drinks and extra services are all charged to that number, and you settle the whole bill at the front desk on your way out.
