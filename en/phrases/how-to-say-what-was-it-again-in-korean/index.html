How to say "What was it again?" in Korean (그게 뭐였더라?)

You can say this

그게 뭐였더라?

geuge mwoyeotdeora

Meaning: What was it again?

Good to know

The ending shows you are recalling something out loud. It is plain speech, but since you are talking to yourself, it is fine even in a polite conversation.
