How to say "Blow on three. One, two, three" in Korean (셋 세면 불어요. 하나, 둘, 셋)

You can say this

셋 세면 불어요. 하나, 둘, 셋!

set semyeon bureoyo hana dul set

Meaning: Blow on three. One, two, three!
