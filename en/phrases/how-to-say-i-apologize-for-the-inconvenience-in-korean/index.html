How to say "I apologize for the inconvenience" in Korean (불편을 드려서 죄송합니다)

You can say this

불편을 드려서 죄송합니다.

bulpyeoneul deuryeoseo joesonghamnida

Meaning: I apologize for the inconvenience.

Good to know

This is the formal apology for inconvenience printed on signs and heard in subway and store announcements all over Korea.
