How to say "Shall I grill it for you?" in Korean (구워 드릴까요?)

You can say this

구워 드릴까요?

guwo deurilkkayo

Meaning: Shall I grill it for you?

Good to know

At many places the staff grill, flip and cut the meat for you, especially beef and marinated cuts that burn easily. It is part of the service, and there is no tipping in Korea.
