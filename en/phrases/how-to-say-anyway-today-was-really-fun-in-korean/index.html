How to say "Anyway, today was really fun" in Korean (아무튼 오늘 진짜 재미있었어요)

You can say this

아무튼 오늘 진짜 재미있었어요.

amuteun oneul jinjja jaemiisseosseoyo

Meaning: Anyway, today was really fun.
