How to say "Can I get a tax refund?" in Korean (택스 리펀 돼요?)

You can say this

택스 리펀 돼요?

taekseu ripeon dwaeyo

Meaning: Can I get a tax refund?

Good to know

At shops with a tax free sign, foreign tourists can have the tax taken off right at the register for purchases from 15,000 won up to 1,000,000 won per receipt. Just show your passport when you pay.
