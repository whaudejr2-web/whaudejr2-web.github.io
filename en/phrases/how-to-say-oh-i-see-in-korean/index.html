How to say "Oh, I see" in Korean (아, 그렇구나)

You can say this

아, 그렇구나.

a geureokuna

Meaning: Oh, I see.

Good to know

This ending is plain speech, but it works as thinking aloud, so you will hear it even in polite conversations. With elders or at work, use the polite version that comes later in this lesson.
