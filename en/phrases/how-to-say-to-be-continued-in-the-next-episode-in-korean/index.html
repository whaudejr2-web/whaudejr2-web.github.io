How to say "To be continued in the next episode" in Korean (다음 화에 계속)

You can say this

다음 화에 계속.

daeum hwae gyesok

Meaning: To be continued in the next episode.

Good to know

Naver Webtoon and Kakao Webtoon release most series on a fixed weekday, so this caption usually means a seven day wait. Paid early access to the next episodes is a main source of income for both platforms.
