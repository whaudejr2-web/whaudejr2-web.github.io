How to say "One more bowl of rice cake soup, please" in Korean (떡국 한 그릇 더 주세요)

You can say this

떡국 한 그릇 더 주세요.

tteokguk han geureut deo juseyo

Meaning: One more bowl of rice cake soup, please.
