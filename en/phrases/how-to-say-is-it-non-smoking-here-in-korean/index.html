How to say "Is it non-smoking here?" in Korean (여기 금연이에요?)

You can say this

여기 금연이에요?

yeogi geumyeonieyo

Meaning: Is it non-smoking here?

Good to know

Smoking inside PC bangs has been banned nationwide since 2014. Most places now have a sealed smoking booth instead, which is why the air is far cleaner than it used to be.
