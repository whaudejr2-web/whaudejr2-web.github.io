How to say "Hello, I'm here to look for a room" in Korean (안녕하세요. 방 좀 알아보러 왔어요)

You can say this

안녕하세요. 방 좀 알아보러 왔어요.

annyeonghaseyo. bang jom araboreo wasseoyo

Meaning: Hello, I'm here to look for a room.

Good to know

Most rooms in Korea are rented through licensed real estate agents, whose street-level offices tape listings to the window. Apps like Zigbang and Dabang show the same listings, but an agent still shows you the room.
