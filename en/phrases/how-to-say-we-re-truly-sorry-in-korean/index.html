What does "손님, 정말 죄송합니다." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

손님, 정말 죄송합니다.

sonnim jeongmal joesonghamnida

Meaning: We're truly sorry.

Good to know

Staff address every customer with the word for guest, which covers sir and ma'am at once. You will hear it in shops, restaurants, hotels and taxis, usually right before an apology or a question.
