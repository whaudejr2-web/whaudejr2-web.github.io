How to say "I'd like to sit with my travel companion" in Korean (일행이랑 같이 앉고 싶어서요)

You can say this

일행이랑 같이 앉고 싶어서요.

ilhaengirang gachi anko sipeoseoyo

Meaning: I'd like to sit with my travel companion.
