How to say "It's a bit far. Take the subway" in Korean (좀 멀어요. 지하철 타세요)

You can say this

좀 멀어요. 지하철 타세요.

jom meoreoyo jihacheol taseyo

Meaning: It's a bit far. Take the subway.

Listen and repeat
