How to say "I like Korean food. For example, bibimbap" in Korean (한국 음식 좋아해요. 예를 들면 비빔밥이요)

You can say this

한국 음식 좋아해요. 예를 들면 비빔밥이요.

hanguk eumsik joahaeyo. yereul deulmyeon bibimbabiyo

Meaning: I like Korean food. For example, bibimbap.
