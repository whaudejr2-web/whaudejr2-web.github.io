How to say "It has sweet red bean paste inside" in Korean (팥이 들어 있어요)

You can say this

팥이 들어 있어요.

pachi deureo isseoyo

Meaning: It has sweet red bean paste inside.

Good to know

Sweet red bean paste is the classic filling in Korean bakeries, so a plain-looking bun may be sweet inside. The red bean bun has been sold in Korea for more than a century.
