How to say "Are you closed on Mondays?" in Korean (월요일은 쉬어요?)

You can say this

월요일은 쉬어요?

woryoireun swieoyo

Meaning: Are you closed on Mondays?

Good to know

Most museums in Korea close on Mondays. Seoul's palaces also close one weekday each, but it is Monday for some and Tuesday for others, so check the day before you go.
