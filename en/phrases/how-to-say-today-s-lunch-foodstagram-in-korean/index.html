How to say "Today's lunch, foodstagram" in Korean (오늘 점심. 먹스타그램)

You can say this

오늘 점심. 먹스타그램.

oneul jeomsim. meokseutageuraem

Meaning: Today's lunch, foodstagram.

Good to know

Koreans glue a word onto the end of Instagram to make hashtags: one for food photos, one for daily life, one for selfies. The food one alone has tens of millions of posts.
