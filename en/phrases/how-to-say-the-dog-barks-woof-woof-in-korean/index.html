How to say "The dog barks, woof woof" in Korean (강아지가 멍멍 짖어요)

You can say this

강아지가 멍멍 짖어요.

gangajiga meongmeong jijeoyo

Meaning: The dog barks, woof woof.

Good to know

Korean dogs say this instead of woof. Cats say something close to meow, pigs grunt with a word that sounds like honey, and roosters crow with a long rising cry.
