How to say "You can get the photos with the QR code" in Korean (큐알 코드로 사진 받을 수 있어요)

You can say this

큐알 코드로 사진 받을 수 있어요.

kyual kodeuro sajin badeul su isseoyo

Meaning: You can get the photos with the QR code.

Good to know

Scan the QR code on the screen or the strip to download the photos, and at many chains a short video of the shoot as well. The link does not last forever, so save it right away.
