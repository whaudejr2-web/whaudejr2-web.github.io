How to say "I had a great time, thanks to you" in Korean (덕분에 즐거운 시간 보냈어요)

You can say this

덕분에 즐거운 시간 보냈어요.

deokbune jeulgeoun sigan bonaesseoyo

Meaning: I had a great time, thanks to you.
