How to say "For here" in Korean (먹고 갈 거예요)

You can say this

먹고 갈 거예요.

meokgo gal geoyeyo

Meaning: For here.

Good to know

Since 2018, Korean cafés are not allowed to serve dine-in customers in disposable cups. Your answer decides whether you get a mug or a paper cup.
