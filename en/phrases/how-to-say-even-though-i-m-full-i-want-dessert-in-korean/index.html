How to say "Even though I'm full, I want dessert" in Korean (배부른데도 디저트 먹을래요)

You can say this

배부른데도 디저트 먹을래요.

baebureundedo dijeoteu meogeullaeyo

Meaning: Even though I'm full, I want dessert.
