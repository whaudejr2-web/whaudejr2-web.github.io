How to say "Get home safe" in Korean (조심히 들어가요)

You can say this

조심히 들어가요.

josimhi deureogayo

Meaning: Get home safe.

Good to know

Koreans say this when parting in the evening and often text it again on the way home. A quick reply that you got home safely is the normal way to close the chat.
