What does "저쪽 큰 문으로 들어가시면 돼요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

저쪽 큰 문으로 들어가시면 돼요.

jeojjok keun muneuro deureogasimyeon dwaeyo

Meaning: Go in through the big gate over there.
