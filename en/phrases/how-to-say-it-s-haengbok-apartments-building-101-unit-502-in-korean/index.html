How to say "It's Haengbok Apartments, building 101, unit 502" in Korean (행복아파트 101동 502호예요)

You can say this

행복아파트 101동 502호예요.

haengbogapateu baegil dong obaegi hoyeyo

Meaning: It's Haengbok Apartments, building 101, unit 502.

Good to know

In a Korean apartment address the building number comes first, then the unit. The unit number tells the floor: 502 is on the fifth floor, 1203 on the twelfth. Riders need both numbers to find you.
