How to say "You worked really hard, thank you. In Jeju dialect" in Korean (폭싹 속았수다)

You can say this

폭싹 속았수다.

pokssak sogatsuda

Meaning: You worked really hard, thank you. In Jeju dialect.

Good to know

In standard Korean this sounds like you were completely fooled, but in Jeju dialect it means you worked really hard. The 2025 Netflix drama When Life Gives You Tangerines took its Korean title from it.
