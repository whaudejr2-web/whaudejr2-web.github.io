How to say "Let's make songpyeon together" in Korean (송편 같이 만들어요)

You can say this

송편 같이 만들어요.

songpyeon gachi mandeureoyo

Meaning: Let's make songpyeon together.

Good to know

Songpyeon are half-moon rice cakes steamed on a bed of pine needles. A folk saying promises that whoever shapes them prettily will have a pretty daughter or meet a good spouse.
