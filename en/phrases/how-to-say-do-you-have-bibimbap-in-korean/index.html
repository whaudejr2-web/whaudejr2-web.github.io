How to say "Do you have bibimbap?" in Korean (비빔밥 있어요?)

You can say this

비빔밥 있어요?

bibimbap isseoyo

Meaning: Do you have bibimbap?

Good to know

Korean Air has served this rice bowl in economy since the late 1990s, and it won an international in-flight catering award in 1998. It comes with a tube of red pepper paste and sesame oil.
