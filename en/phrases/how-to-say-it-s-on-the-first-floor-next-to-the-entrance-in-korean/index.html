How to say "It's on the first floor, next to the entrance" in Korean (일 층 현관 옆에 있어요)

You can say this

일 층 현관 옆에 있어요.

il cheung hyeongwan yeope isseoyo

Meaning: It's on the first floor, next to the entrance.
