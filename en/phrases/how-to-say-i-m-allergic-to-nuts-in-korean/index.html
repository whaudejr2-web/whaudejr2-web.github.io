How to say "I'm allergic to nuts" in Korean (견과류 알레르기가 있어요)

You can say this

견과류 알레르기가 있어요.

gyeongwaryu allereugiga isseoyo

Meaning: I'm allergic to nuts.

Good to know

Packaged foods sold in Korea must list major allergens such as peanuts, eggs, milk, wheat, soy and shellfish on the label. Restaurant dishes carry no such label, so you have to ask the staff.
