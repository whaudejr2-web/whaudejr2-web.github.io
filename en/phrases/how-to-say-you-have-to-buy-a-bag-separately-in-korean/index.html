How to say "You have to buy a bag separately" in Korean (봉투는 따로 사셔야 돼요)

You can say this

봉투는 따로 사셔야 돼요.

bongtuneun ttaro sasyeoya dwaeyo

Meaning: You have to buy a bag separately.

Good to know

Since 2019 large marts in Korea may not hand out disposable plastic bags at all. You buy a paper or reusable bag, or pack your groceries in empty boxes at the packing table.
