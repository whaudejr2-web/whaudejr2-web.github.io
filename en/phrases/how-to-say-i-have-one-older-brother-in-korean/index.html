What does "형이 한 명 있어요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

형이 한 명 있어요.

hyeongi han myeong isseoyo

Meaning: I have one older brother.

Good to know

Korean has four words for older siblings, chosen by the speaker's gender: a man uses one word for older brother and another for older sister, and a woman uses two different ones. There is no neutral option.
