What does "네, 그런데 플래시는 안 돼요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

네, 그런데 플래시는 안 돼요.

ne geureonde peullaesineun an dwaeyo

Meaning: Yes, but no flash.

Good to know

Most museums in Korea allow photos for personal use as long as you turn the flash off. Tripods and selfie sticks are usually banned in the galleries, and some special exhibitions post a no photo sign at the door.
