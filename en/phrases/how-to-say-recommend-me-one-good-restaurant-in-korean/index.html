How to say "Recommend me one good restaurant" in Korean (맛집 하나만 추천해 주세요)

You can say this

맛집 하나만 추천해 주세요.

matjip hanaman chucheonhae juseyo

Meaning: Recommend me one good restaurant.

Good to know

Koreans call a restaurant worth the trip a taste house. Searching that word with a neighborhood name on Naver Map or Instagram is how locals decide where to eat.
