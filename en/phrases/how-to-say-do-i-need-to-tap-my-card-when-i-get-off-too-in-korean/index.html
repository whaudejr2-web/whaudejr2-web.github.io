How to say "Do I need to tap my card when I get off too?" in Korean (내릴 때도 카드 찍어야 돼요?)

You can say this

내릴 때도 카드 찍어야 돼요?

naeril ttaedo kadeu jjigeoya dwaeyo

Meaning: Do I need to tap my card when I get off too?

Good to know

Yes, always tap out on buses. It gives you a free or discounted transfer to the next bus or subway within 30 minutes, and skipping it means you are charged extra on your next ride.
