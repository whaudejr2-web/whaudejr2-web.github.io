How to say "How do you fold the sheep-head towel?" in Korean (양머리 수건은 어떻게 만들어요?)

You can say this

양머리 수건은 어떻게 만들어요?

yangmeori sugeoneun eotteoke mandeureoyo

Meaning: How do you fold the sheep-head towel?

Good to know

Regulars roll the ends of a towel into two ear shaped curls and wear it as a cap, a style everyone calls the sheep head. It keeps hot air off your hair and appears in nearly every Korean drama sauna scene.
