How to say "What comes with the room?" in Korean (옵션은 뭐가 있어요?)

You can say this

옵션은 뭐가 있어요?

opsyeoneun mwoga isseoyo

Meaning: What comes with the room?

Good to know

In Korean listings, option means the appliances and furniture that come with the room: typically a fridge, washing machine, air conditioner, bed and desk. Full option means all of them are there.
