What does "샴푸해 드릴게요. 이쪽으로 오세요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

샴푸해 드릴게요. 이쪽으로 오세요.

syampuhae deurilgeyo ijjogeuro oseyo

Meaning: I'll wash your hair. Come this way.
