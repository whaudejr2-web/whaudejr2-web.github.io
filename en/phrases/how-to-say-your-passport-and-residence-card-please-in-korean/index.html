What does "여권하고 외국인 등록증 주세요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

여권하고 외국인 등록증 주세요.

yeogwonhago oegugin deungnokjeung juseyo

Meaning: Your passport and residence card, please.

Good to know

A monthly plan requires the foreigner registration card that immigration issues after you arrive on a long-term visa. Until it comes, usually a few weeks, a prepaid SIM on your passport is the only option.
