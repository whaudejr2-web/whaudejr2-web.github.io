How to say "How long is the wait for the Seoraksan cable car?" in Korean (설악산 케이블카 대기 시간이 얼마나 돼요?)

You can say this

설악산 케이블카 대기 시간이 얼마나 돼요?

seoraksan keibeulka daegi sigani eolmana dwaeyo

Meaning: How long is the wait for the Seoraksan cable car?

Good to know

The Seoraksan cable car climbs to the Gwongeumseong fortress site in about five minutes. Tickets are sold only on the day, first come first served, so on a fall weekend go early.
