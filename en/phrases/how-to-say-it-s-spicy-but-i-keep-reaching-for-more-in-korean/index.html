How to say "It's spicy, but I keep reaching for more" in Korean (매운데 자꾸 손이 가요)

You can say this

매운데 자꾸 손이 가요.

maeunde jakku soni gayo

Meaning: It's spicy, but I keep reaching for more.

Good to know

Literally my hand keeps going to it. It is the standard way to say a food is addictive, and a snack jingle built on the phrase has been running on Korean TV for decades.
