How to say "Jungkook is called the golden youngest" in Korean (정국은 황금 막내라고 불려요)

You can say this

정국은 황금 막내라고 불려요.

jeonggugeun hwanggeum mangnaerago bullyeoyo

Meaning: Jungkook is called the golden youngest.

Good to know

Jungkook got the nickname golden youngest because he is the youngest member and seems good at everything, from singing and dancing to sports.
