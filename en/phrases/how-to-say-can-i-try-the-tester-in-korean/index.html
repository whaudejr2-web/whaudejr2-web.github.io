How to say "Can I try the tester?" in Korean (테스터 써 봐도 돼요?)

You can say this

테스터 써 봐도 돼요?

teseuteo sseo bwado dwaeyo

Meaning: Can I try the tester?

Good to know

Olive Young, the biggest beauty chain in Korea with over a thousand stores, puts a tester next to almost every product. Trying things on your hand is expected, not rude.
