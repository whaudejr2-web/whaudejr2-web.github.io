How to say "Come out of exit three and turn right" in Korean (삼 번 출구로 나와서 오른쪽으로 도세요)

You can say this

삼 번 출구로 나와서 오른쪽으로 도세요.

sam beon chulguro nawaseo oreunjjogeuro doseyo

Meaning: Come out of exit three and turn right.

Good to know

Subway exits in Korea are numbered, and big stations can have more than ten. Map apps show which exit is closest, so people usually give the exit number first.
