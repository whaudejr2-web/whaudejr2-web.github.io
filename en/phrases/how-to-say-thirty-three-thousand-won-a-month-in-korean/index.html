What does "한 달에 삼만 삼천 원이에요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

한 달에 삼만 삼천 원이에요.

han dare samman samcheon wonieyo

Meaning: Thirty-three thousand won a month.

Good to know

Besides the three big carriers, SK Telecom, KT and LG U+, dozens of budget carriers rent their networks and sell the same coverage for a fraction of the price. Convenience stores sell their SIM cards.
