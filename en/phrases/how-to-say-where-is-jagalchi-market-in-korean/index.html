How to say "Where is Jagalchi Market?" in Korean (자갈치시장은 어디예요?)

You can say this

자갈치시장은 어디예요?

jagalchisijangeun eodiyeyo

Meaning: Where is Jagalchi Market?

Good to know

Jagalchi, next to Busan's old downtown, is the largest seafood market in Korea. You pick a live fish from a tank on the first floor, and a restaurant on the second floor prepares it for a small fee.
