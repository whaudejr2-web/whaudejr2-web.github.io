How to say "The police number is 112" in Korean (경찰은 일일이예요)

You can say this

경찰은 일일이예요.

gyeongchareun il il iyeyo

Meaning: The police number is 112.

Good to know

112 is the police and 119 is fire, ambulance and rescue. Both work from any mobile phone without an area code, and both accept text messages with photos if you cannot speak.
