How to say "Wow, so beautiful" in Korean (와, 진짜 예쁘다)

You can say this

와, 진짜 예쁘다.

wa, jinjja yeppeuda

Meaning: Wow, so beautiful.

Good to know

Comments often end in the plain dictionary form, like here. It is not rude: it reads as talking to yourself out loud, the written version of a gasp.
