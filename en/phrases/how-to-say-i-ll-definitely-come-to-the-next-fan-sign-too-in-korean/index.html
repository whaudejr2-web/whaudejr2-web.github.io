How to say "I'll definitely come to the next fan sign too" in Korean (다음 사인회에도 꼭 올게요)

You can say this

다음 사인회에도 꼭 올게요.

daeum sainhoeedo kkok olgeyo

Meaning: I'll definitely come to the next fan sign too.
