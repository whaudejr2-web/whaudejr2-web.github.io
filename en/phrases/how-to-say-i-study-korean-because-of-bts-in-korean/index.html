How to say "I study Korean because of BTS" in Korean (방탄 때문에 한국어 공부해요)

You can say this

방탄 때문에 한국어 공부해요.

bangtan ttaemune hangugeo gongbuhaeyo

Meaning: I study Korean because of BTS.
