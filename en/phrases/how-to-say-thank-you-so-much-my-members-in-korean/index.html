How to say "Thank you so much, my members" in Korean (우리 멤버들 정말 고마워요)

You can say this

우리 멤버들 정말 고마워요.

uri membeodeul jeongmal gomawoyo

Meaning: Thank you so much, my members.

Listen and repeat
