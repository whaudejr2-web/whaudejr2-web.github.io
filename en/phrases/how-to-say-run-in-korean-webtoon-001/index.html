How to say "Run" in Korean (도망쳐요)

You can say this

도망쳐요!

domangchyeoyo

Meaning: Run!

Good to know

In the speech bubble this is shouted in the short casual form without the polite ending. The polite version here is the one you can safely use with an adult you do not know.
