How to say "Where are the lockers?" in Korean (사물함 어디 있어요?)

You can say this

사물함 어디 있어요?

samulham eodi isseoyo

Meaning: Where are the lockers?

Good to know

Both big parks have lockers near the entrance and at the major coasters. Ride entrances also have open shelves for bags, because loose items are not allowed on the ride.
