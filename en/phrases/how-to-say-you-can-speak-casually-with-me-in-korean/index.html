How to say "You can speak casually with me" in Korean (말 편하게 하셔도 돼요)

You can say this

말 편하게 하셔도 돼요.

mal pyeonhage hasyeodo dwaeyo

Meaning: You can speak casually with me.

Good to know

This invites the other person to drop polite speech with you. The older person normally offers it first, so saying it yourself is a friendly move, especially toward someone your own age.
