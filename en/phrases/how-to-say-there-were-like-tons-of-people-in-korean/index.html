How to say "There were, like, tons of people" in Korean (막 사람들이 엄청 많았어요)

You can say this

막 사람들이 엄청 많았어요.

mak saramdeuri eomcheong manasseoyo

Meaning: There were, like, tons of people.

Good to know

The first word works like the English like and is very common in young people's storytelling. In a presentation or with elders, leave it out.
