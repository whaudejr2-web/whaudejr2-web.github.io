How to say "Where do I put the detergent?" in Korean (세제는 어디에 넣어요?)

You can say this

세제는 어디에 넣어요?

sejeneun eodie neoeoyo

Meaning: Where do I put the detergent?

Good to know

At many Korean laundromats the washer adds detergent and softener automatically, so you do not need to bring any. Check the sign on the machine before buying a packet from the vending machine.
