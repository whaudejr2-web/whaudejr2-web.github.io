How to say "Thank you for always cheering me on" in Korean (항상 응원해 줘서 고마워요)

You can say this

항상 응원해 줘서 고마워요.

hangsang eungwonhae jwoseo gomawoyo

Meaning: Thank you for always cheering me on.
