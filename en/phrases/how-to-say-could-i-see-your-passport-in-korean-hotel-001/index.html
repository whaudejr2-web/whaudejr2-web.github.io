What does "여권 좀 보여 주시겠어요?" mean in Korean?

You will hear this

여권 좀 보여 주시겠어요?

yeogwon jom boyeo jusigesseoyo

Meaning: Could I see your passport?

Good to know

Hotels in Korea are required to keep a record of foreign guests, so showing your passport at check-in is routine. Staff scan or copy it and hand it right back.
