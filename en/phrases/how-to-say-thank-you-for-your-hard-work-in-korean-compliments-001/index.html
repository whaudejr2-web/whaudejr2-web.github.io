How to say "Thank you for your hard work" in Korean (수고하셨어요)

You can say this

수고하셨어요.

sugohasyeosseoyo

Meaning: Thank you for your hard work.

Good to know

Said at the end of a workday, a class or any shared effort. It thanks someone for their effort rather than for a favor, and coworkers say it to each other every day when leaving.
