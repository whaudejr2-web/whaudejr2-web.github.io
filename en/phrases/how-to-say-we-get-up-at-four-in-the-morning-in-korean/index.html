What does "새벽 네 시에 일어나요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

새벽 네 시에 일어나요.

saebyeok ne sie ireonayo

Meaning: We get up at four in the morning.

Good to know

The dawn ceremony at most temples starts between three and four in the morning, and temple stay guests join it. Some rest programs let you sleep in, so check the schedule.
