How to say "Sorry I'm late" in Korean (늦어서 미안해요)

You can say this

늦어서 미안해요.

neujeoseo mianhaeyo

Meaning: Sorry I'm late.

Good to know

Koreans often add a crying face made of two Korean vowel letters that look like eyes with tears streaming down. It softens an apology and shows you feel bad about it.
