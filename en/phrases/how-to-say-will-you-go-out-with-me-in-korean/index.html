How to say "Will you go out with me?" in Korean (우리 사귈래요?)

You can say this

우리 사귈래요?

uri sagwillaeyo

Meaning: Will you go out with me?

Good to know

This is the question that makes a relationship official in Korea. Couples count day one from the yes, and anniversaries are measured in days from that date.
