How to say "I got a missed-delivery slip" in Korean (부재중 안내문 받았어요)

You can say this

부재중 안내문 받았어요.

bujaejung annaemun badasseoyo

Meaning: I got a missed-delivery slip.

Good to know

If nobody is home for registered mail or an international package, the carrier leaves a slip and returns the item to the post office, where it is held for a limited time. You can also book redelivery online or by phone.
