How to say "I booked a room at the pension" in Korean (펜션 예약했는데요)

You can say this

펜션 예약했는데요.

pensyeon yeyakaenneundeyo

Meaning: I booked a room at the pension.

Good to know

In Korea a pension is not a retirement payment but a self-catering holiday house with a kitchen, a few rooms, and a barbecue deck. The word came through Japanese from the French word for a guesthouse.
