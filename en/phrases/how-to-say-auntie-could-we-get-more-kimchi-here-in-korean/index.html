How to say "Auntie, could we get more kimchi here?" in Korean (이모, 여기 김치 좀 더 주세요)

You can say this

이모, 여기 김치 좀 더 주세요.

imo, yeogi gimchi jom deo juseyo

Meaning: Auntie, could we get more kimchi here?
