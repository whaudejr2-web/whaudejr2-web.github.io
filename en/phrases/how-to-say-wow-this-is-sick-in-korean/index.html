How to say "Wow, this is sick" in Korean (와, 이거 쩔어요)

You can say this

와, 이거 쩔어요!

wa igeo jjeoreoyo

Meaning: Wow, this is sick!

Good to know

Rough slang that started as a word for being soaked in salt or sweat and flipped to mean awesome in the 2000s. Fine among friends, but not with a boss or anyone older.
