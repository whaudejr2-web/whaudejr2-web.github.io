How to say "I'm on vacation next week" in Korean (다음 주에 휴가예요)

You can say this

다음 주에 휴가예요.

daeum jue hyugayeyo

Meaning: I'm on vacation next week.

Good to know

By law, full-time workers in Korea get fifteen days of paid annual leave after their first year, and one extra day every two years after that.
