How to say "What's the difference between a serum and an essence?" in Korean (세럼이랑 에센스는 뭐가 달라요?)

You can say this

세럼이랑 에센스는 뭐가 달라요?

sereomirang esenseuneun mwoga dallayo

Meaning: What's the difference between a serum and an essence?
