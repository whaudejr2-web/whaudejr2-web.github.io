How to say "I want to get internet at home" in Korean (집에 인터넷 설치하고 싶어요)

You can say this

집에 인터넷 설치하고 싶어요.

jibe inteonet seolchihago sipeoyo

Meaning: I want to get internet at home.

Good to know

Korean home internet is sold by speed: one hundred megabits, five hundred, one gigabit and even ten gigabits, usually for twenty to forty thousand won a month. Installation is often free with a contract.
