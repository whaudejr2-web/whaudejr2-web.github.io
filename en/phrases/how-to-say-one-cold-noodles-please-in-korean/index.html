How to say "One cold noodles, please" in Korean (냉면 하나 주세요)

You can say this

냉면 하나 주세요.

naengmyeon hana juseyo

Meaning: One cold noodles, please.

Good to know

Cold buckwheat noodles in icy broth are the classic finish after grilled meat. Many barbecue restaurants sell a smaller, cheaper dessert-size bowl for exactly this moment.
