How to say "Where is the spring?" in Korean (약수터가 어디예요?)

You can say this

약수터가 어디예요?

yaksuteoga eodiyeyo

Meaning: Where is the spring?

Good to know

Many Korean mountains have natural springs with a shared ladle or cup. The local district tests the water regularly and posts the result on a sign, so check it says suitable before you fill up.
