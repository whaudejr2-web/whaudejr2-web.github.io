How to say "This is my small but certain happiness" in Korean (이게 제 소확행이에요)

You can say this

이게 제 소확행이에요.

ige je sohwakaengieyo

Meaning: This is my small but certain happiness.

Good to know

Three syllables for small but certain happiness, a phrase the novelist Haruki Murakami coined in a 1986 essay. It became one of Korea's trend words of 2018 and still describes any tiny daily pleasure.
