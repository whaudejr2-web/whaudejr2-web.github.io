How to say "Who is your bias?" in Korean (최애가 누구예요?)

You can say this

최애가 누구예요?

choeaega nuguyeyo

Meaning: Who is your bias?

Good to know

Korean fans call their favorite member by a word that literally means most loved. International fans say bias, and a bias wrecker is the member who tempts you to switch.
