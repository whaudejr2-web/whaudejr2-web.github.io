What does "이사하는 날 현관 비밀번호 알려 드릴게요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

이사하는 날 현관 비밀번호 알려 드릴게요.

isahaneun nal hyeongwan bimilbeonho allyeo deurilgeyo

Meaning: I'll give you the door code on moving day.
