How to say "I came from Argentina" in Korean (저는 아르헨티나에서 왔어요)

You can say this

저는 아르헨티나에서 왔어요.

jeoneun areuhentinaeseo wasseoyo

Meaning: I came from Argentina.

Good to know

Video call fan signs started during the pandemic in 2020 and never went away, so fans abroad can now win the same one-on-one minutes without flying to Seoul.
