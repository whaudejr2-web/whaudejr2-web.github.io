What does "처방전 드릴게요. 약국에서 받으세요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

처방전 드릴게요. 약국에서 받으세요.

cheobangjeon deurilgeyo yakgugeseo badeuseyo

Meaning: I'll give you a prescription. Pick it up at the pharmacy.

Good to know

Since 2000, doctors in Korea prescribe but do not dispense. Take the prescription to a pharmacy; there is usually one on the ground floor of the clinic building or next door.
