How to say "It's okay to rest when you're sick" in Korean (아플 때는 쉬어도 괜찮아요)

You can say this

아플 때는 쉬어도 괜찮아요.

apeul ttaeneun swieodo gwaenchanayo

Meaning: It's okay to rest when you're sick.

Good to know

Korean law does not require paid sick days, so many workers use annual leave when they are ill. In 2022 the government began testing a sickness benefit in a few regions.
