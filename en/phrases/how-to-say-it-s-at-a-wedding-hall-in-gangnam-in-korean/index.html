How to say "It's at a wedding hall in Gangnam" in Korean (강남에 있는 웨딩홀이에요)

You can say this

강남에 있는 웨딩홀이에요.

gangname inneun wedinghorieyo

Meaning: It's at a wedding hall in Gangnam.

Good to know

Most Korean weddings take place in commercial wedding halls that run ceremonies back to back, so the ceremony itself usually lasts about thirty minutes before everyone moves to the buffet.
