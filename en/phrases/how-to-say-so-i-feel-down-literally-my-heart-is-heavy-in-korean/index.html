How to say "So I feel down. Literally, my heart is heavy" in Korean (그래서 마음이 무거워요)

You can say this

그래서 마음이 무거워요.

geuraeseo maeumi mugeowoyo

Meaning: So I feel down. Literally, my heart is heavy.
