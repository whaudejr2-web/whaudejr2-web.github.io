How to say "Do I need to fill out the customs form too?" in Korean (세관 신고서도 써야 돼요?)

You can say this

세관 신고서도 써야 돼요?

segwan singoseodo sseoya dwaeyo

Meaning: Do I need to fill out the customs form too?

Good to know

Since May 2023, Korea only requires the customs form from travelers who have goods to declare. If you have nothing to declare, you walk through without filling anything out.
