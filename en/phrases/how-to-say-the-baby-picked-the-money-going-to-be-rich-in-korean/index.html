How to say "The baby picked the money! Going to be rich" in Korean (돈을 잡았어요! 부자 되겠네요)

You can say this

돈을 잡았어요! 부자 되겠네요.

doneul jabasseoyo buja doegenneyo

Meaning: The baby picked the money! Going to be rich.
