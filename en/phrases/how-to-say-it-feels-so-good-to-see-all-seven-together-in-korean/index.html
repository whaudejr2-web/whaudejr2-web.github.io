How to say "It feels so good to see all seven together" in Korean (일곱 명이 다 같이 있으니까 너무 좋아요)

You can say this

일곱 명이 다 같이 있으니까 너무 좋아요.

ilgop myeongi da gachi isseunikka neomu joayo

Meaning: It feels so good to see all seven together.
