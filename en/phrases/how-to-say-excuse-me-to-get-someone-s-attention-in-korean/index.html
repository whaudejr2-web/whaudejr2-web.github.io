How to say "Excuse me, to get someone's attention" in Korean (저기요)

You can say this

저기요.

jeogiyo

Meaning: Excuse me, to get someone's attention.

Good to know

Literally this means over there. It is the normal way to call a server or get a stranger's attention in Korea, and it is not considered rude.
