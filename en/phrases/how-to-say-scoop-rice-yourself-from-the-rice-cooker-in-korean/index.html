How to say "Scoop rice yourself from the rice cooker" in Korean (밥은 밥솥에서 직접 퍼 가세요)

You can say this

밥은 밥솥에서 직접 퍼 가세요.

babeun bapsoteseo jikjeop peo gaseyo

Meaning: Scoop rice yourself from the rice cooker.
