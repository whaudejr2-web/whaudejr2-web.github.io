What does "백십 번 게이트예요. 셔틀 트레인 타고 가셔야 돼요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

백십 번 게이트예요. 셔틀 트레인 타고 가셔야 돼요.

baeksip beon geiteuyeyo. syeoteul teurein tago gasyeoya dwaeyo

Meaning: Gate 110. You need to take the shuttle train.

Good to know

At Incheon Terminal 1, gates 101 to 132 are in a separate concourse reached by an underground shuttle train. Allow an extra ten to fifteen minutes to get there after security.
