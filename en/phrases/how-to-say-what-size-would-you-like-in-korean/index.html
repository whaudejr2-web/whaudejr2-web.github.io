What does "몇 호로 드릴까요?" mean in Korean?

You will hear this

몇 호로 드릴까요?

myeot horo deurilkkayo

Meaning: What size would you like?

Good to know

Korean cakes are sized by number. A number one is about 15 centimeters across and serves two or three people; a number two is about 18 centimeters and serves four or five.
