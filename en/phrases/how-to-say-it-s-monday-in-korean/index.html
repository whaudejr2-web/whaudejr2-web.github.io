How to say "It's Monday" in Korean (월요일이에요)

You can say this

월요일이에요.

woryoirieyo

Meaning: It's Monday.

Good to know

Korean weekday names are built from the moon, fire, water, wood, metal, earth and the sun. Monday is literally moon day, just as in English, and Sunday is sun day.
