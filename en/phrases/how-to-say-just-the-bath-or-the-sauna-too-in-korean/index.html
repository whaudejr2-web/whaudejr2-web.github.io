What does "목욕만 하세요, 찜질방도 하세요?" mean in Korean?

You will hear this

목욕만 하세요, 찜질방도 하세요?

mogyongman haseyo jjimjilbangdo haseyo

Meaning: Just the bath, or the sauna too?

Good to know

Most bathhouses sell two tickets. The cheaper one covers the bath area only; the sauna ticket adds the dry heat rooms and the common floor and includes a set of clothes. The difference is usually a few thousand won.
