How to say "The stars are twinkling in the night sky" in Korean (밤하늘에 별이 반짝반짝 빛나요)

You can say this

밤하늘에 별이 반짝반짝 빛나요.

bamhaneure byeori banjjakbanjjak binnayo

Meaning: The stars are twinkling in the night sky.

Good to know

A small twinkle. Swap the bright vowel a for the darker eo and it becomes a big flash: in Korean sound words, bright vowels feel small and light, dark vowels big and heavy.
