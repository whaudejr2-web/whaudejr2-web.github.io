How to say "Cross at the crosswalk and it's right there" in Korean (횡단보도 건너서 바로예요)

You can say this

횡단보도 건너서 바로예요.

hoengdanbodo geonneoseo baroyeyo

Meaning: Cross at the crosswalk and it's right there.
