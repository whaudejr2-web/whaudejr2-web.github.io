How to say "Do I need to return it with a full tank?" in Korean (기름 가득 채워서 반납해야 돼요?)

You can say this

기름 가득 채워서 반납해야 돼요?

gireum gadeuk chaewoseo bannapaeya dwaeyo

Meaning: Do I need to return it with a full tank?

Good to know

Most Korean rentals use the same-level rule: staff note the fuel gauge at pickup and you return the car at that level. If it comes back lower, they charge for the difference at a higher price per liter.
