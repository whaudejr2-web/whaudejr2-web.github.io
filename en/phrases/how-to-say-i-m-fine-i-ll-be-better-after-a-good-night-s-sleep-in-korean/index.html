How to say "I'm fine. I'll be better after a good night's sleep" in Korean (괜찮아요. 하룻밤 자면 나을 거예요)

You can say this

괜찮아요. 하룻밤 자면 나을 거예요.

gwaenchanayo harutbam jamyeon naeul geoyeyo

Meaning: I'm fine. I'll be better after a good night's sleep.
