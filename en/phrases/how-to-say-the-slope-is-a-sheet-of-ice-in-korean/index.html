How to say "The slope is a sheet of ice" in Korean (슬로프가 완전 빙판이에요)

You can say this

슬로프가 완전 빙판이에요.

seullopeuga wanjeon bingpanieyo

Meaning: The slope is a sheet of ice.

Good to know

Most Korean slopes depend on machine-made snow, which is denser than natural snow. Under heavy traffic it packs down hard, so icy patches are common by afternoon.
