What does "키오스크에서 티켓 뽑으면 돼요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

키오스크에서 티켓 뽑으면 돼요.

kioseukeueseo tiket ppobeumyeon dwaeyo

Meaning: You can print your ticket at the kiosk.
