What does "언니라고 불러도 돼요?" mean in Korean?

You will hear this

언니라고 불러도 돼요?

eonnirago bulleodo dwaeyo

Meaning: Can I call you unni?

Good to know

Koreans call older friends big sister or big brother, with different words depending on whether the speaker is a woman or a man. Using a first name alone with someone older sounds rude.
