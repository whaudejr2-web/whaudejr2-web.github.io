How to say "I got ripped off at the market. Literally, I wore a gourd bowl" in Korean (시장에서 바가지 썼어요)

You can say this

시장에서 바가지 썼어요.

sijangeseo bagaji sseosseoyo

Meaning: I got ripped off at the market. Literally, I wore a gourd bowl.

Good to know

Comes from an old gambling game played with numbered gourd bowls, where the loser was said to wear the bowl. Today it just means paying way too much.
