How to say "Could you help me a little?" in Korean (좀 도와주실 수 있어요?)

You can say this

좀 도와주실 수 있어요?

jom dowajusil su isseoyo

Meaning: Could you help me a little?

Good to know

The short word that means a little softens any request. Koreans put it in front of almost every favor they ask a stranger, from directions to a glass of water.
