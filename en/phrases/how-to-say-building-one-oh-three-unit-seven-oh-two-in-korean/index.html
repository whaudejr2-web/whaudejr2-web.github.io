How to say "Building one oh three, unit seven oh two" in Korean (백삼 동 칠백이 호예요)

You can say this

백삼 동 칠백이 호예요.

baeksam dong chilbaegi hoyeyo

Meaning: Building one oh three, unit seven oh two.

Good to know

In an apartment complex, the first number is the building and the second is the unit. Unit numbers start with the floor, so 702 is on the seventh floor.
