How to say "It's refreshing. Koreans say it even about hot soup" in Korean (시원해요)

You can say this

시원해요.

siwonhaeyo

Meaning: It's refreshing. Koreans say it even about hot soup.

Good to know

The same word means cool, like a cold drink, and refreshing, like a boiling bowl of soup that clears your head. Koreans say it about both without any irony.
