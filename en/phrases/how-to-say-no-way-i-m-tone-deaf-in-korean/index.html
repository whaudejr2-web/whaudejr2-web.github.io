How to say "No way, I'm tone deaf" in Korean (아니에요, 저 음치예요)

You can say this

아니에요, 저 음치예요.

anieyo jeo eumchiyeyo

Meaning: No way, I'm tone deaf.

Good to know

Koreans have a single word for tone deaf, and matching words for someone who cannot dance and someone with no sense of rhythm. All three get thrown around at karaoke.
