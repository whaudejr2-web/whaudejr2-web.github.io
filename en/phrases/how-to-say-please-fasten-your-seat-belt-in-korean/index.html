How to say "Please fasten your seat belt" in Korean (안전벨트 매 주세요)

You can say this

안전벨트 매 주세요.

anjeonbelteu mae juseyo

Meaning: Please fasten your seat belt.

Good to know

Since 2018, Korean law requires seat belts in every seat, including the back seat of a taxi. Drivers say this as they pull away, so buckle up right after you sit down.
