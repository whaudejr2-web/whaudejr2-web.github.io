How to say "Let's have green onion pancake and makgeolli when we get down" in Korean (내려가서 파전에 막걸리 먹어요)

You can say this

내려가서 파전에 막걸리 먹어요.

naeryeogaseo pajeone makgeolli meogeoyo

Meaning: Let's have green onion pancake and makgeolli when we get down.
