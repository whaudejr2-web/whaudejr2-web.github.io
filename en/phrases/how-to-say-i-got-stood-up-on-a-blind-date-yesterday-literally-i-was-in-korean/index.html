How to say "I got stood up on a blind date yesterday. Literally, I was hit by the wind" in Korean (어제 소개팅에서 바람맞았어요)

You can say this

어제 소개팅에서 바람맞았어요.

eoje sogaetingeseo barammajasseoyo

Meaning: I got stood up on a blind date yesterday. Literally, I was hit by the wind.
