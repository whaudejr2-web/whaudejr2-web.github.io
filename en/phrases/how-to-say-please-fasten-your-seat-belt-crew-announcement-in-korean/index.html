How to say "Please fasten your seat belt. Crew announcement" in Korean (좌석벨트를 매 주세요)

You can say this

좌석벨트를 매 주세요.

jwaseokbelteureul mae juseyo

Meaning: Please fasten your seat belt. Crew announcement.

Good to know

On Korean carriers every announcement is made in Korean first and then in English. The chime and the lit sign above your seat mean the same thing, so follow them even if you miss the words.
