How to say "I'm home" in Korean (다녀왔습니다)

You can say this

다녀왔습니다.

danyeowatseumnida

Meaning: I'm home.

Good to know

Literally: I went and came back. Children and spouses say it as they walk in the door, and whoever is home answers with the welcome in the next sentence.
