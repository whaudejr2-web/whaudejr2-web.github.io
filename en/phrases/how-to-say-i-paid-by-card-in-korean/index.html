How to say "I paid by card" in Korean (카드로 결제했어요)

You can say this

카드로 결제했어요.

kadeuro gyeoljehaesseoyo

Meaning: I paid by card.

Good to know

If you paid by card, most Korean stores can look up the transaction and refund the same card even without the paper receipt. Cash purchases without a receipt are much harder to return.
