How to say "Text me when you get home" in Korean (집에 도착하면 연락해요)

You can say this

집에 도착하면 연락해요.

jibe dochakamyeon yeollakaeyo

Meaning: Text me when you get home.

Good to know

Sending a quick message once you are home is an expected courtesy in Korea after meeting someone. Forgetting it can make the other person worry, so your friend may ask before you leave.
