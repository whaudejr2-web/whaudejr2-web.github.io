How to say "Do a finger heart" in Korean (손가락 하트 해요)

You can say this

손가락 하트 해요.

songarak hateu haeyo

Meaning: Do a finger heart.

Good to know

The finger heart, made by crossing the thumb and index finger, was popularized by Korean celebrities in the 2010s and is now the standard pose in Korea. Make one and locals will know exactly what you mean.
