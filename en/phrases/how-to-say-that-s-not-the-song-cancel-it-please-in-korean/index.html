How to say "That's not the song. Cancel it, please" in Korean (이 노래 아니에요. 취소해 주세요)

You can say this

이 노래 아니에요. 취소해 주세요.

i norae anieyo chwisohae juseyo

Meaning: That's not the song. Cancel it, please.
