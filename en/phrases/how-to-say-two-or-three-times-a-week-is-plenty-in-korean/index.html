How to say "Two or three times a week is plenty" in Korean (일주일에 두세 번이면 충분해요)

You can say this

일주일에 두세 번이면 충분해요.

iljuire duse beonimyeon chungbunhaeyo

Meaning: Two or three times a week is plenty.
