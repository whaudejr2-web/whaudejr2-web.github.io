How to say "Where do I catch the train to Hongdae?" in Korean (홍대 가려면 어디서 타요?)

You can say this

홍대 가려면 어디서 타요?

hongdae garyeomyeon eodiseo tayo

Meaning: Where do I catch the train to Hongdae?

Good to know

Hongdae is the student and nightlife area around Hongik University. Hongik University Station is on Line 2 and also on the airport railroad, so you can ride there directly from Incheon Airport.
