What does "이 킬로니까 사만 칠천 원이에요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

이 킬로니까 사만 칠천 원이에요.

i killonikka saman chilcheon wonieyo

Meaning: It's two kilos, so forty-seven thousand won.
