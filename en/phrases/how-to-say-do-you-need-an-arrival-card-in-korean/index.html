What does "입국 신고서 필요하세요?" mean in Korean?

You will hear this

입국 신고서 필요하세요?

ipguk singoseo piryohaseyo

Meaning: Do you need an arrival card?

Good to know

Since February 2025, foreign visitors can submit the arrival card online within three days before landing. If you already did, you can say no to the paper form.
