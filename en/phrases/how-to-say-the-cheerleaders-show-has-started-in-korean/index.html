How to say "The cheerleaders' show has started" in Korean (치어리더 공연 시작했어요)

You can say this

치어리더 공연 시작했어요.

chieorideo gongyeon sijakaesseoyo

Meaning: The cheerleaders' show has started.

Good to know

Professional cheerleaders dance on a stage in front of the cheering section through the whole game. Several have their own fan clubs, and some have been hired by teams in Taiwan.
