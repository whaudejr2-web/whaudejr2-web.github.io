How to say "One, two, three" in Korean (하나, 둘, 셋)

You can say this

하나, 둘, 셋.

hana, dul, set

Meaning: One, two, three.

Good to know

Korean has two number systems. These native Korean numbers are used for counting items, people and your age, while a separate set based on Chinese is used for prices, dates and phone numbers.
