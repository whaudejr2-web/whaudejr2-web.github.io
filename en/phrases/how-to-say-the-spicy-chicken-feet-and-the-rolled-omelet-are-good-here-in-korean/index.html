What does "여기는 닭발이랑 계란말이가 맛있어요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

여기는 닭발이랑 계란말이가 맛있어요.

yeogineun dakbarirang gyeranmariga masisseoyo

Meaning: The spicy chicken feet and the rolled omelet are good here.
