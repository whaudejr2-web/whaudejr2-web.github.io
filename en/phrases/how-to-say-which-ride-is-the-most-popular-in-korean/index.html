How to say "Which ride is the most popular?" in Korean (어떤 놀이기구가 제일 인기 많아요?)

You can say this

어떤 놀이기구가 제일 인기 많아요?

eotteon norigiguga jeil ingi manayo

Meaning: Which ride is the most popular?
