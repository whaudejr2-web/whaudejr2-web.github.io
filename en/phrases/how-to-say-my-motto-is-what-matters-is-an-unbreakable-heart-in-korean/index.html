How to say "My motto is: what matters is an unbreakable heart" in Korean (제 좌우명은 중꺾마예요)

You can say this

제 좌우명은 중꺾마예요.

je jwaumyeongeun jungkkeongmayeyo

Meaning: My motto is: what matters is an unbreakable heart.

Good to know

Short for: what matters is a heart that will not break. It came from a pro gamer's interview at the 2022 League of Legends Worlds, and Korea's football players held it up on a flag at the 2022 World Cup.
