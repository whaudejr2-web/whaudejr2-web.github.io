How to say "I miss you too. Let's see each other soon" in Korean (저도 보고 싶어요. 빨리 만나요)

You can say this

저도 보고 싶어요. 빨리 만나요.

jeodo bogo sipeoyo. ppalli mannayo

Meaning: I miss you too. Let's see each other soon.
