How to say "Dip the fried items in the tteokbokki sauce" in Korean (튀김은 떡볶이 국물에 찍어 드세요)

You can say this

튀김은 떡볶이 국물에 찍어 드세요.

twigimeun tteokbokki gungmure jjigeo deuseyo

Meaning: Dip the fried items in the tteokbokki sauce.
