How to say "It's the effortless look" in Korean (꾸안꾸 스타일이에요)

You can say this

꾸안꾸 스타일이에요.

kkuankku seutairieyo

Meaning: It's the effortless look.

Good to know

Slang short for dressed up as if not dressed up: the effortless look Koreans love. You'll hear it on fashion channels and in everyday chat about outfits.
