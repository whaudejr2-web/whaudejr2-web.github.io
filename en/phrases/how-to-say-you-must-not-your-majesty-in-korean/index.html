How to say "You must not, Your Majesty" in Korean (아니 되옵니다)

You can say this

아니 되옵니다!

ani doeomnida

Meaning: You must not, Your Majesty!

Good to know

The most quoted historical drama line, chanted by rows of kneeling ministers. The everyday version is the short polite sentence meaning that is not allowed, which Koreans use dozens of times a day.
