How to say "Is it a mechanical parking tower?" in Korean (기계식 주차장이에요?)

You can say this

기계식 주차장이에요?

gigyesik juchajangieyo

Meaning: Is it a mechanical parking tower?

Good to know

In dense neighborhoods a parking tower is a machine lift: you drive onto a platform, get out, and the attendant stores the car. Large SUVs often do not fit, so check the height and weight limit on the sign.
