How to say "In ten thousand won bills, please" in Korean (만 원짜리로 주세요)

You can say this

만 원짜리로 주세요.

man wonjjariro juseyo

Meaning: In ten thousand won bills, please.

Good to know

Korean bills come in one, five, ten and fifty thousand won. The fifty thousand won note, introduced in 2009, is the largest, and small shops sometimes struggle to make change for it.
