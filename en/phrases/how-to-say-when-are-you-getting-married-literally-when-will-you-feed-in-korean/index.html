How to say "When are you getting married? Literally, when will you feed us noodles?" in Korean (국수는 언제 먹여 줄 거예요?)

You can say this

국수는 언제 먹여 줄 거예요?

guksuneun eonje meogyeo jul geoyeyo

Meaning: When are you getting married? Literally, when will you feed us noodles?

Good to know

Korean weddings traditionally served long noodles to guests, a wish for a long marriage. Relatives still use this line to nudge singles at family gatherings.
