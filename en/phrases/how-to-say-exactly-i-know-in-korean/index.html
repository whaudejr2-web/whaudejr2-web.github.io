How to say "Exactly, I know" in Korean (그러니까요)

You can say this

그러니까요.

geureonikkayo

Meaning: Exactly, I know.

Good to know

Literally: that is what I am saying. Use it to agree with a complaint or an opinion, not to answer a yes-or-no question.
