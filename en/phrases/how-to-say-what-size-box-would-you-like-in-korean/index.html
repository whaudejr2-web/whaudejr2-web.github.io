What does "몇 호 상자로 드릴까요?" mean in Korean?

You will hear this

몇 호 상자로 드릴까요?

myeot ho sangjaro deurilkkayo

Meaning: What size box would you like?

Good to know

Post offices sell their own sturdy boxes numbered one to six, from a small number one to a large number six, for a few hundred to a couple of thousand won. Tape is available at the packing desk.
