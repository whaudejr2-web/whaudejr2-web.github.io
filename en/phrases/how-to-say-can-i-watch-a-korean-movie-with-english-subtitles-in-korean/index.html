How to say "Can I watch a Korean movie with English subtitles?" in Korean (한국 영화 영어 자막으로 볼 수 있어요?)

You can say this

한국 영화 영어 자막으로 볼 수 있어요?

hanguk yeonghwa yeongeo jamageuro bol su isseoyo

Meaning: Can I watch a Korean movie with English subtitles?

Good to know

A few Seoul multiplexes, including CGV Yongsan and CGV Myeongdong, run selected Korean films with English subtitles. Look for the English subtitle tag on the showtime list.
