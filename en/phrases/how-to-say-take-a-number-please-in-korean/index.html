What does "번호표 뽑으세요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

번호표 뽑으세요.

beonhopyo ppobeuseyo

Meaning: Take a number, please.

Good to know

Famous bakeries like Sungsimdang in Daejeon have lines out the door. Many use a ticket machine; your number appears on a screen when it is your turn.
