How to say "What's the most popular item right now?" in Korean (요즘 제일 잘 나가는 게 뭐예요?)

You can say this

요즘 제일 잘 나가는 게 뭐예요?

yojeum jeil jal naganeun ge mwoyeyo

Meaning: What's the most popular item right now?

Good to know

Olive Young is Korea's largest health and beauty chain, with more than 1,300 stores. Busy branches mark their best sellers with ranking tags on the shelf, so you can also just look for number one.
