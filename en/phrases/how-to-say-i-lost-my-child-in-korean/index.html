How to say "I lost my child" in Korean (아이를 잃어버렸어요)

You can say this

아이를 잃어버렸어요.

aireul ireobeoryeosseoyo

Meaning: I lost my child.

Good to know

Information desks hand out free lost-child wristbands or stickers where you write your phone number. If a child goes missing, staff page the guardian to the lost child center.
