What does "소맥 말아 드릴까요?" mean in Korean?

You will hear this

소맥 말아 드릴까요?

somaek mara deurilkkayo

Meaning: Shall I mix you a soju and beer?

Good to know

Soju poured into a glass of beer is the most common mixed drink in Korea. Koreans say they roll it, and everyone has an opinion on the ratio, often three parts soju to seven parts beer.
