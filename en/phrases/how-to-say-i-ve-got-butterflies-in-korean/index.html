How to say "I've got butterflies" in Korean (설레요)

You can say this

설레요.

seolleyo

Meaning: I've got butterflies.

Good to know

Korean has a word just for the fluttery feeling before something you are looking forward to, like a trip or a first date. It is not quite excited and not quite nervous, and English has no single word for it.
