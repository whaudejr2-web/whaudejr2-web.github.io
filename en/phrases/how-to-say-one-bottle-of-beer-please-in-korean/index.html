How to say "One bottle of beer, please" in Korean (맥주 한 병 주세요)

You can say this

맥주 한 병 주세요.

maekju han byeong juseyo

Meaning: One bottle of beer, please.

Good to know

Before a counting word, the first four native numbers shorten: one, two, three and four each lose their last sound. That is why one bottle sounds different from the number one on its own.
