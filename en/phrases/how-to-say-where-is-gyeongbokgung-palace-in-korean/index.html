How to say "Where is Gyeongbokgung Palace?" in Korean (경복궁이 어디예요?)

You can say this

경복궁이 어디예요?

gyeongbokgungi eodiyeyo

Meaning: Where is Gyeongbokgung Palace?

Good to know

Gyeongbokgung was the main royal palace of the Joseon dynasty, first built in 1395. It is closed on Tuesdays, and admission is free if you arrive in traditional Korean dress, which you can rent nearby.
