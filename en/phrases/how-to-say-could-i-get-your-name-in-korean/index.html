How to say "Could I get your name?" in Korean (선생님 성함이 어떻게 되세요?)

You can say this

선생님 성함이 어떻게 되세요?

seonsaengnim seonghami eotteoke doeseyo

Meaning: Could I get your name?

Good to know

Stylists in Korea are called designers, and customers address them as teacher, a polite title also used for doctors and tutors. Asking for the same designer by name on your next visit is completely normal.
