How to say "Are you there right now?" in Korean (지금 있어요?)

You can say this

지금 있어요?

jigeum isseoyo

Meaning: Are you there right now?

Good to know

KakaoTalk shows a small number 1 next to a message until the other person reads it. When the 1 disappears they have seen it, so Koreans often say the 1 went away instead of it was read.
