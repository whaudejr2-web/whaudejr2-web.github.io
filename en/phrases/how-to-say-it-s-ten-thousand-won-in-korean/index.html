How to say "It's ten thousand won" in Korean (만 원이에요)

You can say this

만 원이에요.

man wonieyo

Meaning: It's ten thousand won.

Good to know

Korean prices are counted in units of ten thousand, which has its own word. Fifty thousand won is said as five ten thousands, and the largest bill is fifty thousand won.
