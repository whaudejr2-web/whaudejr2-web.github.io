How to say "ARMY, make some noise! Said from the stage" in Korean (아미 여러분, 소리 질러 주세요)

You can say this

아미 여러분, 소리 질러 주세요!

ami yeoreobun sori jilleo juseyo

Meaning: ARMY, make some noise! Said from the stage.
