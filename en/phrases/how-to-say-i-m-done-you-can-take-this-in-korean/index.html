How to say "I'm done. You can take this" in Korean (다 먹었어요. 치워 주셔도 돼요)

You can say this

다 먹었어요. 치워 주셔도 돼요.

da meogeosseoyo chiwo jusyeodo dwaeyo

Meaning: I'm done. You can take this.
