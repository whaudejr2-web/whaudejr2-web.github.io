How to say "The lights on the bridge are amazing" in Korean (다리 불빛이 정말 멋있어요)

You can say this

다리 불빛이 정말 멋있어요.

dari bulbichi jeongmal meosisseoyo

Meaning: The lights on the bridge are amazing.
