How to say "Do you have a helmet?" in Korean (헬멧 있어요?)

You can say this

헬멧 있어요?

helmet isseoyo

Meaning: Do you have a helmet?

Good to know

Public bikes come without a helmet. A 2018 law made helmets mandatory for cyclists but set no fine, so most riders in Seoul skip them. For electric scooters the fine is real.
