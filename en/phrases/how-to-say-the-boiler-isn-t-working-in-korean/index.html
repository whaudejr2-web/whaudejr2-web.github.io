How to say "The boiler isn't working" in Korean (보일러가 안 돼요)

You can say this

보일러가 안 돼요.

boilleoga an dwaeyo

Meaning: The boiler isn't working.

Good to know

Korean homes are heated by hot water pipes under the floor, run by a gas boiler usually on the balcony or in the kitchen. A dead boiler means no heat and no hot water.
