How to say "Hi. Casual" in Korean (안녕)

You can say this

안녕.

annyeong

Meaning: Hi. Casual.

Good to know

The casual form drops the polite ending. Use it with close friends, children or people younger than you; to a stranger or an elder it sounds rude.
