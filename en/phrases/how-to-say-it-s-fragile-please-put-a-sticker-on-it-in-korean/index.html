How to say "It's fragile. Please put a sticker on it" in Korean (깨지기 쉬운 거예요. 스티커 붙여 주세요)

You can say this

깨지기 쉬운 거예요. 스티커 붙여 주세요.

kkaejigi swiun geoyeyo. seutikeo buchyeo juseyo

Meaning: It's fragile. Please put a sticker on it.
