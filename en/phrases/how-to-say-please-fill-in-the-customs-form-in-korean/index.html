What does "세관 신고서 작성해 주세요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

세관 신고서 작성해 주세요.

segwan singoseo jakseonghae juseyo

Meaning: Please fill in the customs form.

Good to know

The customs declaration lists each item, its quantity and value in English, and the receiving country uses it to decide on duties. If you register the shipment on the Korea Post website first, the clerk just prints it.
