How to say "I study Korean thirty minutes every day" in Korean (매일 삼십 분씩 한국어 공부해요)

You can say this

매일 삼십 분씩 한국어 공부해요.

maeil samsip bunssik hangugeo gongbuhaeyo

Meaning: I study Korean thirty minutes every day.

Good to know

Studying is serious business in Korea. In 2023, almost eight out of ten elementary, middle and high school students attended private after-school academies, according to Statistics Korea.
