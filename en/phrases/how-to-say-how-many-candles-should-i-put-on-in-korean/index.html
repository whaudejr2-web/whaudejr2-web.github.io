How to say "How many candles should I put on?" in Korean (초 몇 개 꽂을까요?)

You can say this

초 몇 개 꽂을까요?

cho myeot gae kkojeulkkayo

Meaning: How many candles should I put on?

Good to know

Bakeries in Korea hand out candles, matches and a plastic knife with every cake. The usual code is one long candle for every ten years and one short candle for each extra year.
