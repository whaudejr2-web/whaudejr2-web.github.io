How to say "Let me do the New Year's bow" in Korean (세배 드릴게요)

You can say this

세배 드릴게요.

sebae deurilgeyo

Meaning: Let me do the New Year's bow.

Good to know

On Seollal morning, younger family members kneel and do a deep bow to grandparents and parents while wishing them a good year. Elders answer with a blessing and usually pocket money.
