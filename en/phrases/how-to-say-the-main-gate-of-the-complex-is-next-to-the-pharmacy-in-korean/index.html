How to say "The main gate of the complex is next to the pharmacy" in Korean (약국 옆에 아파트 정문이 있어요)

You can say this

약국 옆에 아파트 정문이 있어요.

yakguk yeope apateu jeongmuni isseoyo

Meaning: The main gate of the complex is next to the pharmacy.
