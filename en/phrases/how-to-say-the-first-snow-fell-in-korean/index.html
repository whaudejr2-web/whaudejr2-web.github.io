How to say "The first snow fell" in Korean (첫눈이 왔어요)

You can say this

첫눈이 왔어요!

cheonnuni wasseoyo

Meaning: The first snow fell!

Good to know

The first snow in Seoul makes the national news every year. Meeting someone you like on the day of the first snow is a classic romantic promise in Korea, and a favorite scene in Korean dramas.
