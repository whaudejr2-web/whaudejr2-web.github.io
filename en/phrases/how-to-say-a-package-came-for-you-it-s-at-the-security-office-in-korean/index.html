How to say "A package came for you. It's at the security office" in Korean (택배 왔어요. 경비실에 있어요)

You can say this

택배 왔어요. 경비실에 있어요.

taekbae wasseoyo. gyeongbisire isseoyo

Meaning: A package came for you. It's at the security office.
