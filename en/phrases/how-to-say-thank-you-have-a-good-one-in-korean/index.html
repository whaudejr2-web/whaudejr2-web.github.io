How to say "Thank you. Have a good one" in Korean (감사합니다. 수고하세요)

You can say this

감사합니다. 수고하세요.

gamsahamnida sugohaseyo

Meaning: Thank you. Have a good one.

Good to know

This send-off literally means keep up the good work, and Koreans say it to anyone on the job: riders, cashiers, taxi drivers. It takes the place of a tip, which is not expected in Korea.
