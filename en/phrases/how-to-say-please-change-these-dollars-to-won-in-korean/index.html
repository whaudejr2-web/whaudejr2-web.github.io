How to say "Please change these dollars to won" in Korean (달러를 원화로 바꿔 주세요)

You can say this

달러를 원화로 바꿔 주세요.

dalleoreul wonhwaro bakkwo juseyo

Meaning: Please change these dollars to won.

Good to know

Airport counters usually give a worse rate than exchange booths in the city, so many travelers change only a small amount here. Korean bills come in 1,000, 5,000, 10,000 and 50,000 won.
