What does "봉투 필요하세요?" mean in Korean?

You will hear this

봉투 필요하세요?

bongtu piryohaseyo

Meaning: Do you need a bag?

Good to know

Bags are not free in Korea. The cashier will ask, and a plastic or paper bag costs a small fee, usually around one hundred won, so many people just carry their items out by hand.
