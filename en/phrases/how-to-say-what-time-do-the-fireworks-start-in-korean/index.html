How to say "What time do the fireworks start?" in Korean (불꽃놀이는 몇 시에 시작해요?)

You can say this

불꽃놀이는 몇 시에 시작해요?

bulkkonnorineun myeot sie sijakaeyo

Meaning: What time do the fireworks start?
