What does "봉투 필요하세요?" mean in Korean?

You will hear this

봉투 필요하세요?

bongtu piryohaseyo

Meaning: Do you need a bag?

Good to know

Korea banned free plastic bags at large supermarkets in 2019, and most shops now charge a small fee, often around 100 won, for a paper or plastic bag. That is why staff ask before giving you one.
