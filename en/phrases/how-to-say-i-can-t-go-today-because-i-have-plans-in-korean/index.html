How to say "I can't go today. Because I have plans" in Korean (오늘은 못 가요. 왜냐하면 약속이 있거든요)

You can say this

오늘은 못 가요. 왜냐하면 약속이 있거든요.

oneureun mot gayo. waenyahamyeon yaksogi itgeodeunyo

Meaning: I can't go today. Because I have plans.
