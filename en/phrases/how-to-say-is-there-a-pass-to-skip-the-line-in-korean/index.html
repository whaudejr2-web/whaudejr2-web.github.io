How to say "Is there a pass to skip the line?" in Korean (줄 안 서는 패스 있어요?)

You can say this

줄 안 서는 패스 있어요?

jul an seoneun paeseu isseoyo

Meaning: Is there a pass to skip the line?

Good to know

Skip-the-line options differ by park: Lotte World sells a paid Magic Pass, while Everland's smart queue is free in its app and gives you a return time for the ride.
