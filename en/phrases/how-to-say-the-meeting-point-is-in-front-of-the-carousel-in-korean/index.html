How to say "The meeting point is in front of the carousel" in Korean (만나는 장소는 회전목마 앞이에요)

You can say this

만나는 장소는 회전목마 앞이에요.

mannaneun jangsoneun hoejeonmongma apieyo

Meaning: The meeting point is in front of the carousel.
