How to say "I grant you the poison. The king's death sentence" in Korean (사약을 내리노라)

You can say this

사약을 내리노라.

sayageul naerinora

Meaning: I grant you the poison. The king's death sentence.

Good to know

Literally: bestowed medicine. In Joseon a noble condemned to death was sent a bowl of poison instead of the executioner, and in dramas the victim bows and thanks the king before drinking it.
