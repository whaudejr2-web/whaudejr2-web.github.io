How to say "Got it. I'll save the address" in Korean (알겠어요. 주소 저장해 둘게요)

You can say this

알겠어요. 주소 저장해 둘게요.

algesseoyo juso jeojanghae dulgeyo

Meaning: Got it. I'll save the address.
