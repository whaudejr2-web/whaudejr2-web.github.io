How to say "It's nutty and savory" in Korean (고소해요)

You can say this

고소해요.

gosohaeyo

Meaning: It's nutty and savory.

Good to know

This taste word has no English equivalent. It describes the rich, toasty flavor of sesame oil, roasted nuts or toasted rice, and Koreans use it all the time.
