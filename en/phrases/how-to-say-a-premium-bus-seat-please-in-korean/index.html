How to say "A premium bus seat, please" in Korean (우등으로 주세요)

You can say this

우등으로 주세요.

udeungeuro juseyo

Meaning: A premium bus seat, please.

Good to know

Express buses come in standard and premium classes. A premium bus has 28 wide seats in a two plus one layout instead of 45, and the fare is higher.
