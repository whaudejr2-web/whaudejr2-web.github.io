How to say "What time is the women divers' show?" in Korean (해녀 공연은 몇 시예요?)

You can say this

해녀 공연은 몇 시예요?

haenyeo gongyeoneun myeot siyeyo

Meaning: What time is the women divers' show?

Good to know

These women free-dive without oxygen tanks to gather abalone, sea urchin, and seaweed, some well into their eighties. UNESCO added the tradition to its heritage list in 2016.
