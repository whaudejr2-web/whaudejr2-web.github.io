How to say "Mind your manners" in Korean (매너 좀 지켜요)

You can say this

매너 좀 지켜요.

maeneo jom jikyeoyo

Meaning: Mind your manners.

Good to know

Manner is one of the most used English loanwords in Korean gaming. A rude player is called no manner, and manner play means going easy on a weaker opponent or being polite in a fight.
