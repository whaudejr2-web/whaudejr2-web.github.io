How to say "Where do I hang a lotus lantern?" in Korean (연등은 어디서 달아요?)

You can say this

연등은 어디서 달아요?

yeondeungeun eodiseo darayo

Meaning: Where do I hang a lotus lantern?

Good to know

Lotus lanterns are hung for Buddha's Birthday, the eighth day of the fourth lunar month, usually in May. The Lotus Lantern Festival has been on the UNESCO heritage list since 2020.
