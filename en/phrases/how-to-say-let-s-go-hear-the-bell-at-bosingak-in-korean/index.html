How to say "Let's go hear the bell at Bosingak" in Korean (보신각 종소리 들으러 가요)

You can say this

보신각 종소리 들으러 가요.

bosingak jongsori deureureo gayo

Meaning: Let's go hear the bell at Bosingak.

Good to know

At midnight on New Year's Eve the Bosingak bell in central Seoul is struck thirty-three times, and the ceremony is shown live on TV.
