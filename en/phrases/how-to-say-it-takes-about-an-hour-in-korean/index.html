How to say "It takes about an hour" in Korean (한 시간쯤 걸려요)

You can say this

한 시간쯤 걸려요.

han siganjjeum geollyeoyo

Meaning: It takes about an hour.

Good to know

Korean has one word for the hour on the clock and a different word for an hour of duration. Both take native Korean numbers, so one hour and one o'clock start with the same number.
