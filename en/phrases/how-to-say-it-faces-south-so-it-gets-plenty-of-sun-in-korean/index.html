What does "남향이라서 햇빛이 아주 잘 들어요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

남향이라서 햇빛이 아주 잘 들어요.

namhyangiraseo haetbichi aju jal deureoyo

Meaning: It faces south, so it gets plenty of sun.
