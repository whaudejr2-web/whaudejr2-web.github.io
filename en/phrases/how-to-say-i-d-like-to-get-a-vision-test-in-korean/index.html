How to say "I'd like to get a vision test" in Korean (시력 검사 받고 싶어요)

You can say this

시력 검사 받고 싶어요.

siryeok geomsa batgo sipeoyo

Meaning: I'd like to get a vision test.

Good to know

In Korea you do not need a doctor's prescription to buy glasses. Optical shops test your eyes for free with their own machines, and the eye clinic is only needed if something looks wrong.
