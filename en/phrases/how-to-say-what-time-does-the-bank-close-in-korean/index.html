How to say "What time does the bank close?" in Korean (은행은 몇 시까지 해요?)

You can say this

은행은 몇 시까지 해요?

eunhaengeun myeot sikkaji haeyo

Meaning: What time does the bank close?

Good to know

Korean banks are usually open from nine in the morning to four in the afternoon on weekdays, and they close on weekends and public holidays. Plan exchanges and transfers around that.
