How to say "Put the rice in, please" in Korean (말아 주세요)

You can say this

말아 주세요.

mara juseyo

Meaning: Put the rice in, please.

  • 밥은 말아 드릴까요, 따로 드릴까요?Rice in the soup, or on the side?

  • 해장국은 뭐가 있어요?What hangover soups do you have?

  • 뼈해장국으로 주세요.The pork bone one, please.
