What does "공연 중에는 사진 촬영 안 돼요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

공연 중에는 사진 촬영 안 돼요.

gongyeon jungeneun sajin chwaryeong an dwaeyo

Meaning: No photos during the show.

Good to know

Filming during the performance is banned at most Korean concerts, and ushers patrol the aisles with warning signs. Some tours allow photos only during the final encore.
