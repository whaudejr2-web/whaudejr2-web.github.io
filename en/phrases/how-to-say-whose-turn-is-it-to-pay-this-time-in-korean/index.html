How to say "Whose turn is it to pay this time?" in Korean (이번엔 누가 낼 차례예요?)

You can say this

이번엔 누가 낼 차례예요?

ibeonen nuga nael charyeyeyo

Meaning: Whose turn is it to pay this time?

Good to know

Among Korean friends it is common for one person to pay the whole bill and the other to cover the next stop, like coffee. Splitting evenly is also normal now, especially among younger people.
