How to say "Thank you for your hard work" in Korean (수고하셨습니다)

You can say this

수고하셨습니다.

sugohasyeotseumnida

Meaning: Thank you for your hard work.

Good to know

Said to staff and crew at the end of an event, this means roughly: you worked hard. Koreans say it instead of thank you whenever someone has done a job for them, including taxi drivers and delivery workers.
