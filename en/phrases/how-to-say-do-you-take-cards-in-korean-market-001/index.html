How to say "Do you take cards?" in Korean (카드 돼요?)

You can say this

카드 돼요?

kadeu dwaeyo

Meaning: Do you take cards?

Good to know

Most stalls at big markets like Gwangjang now take cards and even mobile pay, but a few tiny carts are still cash only, so it helps to carry some small bills.
