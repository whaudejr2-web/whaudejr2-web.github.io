How to say "Please replace the heels on these shoes" in Korean (구두 굽 좀 갈아 주세요)

You can say this

구두 굽 좀 갈아 주세요.

gudu gup jom gara juseyo

Meaning: Please replace the heels on these shoes.

Listen and repeat
