How to say "When is the year-end party?" in Korean (송년회 언제 해요?)

You can say this

송년회 언제 해요?

songnyeonhoe eonje haeyo

Meaning: When is the year-end party?

Good to know

December fills up with year-end dinners with coworkers and old friends, so popular restaurants are booked early.
