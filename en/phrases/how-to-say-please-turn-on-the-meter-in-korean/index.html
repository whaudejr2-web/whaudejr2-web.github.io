How to say "Please turn on the meter" in Korean (미터기 켜 주세요)

You can say this

미터기 켜 주세요.

miteogi kyeo juseyo

Meaning: Please turn on the meter.

Good to know

Every licensed taxi runs on the meter, and fares are set by the city. In Seoul the meter starts at 4,800 won, and expressway tolls are added on top at the end of the ride.
