How to say "Young people's slang is so hard these days" in Korean (요즘 젊은 사람들 말이 너무 어려워요)

You can say this

요즘 젊은 사람들 말이 너무 어려워요.

yojeum jeolmeun saramdeul mari neomu eoryeowoyo

Meaning: Young people's slang is so hard these days.
