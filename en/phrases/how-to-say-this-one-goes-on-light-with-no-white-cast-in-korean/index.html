How to say "This one goes on light with no white cast" in Korean (이건 백탁 없이 가볍게 발려요)

You can say this

이건 백탁 없이 가볍게 발려요.

igeon baektak eopsi gabyeopge ballyeoyo

Meaning: This one goes on light with no white cast.
