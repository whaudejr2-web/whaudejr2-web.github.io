How to say "How much if I sign up for three months?" in Korean (삼 개월 끊으면 얼마예요?)

You can say this

삼 개월 끊으면 얼마예요?

sam gaewol kkeuneumyeon eolmayeyo

Meaning: How much if I sign up for three months?

Good to know

Koreans say they cut a membership, using the same verb as for buying a ticket. It works for gyms, yoga studios and monthly transit passes alike.
