How to say "After 108 bows, I'm sweating" in Korean (백팔 배 하고 나니까 땀이 나요)

You can say this

백팔 배 하고 나니까 땀이 나요.

baekpal bae hago nanikka ttami nayo

Meaning: After 108 bows, I'm sweating.

Good to know

The 108 bows stand for the 108 worldly desires in Buddhist teaching. Many programs have you string a bracelet of 108 beads, one bead after each bow.
