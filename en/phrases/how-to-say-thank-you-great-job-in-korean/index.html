How to say "Thank you. Great job" in Korean (감사합니다. 수고하셨어요)

You can say this

감사합니다. 수고하셨어요.

gamsahamnida sugohasyeosseoyo

Meaning: Thank you. Great job.

Good to know

Literally: you worked hard. It is the standard way to thank someone for their service, so it fits perfectly as you step off the plane.
