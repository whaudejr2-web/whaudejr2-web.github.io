How to say "Can I order food to my seat?" in Korean (자리에서 음식 주문 돼요?)

You can say this

자리에서 음식 주문 돼요?

jarieseo eumsik jumun dwaeyo

Meaning: Can I order food to my seat?

Good to know

Food is a huge part of PC bang culture. You order from a menu on your screen and staff bring it to your desk, so you never have to leave the game.
