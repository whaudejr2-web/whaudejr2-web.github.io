How to say "Can I pick one straight from the tank?" in Korean (수족관에서 직접 골라도 돼요?)

You can say this

수족관에서 직접 골라도 돼요?

sujokgwaneseo jikjeop gollado dwaeyo

Meaning: Can I pick one straight from the tank?
