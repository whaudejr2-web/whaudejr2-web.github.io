How to say "Don't leave me" in Korean (저 버리지 마세요)

You can say this

저 버리지 마세요.

jeo beoriji maseyo

Meaning: Don't leave me.

Good to know

버리다 literally means to throw away, so the line is closer to don't throw me away. That is why it hits so hard.
