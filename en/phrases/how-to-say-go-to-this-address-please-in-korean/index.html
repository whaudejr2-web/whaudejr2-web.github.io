How to say "Go to this address, please" in Korean (이 주소로 가 주세요)

You can say this

이 주소로 가 주세요.

i jusoro ga juseyo

Meaning: Go to this address, please.

Good to know

Show the address in Korean on your phone and the driver will type it into the car's navigation. Naver Map or Kakao Map show Korean addresses more reliably than Google Maps.
