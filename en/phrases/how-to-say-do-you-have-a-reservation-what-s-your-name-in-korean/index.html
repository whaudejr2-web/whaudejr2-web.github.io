What does "예약하셨어요? 성함이 어떻게 되세요?" mean in Korean?

You will hear this

예약하셨어요? 성함이 어떻게 되세요?

yeyakasyeosseoyo seonghami eotteoke doeseyo

Meaning: Do you have a reservation? What's your name?
