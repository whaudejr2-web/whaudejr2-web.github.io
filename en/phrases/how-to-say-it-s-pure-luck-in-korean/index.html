How to say "It's pure luck" in Korean (복불복이에요)

You can say this

복불복이에요.

bokbulbogieyo

Meaning: It's pure luck.

Good to know

Literally: luck or no luck. The word took over Korean TV through the games on the travel show 1 Night 2 Days, where one wrong pick means sleeping outside in winter or skipping dinner.
