How to say "Where can I buy a light stick?" in Korean (응원봉 어디서 사요?)

You can say this

응원봉 어디서 사요?

eungwonbong eodiseo sayo

Meaning: Where can I buy a light stick?

Good to know

Many official light sticks pair with the venue by Bluetooth. You enter your seat number in the app, and during the show the whole arena changes colors in sync with the music.
