How to say "Let's watch the award show together next year too" in Korean (내년에도 같이 시상식 봐요)

You can say this

내년에도 같이 시상식 봐요.

naenyeonedo gachi sisangsik bwayo

Meaning: Let's watch the award show together next year too.
