How to say "My number is zero one zero, two three four five, six seven eight nine" in Korean (제 번호는 공일공 이삼사오 육칠팔구예요)

You can say this

제 번호는 공일공 이삼사오 육칠팔구예요.

je beonhoneun gongilgong isamsao yukchilpalguyeyo

Meaning: My number is zero one zero, two three four five, six seven eight nine.

Good to know

Every Korean mobile number starts with zero one zero, followed by eight digits. Koreans read each digit aloud and pause after the first three and the next four, and the leading zero is pronounced like the word for ball.
