How to say "The salt bread is the most popular" in Korean (소금빵이 제일 인기 많아요)

You can say this

소금빵이 제일 인기 많아요.

sogeumppangi jeil ingi manayo

Meaning: The salt bread is the most popular.

Good to know

Salt bread is a buttery roll with coarse salt on top. It came from Japan and became a huge trend in Korean bakeries around 2023, when popular shops sold out within an hour of each batch.
