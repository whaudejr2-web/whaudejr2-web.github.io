What does "삼십 초 남았어요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

삼십 초 남았어요.

samsip cho namasseoyo

Meaning: Thirty seconds left.

Good to know

Each fan usually gets one to two minutes per member. A staff member stands behind the table, calls out the time and moves you to the next seat, so most fans rehearse their lines in advance.
