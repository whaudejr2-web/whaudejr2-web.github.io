How to say "We're going on an MT this weekend" in Korean (이번 주말에 엠티 가요)

You can say this

이번 주말에 엠티 가요.

ibeon jumare emti gayo

Meaning: We're going on an MT this weekend.

Good to know

MT stands for membership training: an overnight trip by a department or club, usually to a rented pension outside the city, with games, cooking and plenty of drinking.
