What does "현금영수증 필요하세요?" mean in Korean?

You will hear this

현금영수증 필요하세요?

hyeongeumyeongsujeung piryohaseyo

Meaning: Do you need a cash receipt?

Good to know

A cash receipt is a tax record that Korean residents use for deductions, and cashiers ask about it whenever you pay in cash. As a visitor you can simply say no.
