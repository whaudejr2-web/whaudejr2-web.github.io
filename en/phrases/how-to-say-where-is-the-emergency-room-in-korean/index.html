How to say "Where is the emergency room?" in Korean (응급실이 어디예요?)

You can say this

응급실이 어디예요?

eunggeupsiri eodiyeyo

Meaning: Where is the emergency room?

Good to know

In an emergency, dial 119. Public ambulances are free of charge, and the dispatcher can bring an interpreter onto the call.
