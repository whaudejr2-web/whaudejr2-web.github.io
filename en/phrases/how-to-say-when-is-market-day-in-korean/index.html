How to say "When is market day?" in Korean (오일장이 언제예요?)

You can say this

오일장이 언제예요?

oiljangi eonjeyeyo

Meaning: When is market day?

Good to know

Many Korean towns hold a traditional market every five days, on fixed dates such as the second and seventh of each month. City neighborhoods have permanent markets instead.
