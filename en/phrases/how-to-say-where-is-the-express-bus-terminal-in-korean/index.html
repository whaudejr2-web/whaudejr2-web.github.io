How to say "Where is the express bus terminal?" in Korean (고속버스터미널이 어디예요?)

You can say this

고속버스터미널이 어디예요?

gosokbeoseuteomineori eodiyeyo

Meaning: Where is the express bus terminal?

Good to know

Seoul Express Bus Terminal is the main hub for buses to the south of the country. Its subway station, Express Bus Terminal, is served by lines three, seven and nine.
