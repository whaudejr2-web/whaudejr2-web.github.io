How to say "Where is the smoking room?" in Korean (흡연실이 어디예요?)

You can say this

흡연실이 어디예요?

heubyeonsiri eodiyeyo

Meaning: Where is the smoking room?

Good to know

The whole terminal is nonsmoking, but there are enclosed smoking rooms airside near several gates. Their number has been cut over the years, so ask staff for the nearest one.
