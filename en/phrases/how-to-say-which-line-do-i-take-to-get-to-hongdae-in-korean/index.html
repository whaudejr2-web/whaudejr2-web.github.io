How to say "Which line do I take to get to Hongdae?" in Korean (홍대 가려면 몇 호선 타야 돼요?)

You can say this

홍대 가려면 몇 호선 타야 돼요?

hongdae garyeomyeon myeot hoseon taya dwaeyo

Meaning: Which line do I take to get to Hongdae?

Good to know

Seoul subway lines are numbered 1 through 9, and each line has its own color on every sign and map. Several newer lines go by names instead of numbers.
