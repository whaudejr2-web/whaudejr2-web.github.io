How to say "Where do I hand in the gift money?" in Korean (축의금 접수는 어디서 해요?)

You can say this

축의금 접수는 어디서 해요?

chuguigeum jeopsuneun eodiseo haeyo

Meaning: Where do I hand in the gift money?
