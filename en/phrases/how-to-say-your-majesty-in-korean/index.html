How to say "Your Majesty" in Korean (전하)

You can say this

전하.

jeonha

Meaning: Your Majesty.

Good to know

The title for a Joseon king. Crown princes, queens and emperors each get a different one, and the next line is the queen's. Modern Korea has no royals, so today this word lives only on screen.
