How to say "How much is the fee?" in Korean (수수료가 얼마예요?)

You can say this

수수료가 얼마예요?

susuryoga eolmayeyo

Meaning: How much is the fee?

Good to know

Korean ATMs charge a fee of a few thousand won for each foreign card withdrawal, and your own bank may add another. One larger withdrawal is cheaper than several small ones.
