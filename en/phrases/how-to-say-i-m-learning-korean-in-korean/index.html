How to say "I'm learning Korean" in Korean (한국어 배우고 있어요)

You can say this

한국어 배우고 있어요.

hangugeo baeugo isseoyo

Meaning: I'm learning Korean.

Good to know

The official Korean proficiency exam is called TOPIK. It is held several times a year around the world and is used for university admission and some visa applications.
