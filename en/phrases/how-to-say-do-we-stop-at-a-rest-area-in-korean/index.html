How to say "Do we stop at a rest area?" in Korean (휴게소에 들러요?)

You can say this

휴게소에 들러요?

hyugesoe deulleoyo

Meaning: Do we stop at a rest area?

Good to know

On longer routes the bus stops once at a highway rest area for about fifteen minutes. Rest areas are known for their food, like walnut cakes and grilled whole potatoes.
