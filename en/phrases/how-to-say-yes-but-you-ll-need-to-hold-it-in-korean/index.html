How to say "Yes, but you'll need to hold it" in Korean (네, 대신 안고 계셔야 돼요)

You can say this

네, 대신 안고 계셔야 돼요.

ne daesin ango gyesyeoya dwaeyo

Meaning: Yes, but you'll need to hold it.
