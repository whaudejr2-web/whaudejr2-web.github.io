How to say "What time is the parade?" in Korean (퍼레이드 몇 시에 해요?)

You can say this

퍼레이드 몇 시에 해요?

peoreideu myeot sie haeyo

Meaning: What time is the parade?

Good to know

Everland runs a daytime carnival parade and a lit-up night parade, and Lotte World has its own indoor parade. People start sitting along the route well before it begins.
