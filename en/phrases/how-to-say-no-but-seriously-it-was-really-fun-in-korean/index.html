How to say "No, but seriously, it was really fun" in Korean (아니, 근데 진짜 재밌었어요)

You can say this

아니, 근데 진짜 재밌었어요.

ani geunde jinjja jaemisseosseoyo

Meaning: No, but seriously, it was really fun.

Good to know

The first word means no, but here it is only a sentence starter, like no but seriously. Do not take it as a disagreement.
