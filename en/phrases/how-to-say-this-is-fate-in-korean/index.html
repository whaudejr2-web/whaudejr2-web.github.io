How to say "This is fate" in Korean (이건 운명이에요)

You can say this

이건 운명이에요.

igeon unmyeongieyo

Meaning: This is fate.

Good to know

Romance is one of the biggest webtoon genres, and two strangers meeting by fate, often explained later by a past life, is one of its most used openings.
