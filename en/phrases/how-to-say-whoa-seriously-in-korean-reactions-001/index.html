How to say "Whoa, seriously?" in Korean (헐, 진짜예요?)

You can say this

헐, 진짜예요?

heol jinjjayeyo

Meaning: Whoa, seriously?

Good to know

The first word is internet slang from the early two thousands that spread into everyday speech. It is fine among friends, but skip it with your boss or a host.
