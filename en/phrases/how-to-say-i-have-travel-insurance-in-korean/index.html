How to say "I have travel insurance" in Korean (여행자 보험이 있어요)

You can say this

여행자 보험이 있어요.

yeohaengja boheomi isseoyo

Meaning: I have travel insurance.

Good to know

Travelers without Korean health insurance pay the full fee on the spot. Ask for an itemized receipt and a medical certificate; your insurer will want both for the claim.
