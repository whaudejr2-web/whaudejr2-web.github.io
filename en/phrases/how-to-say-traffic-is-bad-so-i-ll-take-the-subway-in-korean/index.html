How to say "Traffic is bad. So I'll take the subway" in Korean (길이 막혀요. 그래서 지하철로 갈게요)

You can say this

길이 막혀요. 그래서 지하철로 갈게요.

giri makyeoyo. geuraeseo jihacheollo galgeyo

Meaning: Traffic is bad. So I'll take the subway.
