How to say "It's fun, but there's so much homework" in Korean (재미있는데 과제가 너무 많아요)

You can say this

재미있는데 과제가 너무 많아요.

jaemiinneunde gwajega neomu manayo

Meaning: It's fun, but there's so much homework.
