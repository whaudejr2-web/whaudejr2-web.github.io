How to say "The broth is in the kettle" in Korean (육수는 주전자에 있어요)

You can say this

육수는 주전자에 있어요.

yuksuneun jujeonjae isseoyo

Meaning: The broth is in the kettle.

Good to know

Cold noodle shops put a metal kettle of broth on the table, warm in winter and chilled in summer. Pour yourself a cup to sip while you wait for the noodles.
