How to say "What's the address here?" in Korean (여기 주소가 뭐예요?)

You can say this

여기 주소가 뭐예요?

yeogi jusoga mwoyeyo

Meaning: What's the address here?

Good to know

Every building in Korea has a blue plate by its entrance showing the road name and building number. Reading that plate aloud is the fastest way to tell a 119 operator where you are.
