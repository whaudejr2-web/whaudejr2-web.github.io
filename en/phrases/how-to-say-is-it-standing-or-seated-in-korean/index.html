How to say "Is it standing or seated?" in Korean (스탠딩이에요, 좌석이에요?)

You can say this

스탠딩이에요, 좌석이에요?

seutaendingieyo jwaseogieyo

Meaning: Is it standing or seated?

Good to know

Standing tickets in Korea carry an entry number, and staff call fans in by number in blocks. Arriving early does not get you closer to the stage than your number allows.
