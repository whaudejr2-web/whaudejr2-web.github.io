How to say "I don't have an appointment. Can you take me now?" in Korean (예약 안 했는데 지금 돼요?)

You can say this

예약 안 했는데 지금 돼요?

yeyak an haenneunde jigeum dwaeyo

Meaning: I don't have an appointment. Can you take me now?

Good to know

Neighborhood salons often take walk-ins, but popular shops in areas like Hongdae and Gangnam fill up through the Naver booking system, so on weekends it is safer to reserve ahead.
