How to say "I really waited for them during the military break" in Korean (군백기 동안 정말 기다렸어요)

You can say this

군백기 동안 정말 기다렸어요.

gunbaekgi dongan jeongmal gidaryeosseoyo

Meaning: I really waited for them during the military break.

Good to know

All seven members finished their mandatory military service by June 2025. Fans call that wait with a word that joins military and blank period.
