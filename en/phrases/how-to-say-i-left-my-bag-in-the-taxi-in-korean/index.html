How to say "I left my bag in the taxi" in Korean (택시에 가방을 두고 내렸어요)

You can say this

택시에 가방을 두고 내렸어요.

taeksie gabangeul dugo naeryeosseoyo

Meaning: I left my bag in the taxi.

Good to know

If you paid by card, the receipt or your card app shows the taxi company, which is how the car gets traced. In Seoul you can also call the city's 120 help line, which has English-speaking operators.
