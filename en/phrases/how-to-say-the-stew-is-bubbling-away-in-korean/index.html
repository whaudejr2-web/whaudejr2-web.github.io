How to say "The stew is bubbling away" in Korean (찌개가 보글보글 끓어요)

You can say this

찌개가 보글보글 끓어요.

jjigaega bogeulbogeul kkeureoyo

Meaning: The stew is bubbling away.

Good to know

Small bubbles in a simmering pot. The word is so loved that the arcade game Bubble Bobble was released in Korea under this name.
