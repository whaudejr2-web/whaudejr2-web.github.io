How to say "I'm looking for a one-room studio" in Korean (원룸 찾고 있어요)

You can say this

원룸 찾고 있어요.

wollum chatgo isseoyo

Meaning: I'm looking for a one-room studio.

Good to know

A one-room is the Korean studio: a single room with a kitchenette and bathroom, usually in a low-rise building. Listings also offer one-and-a-half rooms and two rooms, counting the separate spaces.
