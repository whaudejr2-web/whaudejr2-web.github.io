How to say "I'm going to start over" in Korean (다시 시작할 거예요)

You can say this

다시 시작할 거예요.

dasi sijakal geoyeyo

Meaning: I'm going to start over.

Good to know

Going back in time to redo your life, waking up inside a novel, or being reborn as someone else: these three setups dominate Korean fantasy webtoons, so lines about starting over are everywhere.
