How to say "Do you have an umbrella?" in Korean (우산 있어요?)

You can say this

우산 있어요?

usan isseoyo

Meaning: Do you have an umbrella?

Good to know

If you get caught without one, every convenience store sells cheap umbrellas by the door. Many shops also keep plastic sleeves at the entrance so a wet umbrella does not drip inside.
