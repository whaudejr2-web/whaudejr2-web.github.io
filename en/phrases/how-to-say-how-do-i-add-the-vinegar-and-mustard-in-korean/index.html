How to say "How do I add the vinegar and mustard?" in Korean (식초랑 겨자는 어떻게 넣어요?)

You can say this

식초랑 겨자는 어떻게 넣어요?

sikchorang gyeojaneun eotteoke neoeoyo

Meaning: How do I add the vinegar and mustard?

Good to know

A bottle of vinegar and a tube of hot mustard sit on every cold noodle table. The usual way is a few drops of vinegar over the noodles and a small dab of mustard stirred into the broth.
