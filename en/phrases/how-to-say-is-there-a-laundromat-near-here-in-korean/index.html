How to say "Is there a laundromat near here?" in Korean (이 근처에 빨래방 있어요?)

You can say this

이 근처에 빨래방 있어요?

i geuncheoe ppallaebang isseoyo

Meaning: Is there a laundromat near here?

Good to know

Self-service laundromats are common in Korean cities. They are usually unstaffed, open twenty-four hours, and take cards as well as cash, with a help line number posted on the wall.
