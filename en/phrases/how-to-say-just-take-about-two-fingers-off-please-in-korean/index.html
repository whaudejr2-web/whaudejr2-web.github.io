How to say "Just take about two fingers off, please" in Korean (두 손가락 정도만 잘라 주세요)

You can say this

두 손가락 정도만 잘라 주세요.

du songarak jeongdoman jalla juseyo

Meaning: Just take about two fingers off, please.

Good to know

Koreans often describe length in fingers or knuckles instead of centimeters, and the stylist will pinch the hair to show you how much before cutting. One knuckle is roughly two to three centimeters.
