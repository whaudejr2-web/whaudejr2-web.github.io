How to say "I rented a party room" in Korean (파티룸 빌렸어요)

You can say this

파티룸 빌렸어요.

patirum billyeosseoyo

Meaning: I rented a party room.

Good to know

Party rooms are rented by the hour in most Korean cities and come with a kitchen, speakers and decorations. Since many friends live in small apartments, renting one is a common way to host.
