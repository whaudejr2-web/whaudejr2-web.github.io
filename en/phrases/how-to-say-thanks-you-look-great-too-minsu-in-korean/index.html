How to say "Thanks. You look great too, Minsu" in Korean (고마워요. 민수 씨도 멋있어요)

You can say this

고마워요. 민수 씨도 멋있어요.

gomawoyo. minsu ssido meosisseoyo

Meaning: Thanks. You look great too, Minsu.

Good to know

Koreans rarely say the word you to someone they are still getting to know. They use the person's name plus a polite title instead, even on a date.
