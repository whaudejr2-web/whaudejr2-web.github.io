How to say "I want to take photos in a hanbok" in Korean (한복 입고 사진 찍고 싶어요)

You can say this

한복 입고 사진 찍고 싶어요.

hanbok ipgo sajin jjikgo sipeoyo

Meaning: I want to take photos in a hanbok.

Good to know

Gyeongbokgung and Seoul's other royal palaces give free admission to anyone wearing hanbok. Rental shops around the palaces rent by the hour, and many also do hair and accessories.
