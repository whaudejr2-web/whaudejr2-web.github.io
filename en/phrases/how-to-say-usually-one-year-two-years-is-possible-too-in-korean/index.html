What does "보통 일 년이에요. 이 년도 가능해요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

보통 일 년이에요. 이 년도 가능해요.

botong il nyeonieyo. i nyeondo ganeunghaeyo

Meaning: Usually one year. Two years is possible too.

Good to know

Korea's housing lease law treats any contract shorter than two years as a two-year lease if the tenant wants it, so a one-year contract protects you for two. You can still leave when the first year ends.
