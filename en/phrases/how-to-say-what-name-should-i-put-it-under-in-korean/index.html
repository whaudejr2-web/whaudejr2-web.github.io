What does "성함이 어떻게 되세요?" mean in Korean?

You will hear this

성함이 어떻게 되세요?

seonghami eotteoke doeseyo

Meaning: What name should I put it under?

Good to know

This uses the honorific word for name, which staff use toward customers. When you give your own name, you use the ordinary word for name instead.
