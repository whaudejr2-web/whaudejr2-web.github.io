What does "이건 서비스로 드릴게요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

이건 서비스로 드릴게요.

igeon seobiseuro deurilgeyo

Meaning: This one is on the house.

Good to know

In Korea the English word service means a freebie. When staff say something is service, it is free, and a free side dish or drink is the most common way a restaurant makes up for a mistake.
