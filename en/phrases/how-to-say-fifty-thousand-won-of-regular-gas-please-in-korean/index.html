How to say "Fifty thousand won of regular gas, please" in Korean (휘발유 오만 원어치 넣어 주세요)

You can say this

휘발유 오만 원어치 넣어 주세요.

hwiballyu oman woneochi neoeo juseyo

Meaning: Fifty thousand won of regular gas, please.

Good to know

Gas is sold by the liter. Pump handles are color coded: yellow for gasoline and green for diesel. Most rental cars take gasoline, but check the sticker inside the fuel door before filling up.
