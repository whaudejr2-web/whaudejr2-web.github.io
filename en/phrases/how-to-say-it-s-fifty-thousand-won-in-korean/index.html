How to say "It's fifty thousand won" in Korean (오만 원이에요)

You can say this

오만 원이에요.

oman wonieyo

Meaning: It's fifty thousand won.

Good to know

The fifty thousand won note is the largest Korean bill. It was introduced in 2009 and shows a sixteenth century woman painter and poet, the first woman ever on a Korean banknote.
