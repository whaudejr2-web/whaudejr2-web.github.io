How to say "Could you throw in some samples?" in Korean (샘플 좀 챙겨 주실 수 있어요?)

You can say this

샘플 좀 챙겨 주실 수 있어요?

saempeul jom chaenggyeo jusil su isseoyo

Meaning: Could you throw in some samples?
