How to say "Where is baggage claim?" in Korean (수하물 찾는 곳이 어디예요?)

You can say this

수하물 찾는 곳이 어디예요?

suhamul channeun gosi eodiyeyo

Meaning: Where is baggage claim?

Listen and repeat
