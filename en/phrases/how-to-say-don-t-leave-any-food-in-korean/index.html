What does "음식은 남기지 마세요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

음식은 남기지 마세요.

eumsigeun namgiji maseyo

Meaning: Don't leave any food.

Good to know

In the formal monastic meal you take only what you will eat and finish everything. At the end you rinse the bowl with water and drink it, so not a grain of rice is wasted.
