How to say "What's your MBTI type?" in Korean (엠비티아이가 뭐예요?)

You can say this

엠비티아이가 뭐예요?

embitiaiga mwoyeyo

Meaning: What's your MBTI type?

Good to know

Korea has been obsessed with this personality test since 2020. People ask it on first dates and job interviews, and some job ads even listed preferred types, which drew criticism.
