How to say "No way" in Korean (말도 안 돼요)

You can say this

말도 안 돼요.

maldo an dwaeyo

Meaning: No way.

Good to know

Literally: it does not even make words. Koreans use it for anything unbelievable, from a shocking price to great news, and in dramas it is the standard reaction to a plot twist.
