How to say "My mom is a really good cook" in Korean (우리 엄마는 요리를 정말 잘하세요)

You can say this

우리 엄마는 요리를 정말 잘하세요.

uri eommaneun yorireul jeongmal jalhaseyo

Meaning: My mom is a really good cook.

Good to know

Literally: our mom. Koreans say our for their mother, their home, their school and their country, even when speaking alone. Saying my mom instead sounds a little possessive to Korean ears.
