How to say "You've got this" in Korean (파이팅)

You can say this

파이팅!

paiting

Meaning: You've got this!

Good to know

Borrowed from the English word fighting, this is Korea's all-purpose cheer. It is shouted at sports games, written on gifts before exams, and said to coworkers at the start of a hard day.
