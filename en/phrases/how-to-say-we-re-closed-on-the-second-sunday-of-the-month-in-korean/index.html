How to say "We're closed on the second Sunday of the month" in Korean (둘째 주 일요일은 쉬어요)

You can say this

둘째 주 일요일은 쉬어요.

duljjae ju iryoireun swieoyo

Meaning: We're closed on the second Sunday of the month.

Good to know

By law since 2012, large marts must close two days a month, usually the second and fourth Sunday, to protect traditional markets. A few cities have moved the closing day to a weekday.
