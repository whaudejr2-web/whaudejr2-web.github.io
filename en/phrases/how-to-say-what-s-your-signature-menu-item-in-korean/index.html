How to say "What's your signature menu item?" in Korean (여기 시그니처 메뉴가 뭐예요?)

You can say this

여기 시그니처 메뉴가 뭐예요?

yeogi sigeunicheo menyuga mwoyeyo

Meaning: What's your signature menu item?
