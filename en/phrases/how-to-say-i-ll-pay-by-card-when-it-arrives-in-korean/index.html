How to say "I'll pay by card when it arrives" in Korean (만나서 카드로 할게요)

You can say this

만나서 카드로 할게요.

mannaseo kadeuro halgeyo

Meaning: I'll pay by card when it arrives.

Good to know

Riders carry a portable card terminal, so paying at the door by card is routine; the apps call this option pay on meeting. There is no tipping, and the rider will not expect anything extra.
