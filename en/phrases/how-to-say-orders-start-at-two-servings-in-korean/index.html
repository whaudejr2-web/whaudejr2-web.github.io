How to say "Orders start at two servings" in Korean (이 인분부터 주문 가능해요)

You can say this

이 인분부터 주문 가능해요.

i inbunbuteo jumun ganeunghaeyo

Meaning: Orders start at two servings.

Good to know

Most barbecue restaurants set a two-serving minimum for grilled meat, so a solo diner may be turned away or asked to order two. Some newer places have single-person grills.
