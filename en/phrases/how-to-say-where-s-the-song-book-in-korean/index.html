How to say "Where's the song book?" in Korean (노래책 어디 있어요?)

You can say this

노래책 어디 있어요?

noraechaek eodi isseoyo

Meaning: Where's the song book?

Good to know

Nearly every room runs a machine from TJ Media or Kumyoung, each with its own song numbers. The thick book and the remote list songs by title, singer and country.
