How to say "We start after the elders begin eating" in Korean (어른이 먼저 드시면 시작해요)

You can say this

어른이 먼저 드시면 시작해요.

eoreuni meonjeo deusimyeon sijakaeyo

Meaning: We start after the elders begin eating.
