How to say "My hands are sticky from sweat" in Korean (땀 때문에 손이 끈적끈적해요)

You can say this

땀 때문에 손이 끈적끈적해요.

ttam ttaemune soni kkeunjeokkkeunjeokaeyo

Meaning: My hands are sticky from sweat.
