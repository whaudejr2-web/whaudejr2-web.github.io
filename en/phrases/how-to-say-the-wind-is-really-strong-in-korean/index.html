How to say "The wind is really strong" in Korean (바람이 진짜 세요)

You can say this

바람이 진짜 세요.

barami jinjja seyo

Meaning: The wind is really strong.

Good to know

Jeju has long been called the island of three abundances: wind, stones, and women. The black volcanic stone walls around every field are there to break the wind.
