How to say "Is there a place where I can eat temple food?" in Korean (사찰 음식 먹을 수 있는 데 있어요?)

You can say this

사찰 음식 먹을 수 있는 데 있어요?

sachal eumsik meogeul su inneun de isseoyo

Meaning: Is there a place where I can eat temple food?

Good to know

Korean Buddhist temple food is fully vegetarian and also leaves out garlic, green onions, chives, leeks and wild chives. You can try it at a temple stay program or at temple food restaurants in Seoul.
