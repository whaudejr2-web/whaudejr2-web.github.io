How to say "Let's go do a photo booth" in Korean (인생네컷 찍으러 가요)

You can say this

인생네컷 찍으러 가요.

insaengnekeot jjigeureo gayo

Meaning: Let's go do a photo booth.

Good to know

The name means life four cuts and belongs to the chain that started the boom in 2017, but Koreans now use it for any photo booth. Rivals include Photoism and Haru Film, and most booths are unstaffed and open 24 hours.
