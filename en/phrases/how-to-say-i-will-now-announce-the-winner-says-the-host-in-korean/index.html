How to say "I will now announce the winner, says the host" in Korean (수상자를 발표하겠습니다)

You can say this

수상자를 발표하겠습니다.

susangjareul balpyohagetseumnida

Meaning: I will now announce the winner, says the host.

Good to know

Hosts and winners on stage use the formal polite ending heard here, the one Koreans use for speeches, the news and announcements.
