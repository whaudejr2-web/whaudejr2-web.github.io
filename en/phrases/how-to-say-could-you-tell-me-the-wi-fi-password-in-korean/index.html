How to say "Could you tell me the Wi-Fi password?" in Korean (와이파이 비밀번호 좀 알려 주세요)

You can say this

와이파이 비밀번호 좀 알려 주세요.

waipai bimilbeonho jom allyeo juseyo

Meaning: Could you tell me the Wi-Fi password?
