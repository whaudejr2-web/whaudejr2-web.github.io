What does "돌잡이 시작할게요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

돌잡이 시작할게요.

doljabi sijakalgeyo

Meaning: Let's start the object-picking.

Good to know

The baby is set in front of a row of objects, and the first one grabbed predicts the future: thread for long life, money for wealth, a pencil for study. Modern tables add a microphone, a stethoscope or a gavel.
