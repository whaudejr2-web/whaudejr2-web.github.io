What does "다 나을 때까지 렌즈는 끼지 마세요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

다 나을 때까지 렌즈는 끼지 마세요.

da naeul ttaekkaji renjeuneun kkiji maseyo

Meaning: Don't wear contacts until it's fully healed.
