How to say "Do convenience stores sell medicine too?" in Korean (편의점에서도 약 팔아요?)

You can say this

편의점에서도 약 팔아요?

pyeonuijeomeseodo yak parayo

Meaning: Do convenience stores sell medicine too?

Good to know

Since 2012, convenience stores can sell a short list of basic medicines around the clock: fever reducers, cold tablets, digestive aids and pain relief patches.
