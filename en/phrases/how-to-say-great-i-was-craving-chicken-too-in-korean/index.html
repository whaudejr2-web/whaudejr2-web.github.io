How to say "Great, I was craving chicken too" in Korean (좋아요, 저도 치킨 먹고 싶었어요)

You can say this

좋아요, 저도 치킨 먹고 싶었어요.

joayo, jeodo chikin meokgo sipeosseoyo

Meaning: Great, I was craving chicken too.
