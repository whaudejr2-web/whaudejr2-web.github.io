How to say "Where is the dryer?" in Korean (건조기는 어디예요?)

You can say this

건조기는 어디예요?

geonjogineun eodiyeyo

Meaning: Where is the dryer?

Good to know

Dryers were rare in Korean homes until the late 2010s, and most people still hang laundry on a rack or balcony. Many customers come to a laundromat only to dry clothes, especially in the rainy summer.
