How to say "It's not a semi-basement, right?" in Korean (반지하는 아니죠?)

You can say this

반지하는 아니죠?

banjihaneun anijyo

Meaning: It's not a semi-basement, right?

Good to know

Semi-basement rooms, made famous by the film Parasite, are the cheapest option but get little light and can flood. After deadly floods in 2022, Seoul announced it would phase them out as homes.
