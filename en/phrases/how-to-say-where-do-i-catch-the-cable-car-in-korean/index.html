How to say "Where do I catch the cable car?" in Korean (케이블카 타는 곳이 어디예요?)

You can say this

케이블카 타는 곳이 어디예요?

keibeulka taneun gosi eodiyeyo

Meaning: Where do I catch the cable car?

Good to know

The cable car to N Seoul Tower takes about three minutes each way. A free outdoor elevator by the lower station saves you the steep walk from the subway, and a round trip ticket is cheaper than two one way tickets.
