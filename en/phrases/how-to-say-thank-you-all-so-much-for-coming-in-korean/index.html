How to say "Thank you all so much for coming" in Korean (다들 와 줘서 정말 고마워요)

You can say this

다들 와 줘서 정말 고마워요.

dadeul wa jwoseo jeongmal gomawoyo

Meaning: Thank you all so much for coming.
