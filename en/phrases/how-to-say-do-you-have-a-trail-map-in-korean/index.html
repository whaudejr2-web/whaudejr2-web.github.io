How to say "Do you have a trail map?" in Korean (등산로 지도 있어요?)

You can say this

등산로 지도 있어요?

deungsanno jido isseoyo

Meaning: Do you have a trail map?

Good to know

Trails in Korean national parks have numbered location signs every few hundred meters. If you need help, read the code on the nearest sign when you call 119 so rescuers can find you.
