How to say "Can I get a bag, please?" in Korean (봉투 하나 주세요)

You can say this

봉투 하나 주세요.

bongtu hana juseyo

Meaning: Can I get a bag, please?

Good to know

Since 2019, large supermarkets in Korea are not allowed to hand out free single-use plastic bags, so shoppers bring their own. Small market stalls still usually give you a thin bag for your food.
