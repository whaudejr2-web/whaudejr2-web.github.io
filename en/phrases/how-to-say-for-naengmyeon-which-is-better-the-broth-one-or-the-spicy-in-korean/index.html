How to say "For naengmyeon, which is better, the broth one or the spicy one?" in Korean (냉면은 물이랑 비빔 중에 뭐가 더 맛있어요?)

You can say this

냉면은 물이랑 비빔 중에 뭐가 더 맛있어요?

naengmyeoneun murirang bibim junge mwoga deo masisseoyo

Meaning: For naengmyeon, which is better, the broth one or the spicy one?
