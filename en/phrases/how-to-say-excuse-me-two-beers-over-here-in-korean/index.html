How to say "Excuse me, two beers over here" in Korean (저기요, 맥주 두 잔이요)

You can say this

저기요, 맥주 두 잔이요!

jeogiyo, maekju du janiyo

Meaning: Excuse me, two beers over here!

Good to know

Beer sellers walk the stands with a keg strapped to their back and pour a fresh cup at your seat. They are a famous sight at Jamsil Stadium in Seoul.
