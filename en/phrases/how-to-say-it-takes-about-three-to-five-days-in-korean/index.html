What does "삼 일에서 오 일 정도 걸립니다." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

삼 일에서 오 일 정도 걸립니다.

sam ireseo o il jeongdo geollimnida

Meaning: It takes about three to five days.

Good to know

Card refunds in Korea usually show up on your statement after three to seven business days, depending on the card company, so an instant refund is not something to expect.
