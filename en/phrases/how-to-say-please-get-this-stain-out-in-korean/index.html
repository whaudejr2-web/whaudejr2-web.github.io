How to say "Please get this stain out" in Korean (여기 얼룩 좀 빼 주세요)

You can say this

여기 얼룩 좀 빼 주세요.

yeogi eolluk jom ppae juseyo

Meaning: Please get this stain out.

Listen and repeat
