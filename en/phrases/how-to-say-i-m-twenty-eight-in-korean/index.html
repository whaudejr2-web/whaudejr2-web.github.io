How to say "I'm twenty-eight" in Korean (스물여덟 살이에요)

You can say this

스물여덟 살이에요.

seumullyeodeol sarieyo

Meaning: I'm twenty-eight.

Good to know

Korea switched to international age for official purposes in June 2023. Before that, people counted one year at birth and added a year every January 1st, so many still give an age one or two years higher.
