How to say "Does kimchi have to go in checked luggage?" in Korean (김치는 부치는 짐에 넣어야 돼요?)

You can say this

김치는 부치는 짐에 넣어야 돼요?

gimchineun buchineun jime neoeoya dwaeyo

Meaning: Does kimchi have to go in checked luggage?

Good to know

Kimchi counts as a liquid at security, so it has to travel in checked luggage. Vacuum-packed jars from a supermarket or duty free survive the trip best; double bag them or your suitcase will smell.
