How to say "Have you tried sundae?" in Korean (순대 먹어 봤어요?)

You can say this

순대 먹어 봤어요?

sundae meogeo bwasseoyo

Meaning: Have you tried sundae?

Good to know

Despite the name, this is not ice cream. It is a steamed sausage filled with glass noodles and pork blood, dipped in salt in Seoul and in a soybean chili paste around Busan.
