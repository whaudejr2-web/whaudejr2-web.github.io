How to say "You sing really well" in Korean (노래 진짜 잘하시네요)

You can say this

노래 진짜 잘하시네요.

norae jinjja jalhasineyo

Meaning: You sing really well.

Good to know

Singing rooms, where friends rent a private room with a karaoke machine, are everywhere in Korea, so you will get plenty of chances to use this one.
