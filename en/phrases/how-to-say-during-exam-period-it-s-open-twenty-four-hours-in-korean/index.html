How to say "During exam period it's open twenty-four hours" in Korean (시험 기간에는 이십사 시간 열어요)

You can say this

시험 기간에는 이십사 시간 열어요.

siheom giganeneun isipsa sigan yeoreoyo

Meaning: During exam period it's open twenty-four hours.
