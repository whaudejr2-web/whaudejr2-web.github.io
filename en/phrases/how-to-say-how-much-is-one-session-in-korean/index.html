How to say "How much is one session?" in Korean (한 번에 얼마예요?)

You can say this

한 번에 얼마예요?

han beone eolmayeyo

Meaning: How much is one session?

Good to know

A session usually costs around 4,000 won and includes two printed strips. You pay on the touch screen inside the booth with a card or bills before the shoot begins.
