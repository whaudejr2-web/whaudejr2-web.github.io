What does "좌석은 매진이고 입석만 남았어요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

좌석은 매진이고 입석만 남았어요.

jwaseogeun maejinigo ipseongman namasseoyo

Meaning: The seats are sold out; only standing tickets are left.

Good to know

A standing ticket is cheaper and gets you on the same train. You may sit in any empty seat, but you have to give it up when the ticket holder shows up.
