How to say "It's even prettier when the lights come on at night" in Korean (밤에 불 켜지니까 더 예뻐요)

You can say this

밤에 불 켜지니까 더 예뻐요.

bame bul kyeojinikka deo yeppeoyo

Meaning: It's even prettier when the lights come on at night.

Good to know

Lotte World's outdoor Magic Island sits in the middle of Seokchon Lake, so at night you get the lit castle, the lake and Lotte World Tower in a single photo.
