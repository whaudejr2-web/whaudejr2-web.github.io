How to say "Come to think of it, it's already been a month" in Korean (그러고 보니, 벌써 한 달 됐네요)

You can say this

그러고 보니, 벌써 한 달 됐네요.

geureogo boni beolsseo han dal dwaenneyo

Meaning: Come to think of it, it's already been a month.
