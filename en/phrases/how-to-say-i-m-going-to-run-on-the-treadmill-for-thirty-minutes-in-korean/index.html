How to say "I'm going to run on the treadmill for thirty minutes" in Korean (러닝머신에서 삼십 분 뛸 거예요)

You can say this

러닝머신에서 삼십 분 뛸 거예요.

reoningmeosineseo samsip bun ttwil geoyeyo

Meaning: I'm going to run on the treadmill for thirty minutes.

Good to know

Koreans call the treadmill a running machine. Many gym words are Konglish like this, including the word for the gym itself.
