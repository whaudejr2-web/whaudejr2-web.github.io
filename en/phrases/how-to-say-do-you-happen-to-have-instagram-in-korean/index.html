How to say "Do you happen to have Instagram?" in Korean (혹시 인스타그램 하세요?)

You can say this

혹시 인스타그램 하세요?

hoksi inseutageuraem haseyo

Meaning: Do you happen to have Instagram?

Good to know

Instagram is popular with young Koreans, but the main messaging app is KakaoTalk, installed on nearly every phone in the country. People often add each other there instead of exchanging phone numbers.
