What does "여기서는 목줄 꼭 하셔야 돼요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

여기서는 목줄 꼭 하셔야 돼요.

yeogiseoneun mokjul kkok hasyeoya dwaeyo

Meaning: You have to keep it on a leash here.

Good to know

In public places Korean law requires a leash no longer than two meters. Walking a dog off leash can bring a fine of up to five hundred thousand won.
