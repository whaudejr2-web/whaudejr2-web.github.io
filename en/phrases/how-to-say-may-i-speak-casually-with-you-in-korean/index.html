How to say "May I speak casually with you?" in Korean (말 놓아도 돼요?)

You can say this

말 놓아도 돼요?

mal noado dwaeyo

Meaning: May I speak casually with you?

Good to know

Literally: may I put down my words. Dropping polite endings is normally proposed and agreed to, not assumed, even between people of the same age.
