How to say "I'll eat solo today" in Korean (오늘은 혼밥 할래요)

You can say this

오늘은 혼밥 할래요.

oneureun honbap hallaeyo

Meaning: I'll eat solo today.

Good to know

Short for eating alone. It went mainstream in the mid-2010s as single-person households grew; by 2023 they were 35.5 percent of all households in Korea, and restaurants now set up counters for solo diners.
