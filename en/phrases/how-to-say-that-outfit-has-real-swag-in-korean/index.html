How to say "That outfit has real swag" in Korean (그 옷 진짜 간지 나요)

You can say this

그 옷 진짜 간지 나요.

geu ot jinjja ganji nayo

Meaning: That outfit has real swag.

Good to know

Borrowed from the Japanese word for feeling or vibe, which is why language purists dislike it. Young Koreans use it anyway for anything stylish, from a jacket to a car.
