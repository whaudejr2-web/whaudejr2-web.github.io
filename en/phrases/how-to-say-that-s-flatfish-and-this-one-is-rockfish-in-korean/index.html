What does "광어예요. 이건 우럭이고요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

광어예요. 이건 우럭이고요.

gwangeoyeyo igeon ureogigoyo

Meaning: That's flatfish. And this one is rockfish.

Good to know

Flatfish and rockfish are the two standard choices at any Korean raw fish restaurant. Both are farmed in huge numbers, which keeps them the most affordable options on the menu.
