How to say "The neighbor upstairs is really noisy" in Korean (윗집이 너무 시끄러워요)

You can say this

윗집이 너무 시끄러워요.

witjibi neomu sikkeureowoyo

Meaning: The neighbor upstairs is really noisy.

Good to know

Noise between floors is the top source of neighbor disputes in Korea, where most people live in concrete high-rises. Complexes ask residents to report it to the management office rather than knock upstairs.
