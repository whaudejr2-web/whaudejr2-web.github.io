What does "몸조리 잘하세요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

몸조리 잘하세요.

momjori jalhaseyo

Meaning: Take care of yourself.

Good to know

Literally: take good care of your body. Koreans say it to anyone who is sick or recovering, and staff at clinics and pharmacies use it as a goodbye.
