How to say "Three people. How much for an hour?" in Korean (세 명이요. 한 시간 얼마예요?)

You can say this

세 명이요. 한 시간 얼마예요?

se myeongiyo han sigan eolmayeyo

Meaning: Three people. How much for an hour?
