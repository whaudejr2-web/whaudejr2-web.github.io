How to say "They sell the same thing for half the price elsewhere" in Korean (같은 거를 다른 데서는 반값에 팔아요)

You can say this

같은 거를 다른 데서는 반값에 팔아요.

gateun georeul dareun deseoneun bangapse parayo

Meaning: They sell the same thing for half the price elsewhere.
