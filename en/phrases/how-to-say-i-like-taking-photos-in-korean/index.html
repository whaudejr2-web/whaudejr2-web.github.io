How to say "I like taking photos" in Korean (사진 찍는 걸 좋아해요)

You can say this

사진 찍는 걸 좋아해요.

sajin jjingneun geol joahaeyo

Meaning: I like taking photos.

Good to know

Koreans call a perfect, once-in-a-lifetime photo a life shot, and many cafés and landmarks mark a designated spot where the best one is taken.
