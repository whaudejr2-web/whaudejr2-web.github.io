How to say "Does this bus go to Gamcheon Culture Village?" in Korean (이 버스 감천문화마을 가요?)

You can say this

이 버스 감천문화마을 가요?

i beoseu gamcheonmunhwamaeul gayo

Meaning: Does this bus go to Gamcheon Culture Village?
