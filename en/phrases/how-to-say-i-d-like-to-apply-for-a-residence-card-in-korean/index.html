How to say "I'd like to apply for a residence card" in Korean (외국인 등록증 신청하려고요)

You can say this

외국인 등록증 신청하려고요.

oegugin deungnokjeung sincheongharyeogoyo

Meaning: I'd like to apply for a residence card.
