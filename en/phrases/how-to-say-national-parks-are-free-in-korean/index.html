What does "국립공원은 무료예요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

국립공원은 무료예요.

gungnipgongwoneun muryoyeyo

Meaning: National parks are free.

Good to know

Until 2023, big temples inside national parks charged a fee at the park gate even if you never visited the temple. That fee was abolished in May 2023, so most trails are now free.
