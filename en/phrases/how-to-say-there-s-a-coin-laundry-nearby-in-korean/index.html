How to say "There's a coin laundry nearby" in Korean (근처에 코인 빨래방이 있어요)

You can say this

근처에 코인 빨래방이 있어요.

geuncheoe koin ppallaebangi isseoyo

Meaning: There's a coin laundry nearby.
