How to say "Where do I tap my card?" in Korean (카드 어디에 대요?)

You can say this

카드 어디에 대요?

kadeu eodie daeyo

Meaning: Where do I tap my card?

Good to know

You tap your card on the gate both entering and leaving, because the fare depends on how far you travel. Tap once and wait for the beep before walking through.
