How to say "When is the campus festival?" in Korean (학교 축제 언제예요?)

You can say this

학교 축제 언제예요?

hakgyo chukje eonjeyeyo

Meaning: When is the campus festival?

Good to know

Most universities hold a festival in May with food stalls run by each department and a concert on the last night. Big schools book major K-pop acts, and the lineups make national news.
