How to say "Hello, Venerable. How you greet a monk" in Korean (스님, 안녕하세요)

You can say this

스님, 안녕하세요.

seunim annyeonghaseyo

Meaning: Hello, Venerable. How you greet a monk.

Good to know

Monks are addressed with this title, never by their name alone. Put your palms together at chest height and bow slightly when you greet one.
