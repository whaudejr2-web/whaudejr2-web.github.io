How to say "Eat this and the hangover is gone" in Korean (이거 먹으면 숙취가 싹 풀려요)

You can say this

이거 먹으면 숙취가 싹 풀려요.

igeo meogeumyeon sukchwiga ssak pullyeoyo

Meaning: Eat this and the hangover is gone.
