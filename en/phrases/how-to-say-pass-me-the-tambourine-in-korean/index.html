How to say "Pass me the tambourine" in Korean (탬버린 좀 주세요)

You can say this

탬버린 좀 주세요.

taembeorin jom juseyo

Meaning: Pass me the tambourine.

Good to know

Every room comes with at least one tambourine. Shaking it for whoever is singing is the standard way to cheer, even if you never touch the mic yourself.
