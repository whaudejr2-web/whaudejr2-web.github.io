How to say "What's the Wi-Fi password?" in Korean (와이파이 비밀번호가 뭐예요?)

You can say this

와이파이 비밀번호가 뭐예요?

waipai bimilbeonhoga mwoyeyo

Meaning: What's the Wi-Fi password?
