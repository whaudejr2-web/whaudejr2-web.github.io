How to say "The noodles are chewy and delicious" in Korean (면이 쫄깃해서 맛있어요)

You can say this

면이 쫄깃해서 맛있어요.

myeoni jjolgitaeseo masisseoyo

Meaning: The noodles are chewy and delicious.
