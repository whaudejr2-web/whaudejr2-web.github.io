How to say "What do I do?" in Korean (어떡해요?)

You can say this

어떡해요?

eotteokaeyo

Meaning: What do I do?

Good to know

Short for: how should I handle this. Drama characters cry out the casual form whenever something goes wrong, from a spilled coffee to a missed train, and it also works as oh no.
