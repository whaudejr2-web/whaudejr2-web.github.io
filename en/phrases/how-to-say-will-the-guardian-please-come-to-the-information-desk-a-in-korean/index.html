How to say "Will the guardian please come to the information desk. A park announcement" in Korean (보호자 분은 안내소로 와 주세요)

You can say this

보호자 분은 안내소로 와 주세요.

bohoja buneun annaesoro wa juseyo

Meaning: Will the guardian please come to the information desk. A park announcement.
