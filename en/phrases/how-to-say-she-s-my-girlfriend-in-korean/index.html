How to say "She's my girlfriend" in Korean (이 사람 제 여자친구예요)

You can say this

이 사람 제 여자친구예요.

i saram je yeojachinguyeyo

Meaning: She's my girlfriend.

Good to know

Korean says this person instead of she. 이 사람 is the person right next to you.
