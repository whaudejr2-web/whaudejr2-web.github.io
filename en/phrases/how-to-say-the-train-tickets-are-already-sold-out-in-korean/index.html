How to say "The train tickets are already sold out" in Korean (기차표가 벌써 매진됐어요)

You can say this

기차표가 벌써 매진됐어요.

gichapyoga beolsseo maejindwaesseoyo

Meaning: The train tickets are already sold out.

Good to know

Holiday train tickets go on sale about a month ahead in a special booking window, and the popular KTX departures sell out within minutes of it opening.
