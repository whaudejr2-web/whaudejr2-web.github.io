How to say "Some broth too, please" in Korean (국물도 좀 주세요)

You can say this

국물도 좀 주세요.

gungmuldo jom juseyo

Meaning: Some broth too, please.

Good to know

Fish cake stalls keep a pot of hot broth, and a paper cup of it is free with your skewer. You usually ladle it yourself from the pot on the counter.
