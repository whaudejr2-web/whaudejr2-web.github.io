How to say "How much is the insurance?" in Korean (보험료는 얼마예요?)

You can say this

보험료는 얼마예요?

boheomnyoneun eolmayeyo

Meaning: How much is the insurance?

Good to know

EMS automatically covers loss or damage up to a set amount, and for anything valuable you can add insurance at the counter for a small fee based on the declared value. Keep the receipt, since any claim needs it.
