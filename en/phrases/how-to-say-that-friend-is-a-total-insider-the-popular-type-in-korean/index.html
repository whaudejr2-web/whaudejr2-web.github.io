How to say "That friend is a total insider, the popular type" in Korean (그 친구 완전 인싸예요)

You can say this

그 친구 완전 인싸예요.

geu chingu wanjeon inssayeyo

Meaning: That friend is a total insider, the popular type.

Good to know

From the English word insider, as in the people at the center of every group; the opposite is the outsider in the next line. Add the word nuclear in front for the most popular person in the room.
