How to say "The whole crowd singing gave me chills" in Korean (떼창 진짜 소름 돋았어요)

You can say this

떼창 진짜 소름 돋았어요.

ttechang jinjja soreum dodasseoyo

Meaning: The whole crowd singing gave me chills.
