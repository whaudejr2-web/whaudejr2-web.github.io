How to say "Let go of that hand" in Korean (그 손 놔요)

You can say this

그 손 놔요.

geu son nwayo

Meaning: Let go of that hand.

Good to know

The famous wrist grab scene: one lead grabs the heroine's wrist, the other one steps in with this line.
