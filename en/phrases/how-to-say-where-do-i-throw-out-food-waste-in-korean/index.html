How to say "Where do I throw out food waste?" in Korean (음식물 쓰레기는 어디에 버려요?)

You can say this

음식물 쓰레기는 어디에 버려요?

eumsingmul sseuregineun eodie beoryeoyo

Meaning: Where do I throw out food waste?

Good to know

Food waste is collected separately everywhere in Korea. Apartments usually have a bin that weighs it with a resident card and charges by the kilogram, or you buy special food waste bags.
