How to say "Usually fifty or a hundred thousand won" in Korean (보통 오만 원이나 십만 원 해요)

You can say this

보통 오만 원이나 십만 원 해요.

botong oman wonina simman won haeyo

Meaning: Usually fifty or a hundred thousand won.

Good to know

Fifty thousand won is typical for a coworker or acquaintance and a hundred thousand for a close friend. Odd amounts are preferred, and forty thousand is avoided because four sounds like death in Korean.
