What does "스마트키예요. 버튼 누르고 시동 거세요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

스마트키예요. 버튼 누르고 시동 거세요.

seumateukiyeyo beoteun nureugo sidong geoseyo

Meaning: It's a smart key. Press the button to start the engine.
