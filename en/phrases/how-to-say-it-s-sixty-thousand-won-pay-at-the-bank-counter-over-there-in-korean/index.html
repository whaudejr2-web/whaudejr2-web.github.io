What does "육만 원이에요. 저쪽 은행 창구에서 내세요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

육만 원이에요. 저쪽 은행 창구에서 내세요.

yungman wonieyo jeojjok eunhaeng changgueseo naeseyo

Meaning: It's sixty thousand won. Pay at the bank counter over there.
