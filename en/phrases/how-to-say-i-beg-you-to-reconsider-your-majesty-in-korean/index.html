How to say "I beg you to reconsider, Your Majesty" in Korean (통촉하여 주시옵소서)

You can say this

통촉하여 주시옵소서.

tongchokayeo jusiopsoseo

Meaning: I beg you to reconsider, Your Majesty.

Good to know

Literally: please see it clearly. Ministers shout it in unison when they disagree with the king, often while kneeling in the courtyard for days. In modern Korean you would simply ask someone to think it over once more.
