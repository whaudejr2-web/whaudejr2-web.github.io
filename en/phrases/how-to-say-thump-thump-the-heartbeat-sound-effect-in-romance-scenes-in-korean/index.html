How to say "Thump, thump. The heartbeat sound effect in romance scenes" in Korean (두근두근)

You can say this

두근두근.

dugeundugeun

Meaning: Thump, thump. The heartbeat sound effect in romance scenes.

Good to know

Korean comics draw sound effects into the art, and quiet ones count too. This heartbeat shows up in almost every romance scene, usually in big letters right next to the character's face.
