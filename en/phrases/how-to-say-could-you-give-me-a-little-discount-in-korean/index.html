How to say "Could you give me a little discount?" in Korean (좀 깎아 주세요)

You can say this

좀 깎아 주세요.

jom kkakka juseyo

Meaning: Could you give me a little discount?

Good to know

Friendly haggling is normal at stalls selling clothes, souvenirs or dried goods, but not at food stalls with posted prices. Vendors often toss in a little extra instead of cutting the price.
