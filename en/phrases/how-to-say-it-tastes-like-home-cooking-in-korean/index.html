How to say "It tastes like home cooking" in Korean (집밥 맛이에요)

You can say this

집밥 맛이에요.

jipbap masieyo

Meaning: It tastes like home cooking.

Good to know

Home-cooked food is literally called house rice, and a meal is simply called rice, which is why asking someone whether they have eaten rice is a normal way to say hello.
