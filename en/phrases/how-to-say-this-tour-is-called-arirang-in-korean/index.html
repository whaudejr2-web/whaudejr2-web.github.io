How to say "This tour is called Arirang" in Korean (이번 투어 이름이 아리랑이에요)

You can say this

이번 투어 이름이 아리랑이에요.

ibeon tueo ireumi arirangieyo

Meaning: This tour is called Arirang.

Good to know

Arirang is a Korean folk song added to the UNESCO list of Intangible Cultural Heritage in 2012, and BTS named their 2026 world tour after it.
