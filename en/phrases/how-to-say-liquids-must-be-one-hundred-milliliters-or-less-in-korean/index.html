What does "액체류는 백 밀리리터 이하만 돼요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

액체류는 백 밀리리터 이하만 돼요.

aekcheryuneun baek milliriteo ihaman dwaeyo

Meaning: Liquids must be one hundred milliliters or less.

Good to know

Each container must be one hundred milliliters or less, and they all go in one clear one-liter zip bag. Duty free liquids are fine as long as they stay in the sealed bag with the receipt inside.
