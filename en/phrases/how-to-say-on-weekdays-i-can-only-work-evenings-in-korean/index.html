How to say "On weekdays I can only work evenings" in Korean (평일에는 저녁에만 할 수 있어요)

You can say this

평일에는 저녁에만 할 수 있어요.

pyeongireneun jeonyeogeman hal su isseoyo

Meaning: On weekdays I can only work evenings.
