How to say "Is there a seat with a power outlet?" in Korean (콘센트 있는 자리 있어요?)

You can say this

콘센트 있는 자리 있어요?

konsenteu inneun jari isseoyo

Meaning: Is there a seat with a power outlet?

Good to know

Studying at cafés is so common in Korea that there is a word for it: kagong, which combines café and study.
