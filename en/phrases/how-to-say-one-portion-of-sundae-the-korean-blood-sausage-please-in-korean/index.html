How to say "One portion of sundae, the Korean blood sausage, please" in Korean (순대 일 인분 주세요)

You can say this

순대 일 인분 주세요.

sundae il inbun juseyo

Meaning: One portion of sundae, the Korean blood sausage, please.

Good to know

Blood sausage stuffed with glass noodles, steamed and sliced. What you dip it in depends on the region: salt in Seoul, soybean paste in Busan, chili vinegar sauce in Gwangju.
