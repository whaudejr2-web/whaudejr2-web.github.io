How to say "Can I come in?" in Korean (들어가도 돼요?)

You can say this

들어가도 돼요?

deureogado dwaeyo

Meaning: Can I come in?

Good to know

The permission pattern ends with the same word you will hear in the reply: fine, allowed. A yes plus that word means go ahead; a drawn-out hmm usually means no.
