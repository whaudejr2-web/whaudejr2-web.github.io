How to say "Summer is hot and winter is cold" in Korean (여름은 덥고 겨울은 추워요)

You can say this

여름은 덥고 겨울은 추워요.

yeoreumeun deopgo gyeoureun chuwoyo

Meaning: Summer is hot and winter is cold.
