How to say "A cup rice too, please" in Korean (컵밥도 하나 주세요)

You can say this

컵밥도 하나 주세요.

keopbapdo hana juseyo

Meaning: A cup rice too, please.

Good to know

Rice with toppings served in a paper cup. It was born at the street stalls of Noryangjin in Seoul, where students cramming for civil service exams eat standing up between classes.
