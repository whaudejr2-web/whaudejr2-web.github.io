How to say "The performance was legendary too" in Korean (무대도 레전드였어요)

You can say this

무대도 레전드였어요.

mudaedo rejeondeuyeosseoyo

Meaning: The performance was legendary too.

Good to know

Korean fans borrow the English word legend for a stage they will never forget, and keep calling a famous performance that for years.
