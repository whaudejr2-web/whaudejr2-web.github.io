What does "어서 오세요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

어서 오세요.

eoseo oseyo

Meaning: Welcome.

Good to know

Korea has more than fifty thousand convenience stores, and many of them are open twenty-four hours a day, so you will hear this greeting at any hour.
