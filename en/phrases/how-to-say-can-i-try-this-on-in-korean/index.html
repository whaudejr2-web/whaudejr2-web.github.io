How to say "Can I try this on?" in Korean (이거 입어 봐도 돼요?)

You can say this

이거 입어 봐도 돼요?

igeo ibeo bwado dwaeyo

Meaning: Can I try this on?

Good to know

Many clothing stores hand you a thin mesh face cover to pull over your head before trying on a top, so makeup does not get on the fabric. Some small market stalls do not allow trying on at all.
