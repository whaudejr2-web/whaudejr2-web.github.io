How to say "I've been learning Korean for six months" in Korean (한국어 배운 지 육 개월 됐어요)

You can say this

한국어 배운 지 육 개월 됐어요.

hangugeo baeun ji yuk gaewol dwaesseoyo

Meaning: I've been learning Korean for six months.
