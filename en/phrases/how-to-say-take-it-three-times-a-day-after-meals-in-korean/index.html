What does "하루 세 번 식후에 드세요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

하루 세 번 식후에 드세요.

haru se beon sikhue deuseyo

Meaning: Take it three times a day after meals.

Good to know

Pharmacies pack each dose in its own small packet, often printed with morning, noon and evening. Tear one open at each meal and you will never lose track.
