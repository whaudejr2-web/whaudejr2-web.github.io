How to say "I lost my passport" in Korean (여권을 잃어버렸어요)

You can say this

여권을 잃어버렸어요.

yeogwoneul ireobeoryeosseoyo

Meaning: I lost my passport.

Good to know

Report it to the police first and ask for the written confirmation; your embassy needs that paper before it can issue an emergency passport. A photo of your passport page on your phone speeds everything up.
