What does "방은 칠 층이에요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

방은 칠 층이에요.

bangeun chil cheungieyo

Meaning: Your room is on the seventh floor.

Good to know

Some older buildings in Korea label the fourth floor with the letter F instead of the number, because four sounds like the word for death in Korean. Newer hotels usually just use the number.
