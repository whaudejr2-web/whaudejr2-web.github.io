How to say "Nice to meet you too. Let's get along" in Korean (네, 반가워요. 앞으로 잘 지내요)

You can say this

네, 반가워요. 앞으로 잘 지내요.

ne, bangawoyo. apeuro jal jinaeyo

Meaning: Nice to meet you too. Let's get along.
