How to say "Sing with me. This one is a duet" in Korean (같이 불러요. 이거 듀엣곡이에요)

You can say this

같이 불러요. 이거 듀엣곡이에요.

gachi bulleoyo igeo dyuetgogieyo

Meaning: Sing with me. This one is a duet.
