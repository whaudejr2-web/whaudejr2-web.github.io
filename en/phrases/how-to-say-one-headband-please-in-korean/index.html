How to say "One headband, please" in Korean (머리띠 하나 주세요)

You can say this

머리띠 하나 주세요.

meoritti hana juseyo

Meaning: One headband, please.

Good to know

Character headbands are the standard theme park souvenir in Korea, and adults and couples wear matching ones all day. Everland's panda Fubao got so famous in 2023 that her goods had their own pop-up shop.
