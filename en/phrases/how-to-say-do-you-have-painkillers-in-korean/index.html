How to say "Do you have painkillers?" in Korean (진통제 있어요?)

You can say this

진통제 있어요?

jintongje isseoyo

Meaning: Do you have painkillers?

Good to know

Since 2012, convenience stores have been allowed to sell a short list of basic medicines, including pain relievers and cold medicine, so you do not need a pharmacy for a simple headache.
