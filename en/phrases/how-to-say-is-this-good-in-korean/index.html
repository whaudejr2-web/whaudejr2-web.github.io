How to say "Is this good?" in Korean (이거 맛있어요?)

You can say this

이거 맛있어요?

igeo masisseoyo

Meaning: Is this good?

Good to know

In Busan dialect, a yes-or-no question like this one ends in a different syllable from an open question like what is it, and the pitch drops at the end. Keep using standard polite Korean; everyone understands it.
