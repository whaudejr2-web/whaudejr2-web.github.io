How to say "Two jjajangmyeon and one small sweet and sour pork" in Korean (짜장면 둘이랑 탕수육 소자 하나요)

You can say this

짜장면 둘이랑 탕수육 소자 하나요.

jjajangmyeon durirang tangsuyuk soja hanayo

Meaning: Two jjajangmyeon and one small sweet and sour pork.

Good to know

Chinese-Korean restaurants were delivering long before the apps, and many still bring black bean noodles in reusable bowls. After eating, rinse the bowls and leave them outside your door for pickup.
