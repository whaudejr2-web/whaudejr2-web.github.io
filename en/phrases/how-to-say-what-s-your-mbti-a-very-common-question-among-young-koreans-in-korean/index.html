How to say "What's your MBTI? A very common question among young Koreans" in Korean (엠비티아이가 뭐예요?)

You can say this

엠비티아이가 뭐예요?

embitiaiga mwoyeyo

Meaning: What's your MBTI? A very common question among young Koreans.

Good to know

The MBTI personality test became a craze in Korea around 2020. Young people ask your four letters as naturally as your age, and the answer shows up in dating profiles and even some job applications.
