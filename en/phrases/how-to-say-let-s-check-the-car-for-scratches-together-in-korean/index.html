How to say "Let's check the car for scratches together" in Korean (차에 흠집 있는지 같이 확인해요)

You can say this

차에 흠집 있는지 같이 확인해요.

chae heumjip inneunji gachi hwaginhaeyo

Meaning: Let's check the car for scratches together.

Good to know

Walk around the car with the staff and photograph every scratch before you leave. Existing damage is marked on a diagram, and anything not marked there may be charged to you at return.
