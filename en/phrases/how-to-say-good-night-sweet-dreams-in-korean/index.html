How to say "Good night. Sweet dreams" in Korean (잘 자요. 좋은 꿈 꿔요)

You can say this

잘 자요. 좋은 꿈 꿔요.

jal jayo. joeun kkum kkwoyo

Meaning: Good night. Sweet dreams.
