How to say "What Korean food have you tried?" in Korean (한국 음식 중에 뭐 먹어 봤어요?)

You can say this

한국 음식 중에 뭐 먹어 봤어요?

hanguk eumsik junge mwo meogeo bwasseoyo

Meaning: What Korean food have you tried?
