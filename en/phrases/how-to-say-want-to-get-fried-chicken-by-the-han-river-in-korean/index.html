How to say "Want to get fried chicken by the Han River?" in Korean (한강에서 치킨 먹을래요?)

You can say this

한강에서 치킨 먹을래요?

hangangeseo chikin meogeullaeyo

Meaning: Want to get fried chicken by the Han River?

Good to know

A picnic at the Han River parks is a classic cheap date in Seoul. Fried chicken is delivered right to the riverbank, and the convenience stores there have instant noodle cookers.
