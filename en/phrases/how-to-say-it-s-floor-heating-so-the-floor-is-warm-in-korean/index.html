How to say "It's floor heating, so the floor is warm" in Korean (온돌이라 바닥이 따뜻해요)

You can say this

온돌이라 바닥이 따뜻해요.

ondorira badagi ttatteutaeyo

Meaning: It's floor heating, so the floor is warm.

Good to know

Korean floor heating goes back more than a thousand years, when smoke from the kitchen fire was channeled under the stone floor. Today hot water pipes do the job, and the spot nearest the boiler is the warmest.
