How to say "Just cut enough sashimi for the two of us" in Korean (둘이 먹을 만큼만 떠 주세요)

You can say this

둘이 먹을 만큼만 떠 주세요.

duri meogeul mankeumman tteo juseyo

Meaning: Just cut enough sashimi for the two of us.
