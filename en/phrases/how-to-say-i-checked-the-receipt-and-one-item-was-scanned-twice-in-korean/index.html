How to say "I checked the receipt and one item was scanned twice" in Korean (영수증 확인해 보니까 하나 두 번 찍혔어요)

You can say this

영수증 확인해 보니까 하나 두 번 찍혔어요.

yeongsujeung hwaginhae bonikka hana du beon jjikyeosseoyo

Meaning: I checked the receipt and one item was scanned twice.
