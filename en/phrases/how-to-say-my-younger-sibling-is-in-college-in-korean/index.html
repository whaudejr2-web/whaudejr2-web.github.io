How to say "My younger sibling is in college" in Korean (동생은 대학생이에요)

You can say this

동생은 대학생이에요.

dongsaengeun daehaksaengieyo

Meaning: My younger sibling is in college.

Good to know

The word for a younger sibling is the same for brothers and sisters; you add the syllable for male or female in front only when you want to specify. Age, not gender, is what Korean family words mark first.
