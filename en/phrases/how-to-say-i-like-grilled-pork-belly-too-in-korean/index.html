How to say "I like grilled pork belly too" in Korean (삼겹살도 좋아해요)

You can say this

삼겹살도 좋아해요.

samgyeopsaldo joahaeyo

Meaning: I like grilled pork belly too.

Good to know

Pork belly is grilled at the table and eaten wrapped in a lettuce leaf with garlic, sauce and kimchi. March 3rd is an unofficial pork belly day in Korea, because the dish's name begins with the word for three.
