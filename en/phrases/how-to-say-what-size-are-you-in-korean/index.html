What does "사이즈가 어떻게 되세요?" mean in Korean?

You will hear this

사이즈가 어떻게 되세요?

saijeuga eotteoke doeseyo

Meaning: What size are you?

Good to know

Korean shoe sizes are given in millimeters, so a 250 is about a US women's 8. Tops are often labeled 90, 95, 100 or 105, numbers based on chest size, instead of S, M and L.
