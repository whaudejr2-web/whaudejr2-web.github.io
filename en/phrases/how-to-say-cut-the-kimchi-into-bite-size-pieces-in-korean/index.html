How to say "Cut the kimchi into bite-size pieces" in Korean (김치는 한입 크기로 잘라요)

You can say this

김치는 한입 크기로 잘라요.

gimchineun hanip keugiro jallayo

Meaning: Cut the kimchi into bite-size pieces.

Good to know

In a Korean kitchen kimchi is cut with scissors, not a knife, often right in the pot. Kitchen scissors are also used for noodles, grilled meat and even pizza.
