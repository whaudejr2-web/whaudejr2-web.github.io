How to say "One tteokbokki with lots of fish cake, please" in Korean (떡볶이 일 인분에 어묵 많이 넣어 주세요)

You can say this

떡볶이 일 인분에 어묵 많이 넣어 주세요.

tteokbokki il inbune eomuk mani neoeo juseyo

Meaning: One tteokbokki with lots of fish cake, please.

Good to know

Chewy rice cakes simmered in a sweet chili sauce, with slices of fish cake cooked in. It is sold by the portion, and one portion is a filling meal for one person.
