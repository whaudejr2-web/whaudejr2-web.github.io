What does "동물 등록 하셨어요?" mean in Korean?

You will hear this

동물 등록 하셨어요?

dongmul deungnok hasyeosseoyo

Meaning: Have you registered your dog?

Good to know

Since 2014 every dog older than two months must be registered with a microchip or a tag, and the vet can do it on the spot. Cat registration is still voluntary.
