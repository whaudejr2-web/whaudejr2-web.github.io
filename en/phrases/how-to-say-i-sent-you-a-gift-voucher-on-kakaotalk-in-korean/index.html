How to say "I sent you a gift voucher on KakaoTalk" in Korean (카톡으로 기프티콘 보냈어요)

You can say this

카톡으로 기프티콘 보냈어요.

katogeuro gipeutikon bonaesseoyo

Meaning: I sent you a gift voucher on KakaoTalk.

Good to know

KakaoTalk shows your friends' birthdays at the top of the friend list, and sending a mobile gift voucher for coffee or cake through the app is the most common way to say happy birthday from afar.
