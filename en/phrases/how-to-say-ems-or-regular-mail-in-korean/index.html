What does "이엠에스로 하시겠어요, 일반으로 하시겠어요?" mean in Korean?

You will hear this

이엠에스로 하시겠어요, 일반으로 하시겠어요?

iemeseuro hasigesseoyo, ilbaneuro hasigesseoyo

Meaning: EMS or regular mail?

Good to know

EMS is the express international service run by post offices worldwide, with full tracking and delivery in about three to seven days. Regular airmail is cheaper but slower and gets only basic tracking.
