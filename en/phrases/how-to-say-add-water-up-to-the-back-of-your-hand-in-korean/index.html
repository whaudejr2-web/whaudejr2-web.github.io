How to say "Add water up to the back of your hand" in Korean (물은 손등까지 넣으면 돼요)

You can say this

물은 손등까지 넣으면 돼요.

mureun sondeungkkaji neoeumyeon dwaeyo

Meaning: Add water up to the back of your hand.

Good to know

This is the traditional measure: lay your palm flat on the rinsed rice and add water until it just covers the back of your hand. Rice cooker pots also have a line for each cup.
