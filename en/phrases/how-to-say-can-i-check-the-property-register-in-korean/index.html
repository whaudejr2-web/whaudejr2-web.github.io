How to say "Can I check the property register?" in Korean (등기부등본 확인할 수 있어요?)

You can say this

등기부등본 확인할 수 있어요?

deunggibudeungbon hwaginhal su isseoyo

Meaning: Can I check the property register?

Good to know

The property register lists the real owner and any mortgages on the building. After a wave of rental deposit fraud in 2022 and 2023, agents now expect tenants to ask for it before signing anything.
