How to say "I'm hardworking and always on time" in Korean (저는 성실하고 시간 약속을 잘 지켜요)

You can say this

저는 성실하고 시간 약속을 잘 지켜요.

jeoneun seongsilhago sigan yaksogeul jal jikyeoyo

Meaning: I'm hardworking and always on time.

Good to know

Nearly every Korean part-time job ad asks for a sincere, hardworking person, and punctuality is the first thing owners mention. Naming these two traits is the standard interview answer, not bragging.
