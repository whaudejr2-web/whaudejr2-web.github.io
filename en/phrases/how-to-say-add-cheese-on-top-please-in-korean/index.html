How to say "Add cheese on top, please" in Korean (치즈 추가해 주세요)

You can say this

치즈 추가해 주세요.

chijeu chugahae juseyo

Meaning: Add cheese on top, please.

Good to know

A slice of processed cheese melted over the rice cakes is the most popular add-on. It costs around one thousand won and softens the heat.
