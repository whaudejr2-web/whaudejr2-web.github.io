How to say "Is the museum free?" in Korean (박물관 입장료는 무료예요?)

You can say this

박물관 입장료는 무료예요?

bangmulgwan ipjangnyoneun muryoyeyo

Meaning: Is the museum free?

Good to know

The National Museum of Korea in Seoul does not charge for its permanent collection, and it stays open until nine at night on Wednesdays and Saturdays. Only special exhibitions need a ticket.
