How to say "One hundred, one thousand, ten thousand" in Korean (백, 천, 만)

You can say this

백, 천, 만.

baek, cheon, man

Meaning: One hundred, one thousand, ten thousand.

Good to know

Korean counts big numbers in units of ten thousand, which has its own word. You say it alone with no one in front, and one hundred thousand becomes ten ten-thousands.
