How to say "What TOPIK level do you have?" in Korean (토픽 몇 급 있으세요?)

You can say this

토픽 몇 급 있으세요?

topik myeot geup isseuseyo

Meaning: What TOPIK level do you have?

Good to know

TOPIK is the official Korean proficiency test, with six levels. Level three or four is what most universities ask for admission, and level six is the highest.
