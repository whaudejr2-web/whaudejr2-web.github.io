What does "무게가 삼 킬로 초과됐어요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

무게가 삼 킬로 초과됐어요.

mugega sam killo chogwadwaesseoyo

Meaning: It's three kilos over the limit.

Good to know

On most long-haul economy tickets from Korea, each checked bag may weigh up to twenty-three kilos. Fees for extra weight are charged right at the counter, so weigh your suitcase before you leave the hotel.
