How to say "I'd like to cancel my reservation" in Korean (예약 취소하고 싶어요)

You can say this

예약 취소하고 싶어요.

yeyak chwisohago sipeoyo

Meaning: I'd like to cancel my reservation.

Good to know

Many restaurants in Korea now ask for a deposit when you book because of no-shows. If your plans change, a quick cancellation call is expected.
