How to say "My number is 010, 1234, 5678" in Korean (제 번호는 공일공, 일이삼사, 오육칠팔이에요)

You can say this

제 번호는 공일공, 일이삼사, 오육칠팔이에요.

je beonhoneun gongilgong, ilisamsa, oyukchilparieyo

Meaning: My number is 010, 1234, 5678.

Good to know

Every Korean mobile number starts with 010 and has eleven digits. Koreans read numbers digit by digit, and zero is usually said with a special word rather than the counting number.
