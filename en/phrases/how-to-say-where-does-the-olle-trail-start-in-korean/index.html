How to say "Where does the Olle trail start?" in Korean (올레길은 어디서 시작해요?)

You can say this

올레길은 어디서 시작해요?

ollegireun eodiseo sijakaeyo

Meaning: Where does the Olle trail start?

Good to know

The Jeju Olle is a 437-kilometer walking route around the island, divided into 27 courses. Follow the blue and orange ribbons and the small horse-shaped signposts.
