How to say "Please make sure you eat well" in Korean (밥 잘 챙겨 드세요)

You can say this

밥 잘 챙겨 드세요.

bap jal chaenggyeo deuseyo

Meaning: Please make sure you eat well.

Good to know

Did you eat? works as a greeting in Korea, so telling an idol to eat well is less about food and more a way of saying I care about you. It is one of the most common wishes in fan letters.
