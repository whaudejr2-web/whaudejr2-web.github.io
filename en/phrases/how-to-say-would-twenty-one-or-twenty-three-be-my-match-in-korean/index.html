How to say "Would twenty-one or twenty-three be my match?" in Korean (이십일 호가 맞을까요, 이십삼 호가 맞을까요?)

You can say this

이십일 호가 맞을까요, 이십삼 호가 맞을까요?

isibil hoga majeulkkayo isipsam hoga majeulkkayo

Meaning: Would twenty-one or twenty-three be my match?

Good to know

Korean foundation shades are numbered. Twenty-one is light and twenty-three is medium, and most brands stop around twenty-five, so deeper skin tones may need to look at global brands.
