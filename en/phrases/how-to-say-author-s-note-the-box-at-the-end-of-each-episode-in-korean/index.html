How to say "Author's note. The box at the end of each episode" in Korean (작가의 말)

You can say this

작가의 말.

jakgaui mal

Meaning: Author's note. The box at the end of each episode.

Good to know

Most Korean webtoons end each episode with a short comment from the creator, and readers reply in the comments underneath. For many fans the comment section is half the fun of a new episode.
