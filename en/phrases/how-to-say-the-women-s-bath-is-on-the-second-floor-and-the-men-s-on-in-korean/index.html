What does "여탕은 이 층, 남탕은 삼 층이에요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

여탕은 이 층, 남탕은 삼 층이에요.

yeotangeun i cheung namtangeun sam cheungieyo

Meaning: The women's bath is on the second floor and the men's on the third.
