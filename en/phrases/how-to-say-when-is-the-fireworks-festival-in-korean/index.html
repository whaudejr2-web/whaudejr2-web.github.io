How to say "When is the fireworks festival?" in Korean (불꽃축제가 언제예요?)

You can say this

불꽃축제가 언제예요?

bulkkotchukjega eonjeyeyo

Meaning: When is the fireworks festival?

Good to know

The Busan Fireworks Festival has been held at Gwangalli Beach every autumn since 2005, usually in late October or November. The fireworks go up from the Gwangan Bridge, so the sand fills up hours ahead.
