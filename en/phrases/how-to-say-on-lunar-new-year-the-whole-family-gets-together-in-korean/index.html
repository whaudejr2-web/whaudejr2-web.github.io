How to say "On Lunar New Year the whole family gets together" in Korean (설날에는 가족이 다 모여요)

You can say this

설날에는 가족이 다 모여요.

seollareneun gajogi da moyeoyo

Meaning: On Lunar New Year the whole family gets together.

Good to know

Lunar New Year and Chuseok, the autumn harvest holiday, are three-day holidays when families gather at the parents' home. They are the busiest travel days of the year; train tickets sell out within minutes.
