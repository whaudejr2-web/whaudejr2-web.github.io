How to say "Where can I exchange money?" in Korean (환전은 어디서 해요?)

You can say this

환전은 어디서 해요?

hwanjeoneun eodiseo haeyo

Meaning: Where can I exchange money?

Good to know

Licensed exchange booths in Seoul's Myeongdong usually offer a better rate than the bank counters at the airport. Rates are posted on a board, so compare a couple before you exchange.
