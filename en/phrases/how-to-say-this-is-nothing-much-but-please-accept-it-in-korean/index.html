How to say "This is nothing much, but please accept it" in Korean (이거 별거 아닌데 받아 주세요)

You can say this

이거 별거 아닌데 받아 주세요.

igeo byeolgeo aninde bada juseyo

Meaning: This is nothing much, but please accept it.
