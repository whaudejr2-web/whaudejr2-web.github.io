How to say "Shall we go to round two?" in Korean (이차 갈까요?)

You can say this

이차 갈까요?

icha galkkayo

Meaning: Shall we go to round two?

Good to know

A Korean night out moves from place to place: dinner is round one, a bar is round two, and karaoke is often round three. Each stop has its own number.
