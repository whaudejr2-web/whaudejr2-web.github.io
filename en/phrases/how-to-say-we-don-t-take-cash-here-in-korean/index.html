What does "여기는 현금 안 받아요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

여기는 현금 안 받아요.

yeogineun hyeongeum an badayo

Meaning: We don't take cash here.

Good to know

Cash-free stores are legal in Korea. Starbucks Korea began running cashless stores in 2018, and many cafés and some Seoul city buses now take only cards or phone payments.
