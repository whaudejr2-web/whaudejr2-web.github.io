How to say "One jjajangmyeon and one jjamppong, please" in Korean (짜장면 하나랑 짬뽕 하나 주세요)

You can say this

짜장면 하나랑 짬뽕 하나 주세요.

jjajangmyeon hanarang jjamppong hana juseyo

Meaning: One jjajangmyeon and one jjamppong, please.
