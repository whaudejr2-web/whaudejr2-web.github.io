What does "빌릴 때 있던 만큼만 채워 오시면 돼요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

빌릴 때 있던 만큼만 채워 오시면 돼요.

billil ttae itdeon mankeumman chaewo osimyeon dwaeyo

Meaning: Just bring it back with as much gas as it had when you picked it up.
