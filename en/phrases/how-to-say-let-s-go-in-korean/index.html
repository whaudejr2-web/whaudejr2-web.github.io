How to say "Let's go" in Korean (고고)

You can say this

고고!

gogo

Meaning: Let's go!

Good to know

In chat this is typed as just the first consonant of each syllable, two letters in total. Korean players shorten most chat words this way, so what you see on screen rarely looks like a full word.
