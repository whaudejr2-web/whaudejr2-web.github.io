How to say "You've been waiting a while, haven't you?" in Korean (많이 기다리셨죠?)

You can say this

많이 기다리셨죠?

mani gidarisyeotjyo

Meaning: You've been waiting a while, haven't you?

Good to know

Restaurant staff say this as they set down your food after a wait. It is a courtesy, and a simple no, it's fine is the usual reply.
