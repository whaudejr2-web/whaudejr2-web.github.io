How to say "Wrong! The buzzer sound" in Korean (땡)

You can say this

땡!

ttaeng

Meaning: Wrong! The buzzer sound.

Good to know

On Korean quiz shows a right answer gets a three-note chime and a wrong one gets this flat bell. People now say the sound itself to mean wrong, even in daily conversation.
