How to say "The fine dust is bad today" in Korean (오늘 미세먼지가 심해요)

You can say this

오늘 미세먼지가 심해요.

oneul misemeonjiga simhaeyo

Meaning: The fine dust is bad today.

Good to know

Korea rates fine dust in four levels from good to very bad, and when it spikes an emergency alert buzzes on every phone in the area. Many Koreans check the dust level before going out.
