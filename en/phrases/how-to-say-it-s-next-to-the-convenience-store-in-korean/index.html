How to say "It's next to the convenience store" in Korean (편의점 옆에 있어요)

You can say this

편의점 옆에 있어요.

pyeonuijeom yeope isseoyo

Meaning: It's next to the convenience store.

Good to know

Korea has more than 55,000 convenience stores, one of the highest densities in the world, so people use them as landmarks all the time. The big chains are CU, GS25, 7-Eleven and Emart24.
