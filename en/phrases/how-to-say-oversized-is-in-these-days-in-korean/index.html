How to say "Oversized is in these days" in Korean (요즘은 오버핏이 유행이에요)

You can say this

요즘은 오버핏이 유행이에요.

yojeumeun obeopisi yuhaengieyo

Meaning: Oversized is in these days.

Good to know

Koreans say over-fit for oversized. Since the late 2010s it has been the default silhouette in Korean streetwear, so a size down is often safer than you'd think.
