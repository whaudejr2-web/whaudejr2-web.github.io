What does "소고기 드릴까요, 닭고기 드릴까요?" mean in Korean?

You will hear this

소고기 드릴까요, 닭고기 드릴까요?

sogogi deurilkkayo dakgogi deurilkkayo

Meaning: Beef or chicken?
