How to say "Let's grab chicken and beer on the way in" in Korean (치킨이랑 맥주 사 가요)

You can say this

치킨이랑 맥주 사 가요.

chikinirang maekju sa gayo

Meaning: Let's grab chicken and beer on the way in.

Good to know

Korean ballparks let you bring your own food and canned beer through the gate. Fried chicken is the classic, and delivery riders even hand orders to fans at the stadium entrance.
