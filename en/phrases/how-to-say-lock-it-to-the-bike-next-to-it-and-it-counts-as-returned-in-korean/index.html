What does "옆 자전거에 연결해서 반납하면 돼요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

옆 자전거에 연결해서 반납하면 돼요.

yeop jajeongeoe yeongyeolhaeseo bannapamyeon dwaeyo

Meaning: Lock it to the bike next to it and it counts as returned.
