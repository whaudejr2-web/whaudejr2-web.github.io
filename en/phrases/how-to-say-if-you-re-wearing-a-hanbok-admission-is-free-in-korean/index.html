What does "한복 입으시면 무료예요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

한복 입으시면 무료예요.

hanbok ibeusimyeon muryoyeyo

Meaning: If you're wearing a hanbok, admission is free.

Good to know

Since 2013 anyone wearing traditional Korean dress enters Seoul's royal palaces for free. Rental shops cluster around the palace gates and charge by the hour, so most visitors rent for two to four hours.
