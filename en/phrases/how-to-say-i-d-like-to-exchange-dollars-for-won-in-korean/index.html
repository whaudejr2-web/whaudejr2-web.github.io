How to say "I'd like to exchange dollars for won" in Korean (달러를 원화로 바꾸고 싶어요)

You can say this

달러를 원화로 바꾸고 싶어요.

dalleoreul wonhwaro bakkugo sipeoyo

Meaning: I'd like to exchange dollars for won.
