How to say "Workout done for today" in Korean (오늘 운동 완료)

You can say this

오늘 운동 완료!

oneul undong wallyo

Meaning: Workout done for today!

Good to know

Koreans post gym selfies with a three-syllable hashtag that shortens this sentence: today's workout complete. It is one of the most used fitness tags on Korean Instagram.
