How to say "Auntie, can we sit here?" in Korean (이모, 여기 앉아도 돼요?)

You can say this

이모, 여기 앉아도 돼요?

imo yeogi anjado dwaeyo

Meaning: Auntie, can we sit here?

Good to know

Customers call the woman running a tent or small bar Auntie, literally mother's sister. It is warm, not rude, and it usually gets you friendlier service.
