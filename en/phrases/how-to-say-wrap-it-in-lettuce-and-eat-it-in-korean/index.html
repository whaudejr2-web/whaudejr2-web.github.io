How to say "Wrap it in lettuce and eat it" in Korean (상추에 싸서 드세요)

You can say this

상추에 싸서 드세요.

sangchue ssaseo deuseyo

Meaning: Wrap it in lettuce and eat it.

Good to know

A wrap is eaten in one bite: a lettuce or perilla leaf, a piece of meat, a dab of sauce and maybe garlic or rice. Koreans fold it closed and eat the whole thing at once rather than biting it in half.
