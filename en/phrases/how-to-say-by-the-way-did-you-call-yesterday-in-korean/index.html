How to say "By the way, did you call yesterday?" in Korean (참, 어제 전화했어요?)

You can say this

참, 어제 전화했어요?

cham, eoje jeonhwahaesseoyo

Meaning: By the way, did you call yesterday?

Good to know

This little word literally means truly, but at the start of a sentence it means oh right, something just came to mind.
