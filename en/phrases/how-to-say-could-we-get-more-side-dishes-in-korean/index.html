How to say "Could we get more side dishes?" in Korean (반찬 좀 더 주세요)

You can say this

반찬 좀 더 주세요.

banchan jom deo juseyo

Meaning: Could we get more side dishes?

Good to know

Side dishes come free with every meal, and refills are free too. Just ask for the one you want more of; no one will charge you for it.
