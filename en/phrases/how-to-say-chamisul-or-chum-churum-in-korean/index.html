What does "참이슬로 드릴까요, 처음처럼으로 드릴까요?" mean in Korean?

You will hear this

참이슬로 드릴까요, 처음처럼으로 드릴까요?

chamiseullo deurilkkayo cheoeumcheoreomeuro deurilkkayo

Meaning: Chamisul or Chum-Churum?

Good to know

Chamisul by Jinro and Chum-Churum by Lotte are the two big soju brands. Jinro has been the best-selling spirit brand in the world by volume for more than twenty years.
