How to say "I got home safe. Thanks for today" in Korean (저 잘 들어왔어요. 오늘 고마웠어요)

You can say this

저 잘 들어왔어요. 오늘 고마웠어요.

jeo jal deureowasseoyo. oneul gomawosseoyo

Meaning: I got home safe. Thanks for today.
