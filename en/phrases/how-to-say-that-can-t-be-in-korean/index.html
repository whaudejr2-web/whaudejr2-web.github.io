How to say "That can't be" in Korean (말도 안 돼요)

You can say this

말도 안 돼요.

maldo an dwaeyo

Meaning: That can't be.

Good to know

Literally: it does not even make sense as words. It is the standard gasp at a plot twist, and on screen it is almost always the casual form without the polite ending.
