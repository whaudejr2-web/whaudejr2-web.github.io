How to say "One T-money card, please" in Korean (티머니 하나 주세요)

You can say this

티머니 하나 주세요.

timeoni hana juseyo

Meaning: One T-money card, please.

Good to know

Any convenience store sells T-money cards, and the card alone costs a few thousand won. One card works on subways, buses and most taxis, not just in Seoul but across Korea.
