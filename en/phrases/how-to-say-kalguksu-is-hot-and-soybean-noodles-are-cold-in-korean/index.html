How to say "Kalguksu is hot and soybean noodles are cold" in Korean (칼국수는 뜨겁고 콩국수는 차가워요)

You can say this

칼국수는 뜨겁고 콩국수는 차가워요.

kalguksuneun tteugeopgo kongguksuneun chagawoyo

Meaning: Kalguksu is hot and soybean noodles are cold.
