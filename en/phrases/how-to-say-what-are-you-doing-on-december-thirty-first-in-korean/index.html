How to say "What are you doing on December thirty-first?" in Korean (십이월 삼십일일에 뭐 해요?)

You can say this

십이월 삼십일일에 뭐 해요?

sibiwol samsibirire mwo haeyo

Meaning: What are you doing on December thirty-first?
