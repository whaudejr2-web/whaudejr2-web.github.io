How to say "Where is the nearest hospital?" in Korean (가까운 병원이 어디예요?)

You can say this

가까운 병원이 어디예요?

gakkaun byeongwoni eodiyeyo

Meaning: Where is the nearest hospital?

Good to know

University hospitals run emergency rooms around the clock, and the big ones in Seoul have international clinics with English-speaking staff. Bring your passport; you pay when treatment is finished.
