How to say "I'm full" in Korean (배불러요)

You can say this

배불러요.

baebulleoyo

Meaning: I'm full.

Good to know

Koreans say this to the person who cooked or paid, right before the set phrase that means I ate well. Telling your host you are full is taken as a compliment, not a complaint.
