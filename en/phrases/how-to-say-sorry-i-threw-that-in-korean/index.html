How to say "Sorry, I threw that" in Korean (미안해요, 제가 던졌어요)

You can say this

미안해요, 제가 던졌어요.

mianhaeyo jega deonjyeosseoyo

Meaning: Sorry, I threw that.

Good to know

The verb throw is Korean gaming slang for losing a game you should have won, just like in English. Players also use it for a single bad play that hands the enemy a free kill.
