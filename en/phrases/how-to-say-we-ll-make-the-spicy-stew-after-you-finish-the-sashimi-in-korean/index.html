What does "매운탕은 회 드시고 나서 끓여 드릴게요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

매운탕은 회 드시고 나서 끓여 드릴게요.

maeuntangeun hoe deusigo naseo kkeuryeo deurilgeyo

Meaning: We'll make the spicy stew after you finish the sashimi.
