What does "한번 드셔 보세요. 오늘 행사해요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

한번 드셔 보세요. 오늘 행사해요.

hanbeon deusyeo boseyo oneul haengsahaeyo

Meaning: Try one, it's on promotion today.
