How to say "I'd like to book a surfing lesson" in Korean (서핑 강습 예약하고 싶어요)

You can say this

서핑 강습 예약하고 싶어요.

seoping gangseup yeyakago sipeoyo

Meaning: I'd like to book a surfing lesson.

Good to know

Yangyang, just south of Sokcho, is the center of Korean surfing. Jukdo Beach is lined with surf shops that rent boards and wetsuits and teach beginner lessons from spring to fall.
