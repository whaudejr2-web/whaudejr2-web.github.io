How to say "What time is the changing of the guard?" in Korean (수문장 교대식은 몇 시에 해요?)

You can say this

수문장 교대식은 몇 시에 해요?

sumunjang gyodaesigeun myeot sie haeyo

Meaning: What time is the changing of the guard?

Good to know

At the largest palace in Seoul, the royal guard changes at the main gate at ten in the morning and two in the afternoon, every day the palace is open. It lasts about twenty minutes and is free to watch.
