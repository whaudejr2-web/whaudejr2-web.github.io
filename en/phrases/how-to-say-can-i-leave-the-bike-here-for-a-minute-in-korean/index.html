How to say "Can I leave the bike here for a minute?" in Korean (자전거 잠깐 여기 세워도 돼요?)

You can say this

자전거 잠깐 여기 세워도 돼요?

jajeongeo jamkkan yeogi sewodo dwaeyo

Meaning: Can I leave the bike here for a minute?
