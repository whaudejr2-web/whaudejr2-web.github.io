How to say "I can't miss the last train" in Korean (막차 놓치면 안 돼요)

You can say this

막차 놓치면 안 돼요.

makcha nochimyeon an dwaeyo

Meaning: I can't miss the last train.

Good to know

Seoul's subway stops running around midnight, and the last trains on many lines leave even earlier from the ends of the line. After that, taxis are the only option and can be hard to catch.
