What does "보증금 오백에 월세 오십이에요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

보증금 오백에 월세 오십이에요.

bojeunggeum obaege wolse osibieyo

Meaning: Five million deposit and five hundred thousand a month.

Good to know

Rent is quoted in units of ten thousand won, so five hundred means a five million won deposit and fifty means five hundred thousand a month. The deposit comes back in full when you move out.
