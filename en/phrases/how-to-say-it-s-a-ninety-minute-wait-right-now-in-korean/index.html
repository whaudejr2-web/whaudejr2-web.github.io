What does "지금 구십 분 기다리셔야 돼요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

지금 구십 분 기다리셔야 돼요.

jigeum gusip bun gidarisyeoya dwaeyo

Meaning: It's a ninety minute wait right now.

Good to know

Everland's app shows live wait times for every ride, and on busy weekends its wooden coaster T Express can show more than two hours. Check the app before you walk over.
