How to say "Please stamp my loyalty card" in Korean (쿠폰에 도장 찍어 주세요)

You can say this

쿠폰에 도장 찍어 주세요.

kupone dojang jjigeo juseyo

Meaning: Please stamp my loyalty card.

Good to know

Small independent cafés still hand out paper stamp cards. Ten stamps usually earn a free drink, so ask for the stamp before you put your receipt away.
