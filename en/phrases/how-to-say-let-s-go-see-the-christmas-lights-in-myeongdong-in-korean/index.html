How to say "Let's go see the Christmas lights in Myeongdong" in Korean (명동 크리스마스 불빛 보러 가요)

You can say this

명동 크리스마스 불빛 보러 가요.

myeongdong keuriseumaseu bulbit boreo gayo

Meaning: Let's go see the Christmas lights in Myeongdong.

Good to know

Every December the department stores around Myeongdong cover their walls in huge light shows, and crowds come at night to take photos.
