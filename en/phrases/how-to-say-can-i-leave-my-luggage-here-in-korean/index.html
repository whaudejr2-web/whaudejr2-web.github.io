How to say "Can I leave my luggage here?" in Korean (짐 좀 맡길 수 있어요?)

You can say this

짐 좀 맡길 수 있어요?

jim jom matgil su isseoyo

Meaning: Can I leave my luggage here?

Good to know

Hotels in Korea almost always store luggage for free before check-in and after checkout, usually behind the front desk. You get a tag or a number slip to claim your bags.
