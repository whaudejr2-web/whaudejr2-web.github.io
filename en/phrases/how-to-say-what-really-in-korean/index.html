How to say "What? Really?" in Korean (네? 진짜요?)

You can say this

네? 진짜요?

ne jinjjayo

Meaning: What? Really?

Good to know

The single most common reaction in Korean conversation. Stretching the vowel makes it sound more surprised; a short flat version just means I am listening.
