What does "체류 기간 만료 전에 꼭 신청하세요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

체류 기간 만료 전에 꼭 신청하세요.

cheryu gigan mallyo jeone kkok sincheonghaseyo

Meaning: Be sure to apply before your stay expires.

Good to know

You can apply for an extension up to four months before your stay expires. Overstaying is fined, and the fine grows the longer you wait, so set a reminder a month ahead.
