How to say "The fastest one, please" in Korean (제일 빠른 걸로 주세요)

You can say this

제일 빠른 걸로 주세요.

jeil ppareun geollo juseyo

Meaning: The fastest one, please.

Good to know

The KTX high-speed train covers the roughly 400 kilometers from Seoul to Busan in about two and a half hours. Slower trains on the same route cost less but take four hours or more.
