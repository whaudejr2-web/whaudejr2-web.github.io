How to say "I'm going to hang out with friends tonight" in Korean (오늘 밤에 친구들이랑 놀 거예요)

You can say this

오늘 밤에 친구들이랑 놀 거예요.

oneul bame chingudeurirang nol geoyeyo

Meaning: I'm going to hang out with friends tonight.
