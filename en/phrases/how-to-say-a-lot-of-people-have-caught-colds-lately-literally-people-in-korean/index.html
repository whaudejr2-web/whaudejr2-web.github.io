How to say "A lot of people have caught colds lately. Literally, people the cold has caught" in Korean (요즘 감기 걸린 사람이 많아요)

You can say this

요즘 감기 걸린 사람이 많아요.

yojeum gamgi geollin sarami manayo

Meaning: A lot of people have caught colds lately. Literally, people the cold has caught.
