How to say "The game is stopped because of rain" in Korean (비 와서 경기가 중단됐어요)

You can say this

비 와서 경기가 중단됐어요.

bi waseo gyeonggiga jungdandwaesseoyo

Meaning: The game is stopped because of rain.

Good to know

A game becomes official after five innings. If rain cancels it before that, tickets are refunded or exchanged, so fans check the forecast closely in the July monsoon.
