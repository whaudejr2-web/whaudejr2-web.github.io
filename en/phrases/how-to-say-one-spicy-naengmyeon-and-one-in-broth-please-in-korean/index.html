How to say "One spicy naengmyeon and one in broth, please" in Korean (비빔냉면 하나랑 물냉면 하나 주세요)

You can say this

비빔냉면 하나랑 물냉면 하나 주세요.

bibimnaengmyeon hanarang mullaengmyeon hana juseyo

Meaning: One spicy naengmyeon and one in broth, please.
