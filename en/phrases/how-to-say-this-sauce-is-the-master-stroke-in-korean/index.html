How to say "This sauce is the master stroke" in Korean (이 소스가 신의 한 수예요)

You can say this

이 소스가 신의 한 수예요.

i soseuga sinui han suyeyo

Meaning: This sauce is the master stroke.

Good to know

Literally a move of god, a term from the board game Go for one brilliant move that decides the game. Food vloggers use it for the single ingredient that makes the whole dish.
