How to say "Season it with salted shrimp" in Korean (새우젓 넣어서 드세요)

You can say this

새우젓 넣어서 드세요.

saeujeot neoeoseo deuseyo

Meaning: Season it with salted shrimp.

Good to know

Pork soup is seasoned with tiny fermented salted shrimp instead of plain salt. A small spoonful seasons the whole bowl and takes away any pork smell.
