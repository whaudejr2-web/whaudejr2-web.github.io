How to say "Where is the taxi stand?" in Korean (택시 타는 곳이 어디예요?)

You can say this

택시 타는 곳이 어디예요?

taeksi taneun gosi eodiyeyo

Meaning: Where is the taxi stand?

Good to know

The taxi stand is right outside the arrivals hall. The ride to central Seoul takes about an hour, and the expressway toll is added on top of the meter fare.
