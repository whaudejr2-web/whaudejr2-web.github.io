How to say "What's the speed limit?" in Korean (제한 속도가 몇이에요?)

You can say this

제한 속도가 몇이에요?

jehan sokdoga myeochieyo

Meaning: What's the speed limit?

Good to know

Since 2021, the limit on main city roads is 50 kilometers per hour and 30 on residential side streets. Speed cameras are announced by the navigation, and fines reach you through the rental company.
