How to say "Which team do you root for?" in Korean (어느 팀 응원해요?)

You can say this

어느 팀 응원해요?

eoneu tim eungwonhaeyo

Meaning: Which team do you root for?

Good to know

The KBO League has ten teams, each named after a company and tied to a home city: the Samsung Lions in Daegu, the Lotte Giants in Busan, the LG Twins and Doosan Bears in Seoul.
