How to say "I'll order through a delivery app" in Korean (배달 앱으로 시킬게요)

You can say this

배달 앱으로 시킬게요.

baedal aebeuro sikilgeyo

Meaning: I'll order through a delivery app.

Good to know

Baemin, launched in 2010, is the largest delivery app; its full name means the people of delivery. Coupang Eats, run by the e-commerce company Coupang, grew on the promise of one rider per order.
