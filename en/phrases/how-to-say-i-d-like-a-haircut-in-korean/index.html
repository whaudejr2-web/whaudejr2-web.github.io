How to say "I'd like a haircut" in Korean (커트하려고요)

You can say this

커트하려고요.

keoteuharyeogoyo

Meaning: I'd like a haircut.

Good to know

A basic cut at a neighborhood salon in Seoul usually runs about 15,000 to 30,000 won. Price boards list men's and women's cuts separately, and the women's price is usually higher.
