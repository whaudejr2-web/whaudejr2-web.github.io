How to say "Here's your change" in Korean (거스름돈 받으세요)

You can say this

거스름돈 받으세요.

geoseureumdon badeuseyo

Meaning: Here's your change.

Good to know

Tipping is not a custom in Korea, so you take all of your change. Restaurant and taxi prices already include everything, and leaving extra money can confuse the staff.
