How to say "Want me to make you some citron tea? It's good for your throat" in Korean (유자차 타 줄까요? 목에 좋아요)

You can say this

유자차 타 줄까요? 목에 좋아요.

yujacha ta julkkayo moge joayo

Meaning: Want me to make you some citron tea? It's good for your throat.

Good to know

Citron tea is made by stirring a spoonful of thick citron and sugar preserve into hot water. Jars are sold in most Korean supermarkets, and many people drink it for a sore throat.
