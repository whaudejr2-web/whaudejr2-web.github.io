How to say "Take my picture at the summit stone, please" in Korean (정상석에서 사진 찍어 주세요)

You can say this

정상석에서 사진 찍어 주세요.

jeongsangseogeseo sajin jjigeo juseyo

Meaning: Take my picture at the summit stone, please.

Good to know

Every major Korean peak has a stone marker carved with its name and height, and on weekends hikers line up to pose with it. Hand your phone to the next person in line and they will take your photo.
