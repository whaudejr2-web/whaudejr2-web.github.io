How to say "I'm an only child" in Korean (저는 외동이에요)

You can say this

저는 외동이에요.

jeoneun oedongieyo

Meaning: I'm an only child.

Good to know

Korea has the world's lowest birth rate, about 0.75 children per woman in 2024, so being an only child is common among young Koreans. Saying it rarely draws a surprised reaction.
