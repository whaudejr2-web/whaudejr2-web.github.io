How to say "I'd like to open an account" in Korean (계좌를 만들고 싶어요)

You can say this

계좌를 만들고 싶어요.

gyejwareul mandeulgo sipeoyo

Meaning: I'd like to open an account.

Good to know

To open an account you usually need a passport and an alien registration card. New accounts start as limited accounts with low daily transfer caps until you show proof of income or purpose.
