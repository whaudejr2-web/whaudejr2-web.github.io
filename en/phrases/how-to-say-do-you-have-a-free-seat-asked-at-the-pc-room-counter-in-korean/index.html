How to say "Do you have a free seat? Asked at the PC room counter" in Korean (피시방 자리 있어요?)

You can say this

피시방 자리 있어요?

pisibang jari isseoyo

Meaning: Do you have a free seat? Asked at the PC room counter.

Good to know

Korea has thousands of PC rooms open around the clock. You pay by the hour, usually one to two thousand won, for a high end machine, and most kitchens send ramen or fried rice straight to your seat.
