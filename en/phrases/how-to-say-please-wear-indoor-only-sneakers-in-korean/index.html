How to say "Please wear indoor-only sneakers" in Korean (실내용 운동화 꼭 신으세요)

You can say this

실내용 운동화 꼭 신으세요.

sillaeyong undonghwa kkok sineuseyo

Meaning: Please wear indoor-only sneakers.

Good to know

Many Korean gyms require a separate pair of sneakers that never touches the street. You change shoes in the locker room, just like taking them off at home.
