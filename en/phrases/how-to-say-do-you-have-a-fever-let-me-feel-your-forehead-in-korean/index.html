How to say "Do you have a fever? Let me feel your forehead" in Korean (열 있어요? 이마 좀 만져 볼게요)

You can say this

열 있어요? 이마 좀 만져 볼게요.

yeol isseoyo ima jom manjyeo bolgeyo

Meaning: Do you have a fever? Let me feel your forehead.
