How to say "Author, please give us the next episode fast! A reader comment" in Korean (작가님, 다음 화 빨리 주세요)

You can say this

작가님, 다음 화 빨리 주세요!

jakganim daeum hwa ppalli juseyo

Meaning: Author, please give us the next episode fast! A reader comment.

Good to know

Readers address the creator with a respectful word for author, and begging for the next episode is the most common comment under a cliffhanger. Creators sometimes answer in the next author's note.
