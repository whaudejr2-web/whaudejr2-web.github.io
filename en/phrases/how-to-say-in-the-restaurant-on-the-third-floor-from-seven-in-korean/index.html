What does "삼 층 식당에서 일곱 시부터예요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

삼 층 식당에서 일곱 시부터예요.

sam cheung sikdangeseo ilgop sibuteoyeyo

Meaning: In the restaurant on the third floor, from seven.
