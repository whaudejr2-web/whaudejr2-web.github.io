How to say "I need an interpreter" in Korean (통역이 필요해요)

You can say this

통역이 필요해요.

tongyeogi piryohaeyo

Meaning: I need an interpreter.

Good to know

The Korea Travel Hotline, 1330, answers 24 hours a day in English, Chinese, Japanese and other languages, and can stay on the line to interpret while you deal with the police or an ambulance crew.
