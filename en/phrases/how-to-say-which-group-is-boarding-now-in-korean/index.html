How to say "Which group is boarding now?" in Korean (지금 몇 번 그룹 탑승해요?)

You can say this

지금 몇 번 그룹 탑승해요?

jigeum myeot beon geurup tapseunghaeyo

Meaning: Which group is boarding now?
