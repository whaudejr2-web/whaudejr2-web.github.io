How to say "The internal medicine clinic nearby should still be open" in Korean (근처 내과 아직 문 열었을 거예요)

You can say this

근처 내과 아직 문 열었을 거예요.

geuncheo naegwa ajik mun yeoreosseul geoyeyo

Meaning: The internal medicine clinic nearby should still be open.

Good to know

Neighborhood clinics in Korea generally take walk-in patients, and for a cold or flu most people go to an internal medicine clinic. Many are open on Saturday mornings.
