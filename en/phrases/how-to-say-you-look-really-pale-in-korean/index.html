How to say "You look really pale" in Korean (얼굴이 많이 안 좋아 보여요)

You can say this

얼굴이 많이 안 좋아 보여요.

eolguri mani an joa boyeoyo

Meaning: You look really pale.

Good to know

Literally: your face looks really bad. In Korean this is a caring remark that someone looks unwell or tired, not a comment on their looks.
