How to say "What's the expiration date?" in Korean (유통기한이 언제까지예요?)

You can say this

유통기한이 언제까지예요?

yutonggihani eonjekkajiyeyo

Meaning: What's the expiration date?

Good to know

In 2023 Korea switched food labels from a sell-by date to a use-by date, which is usually later. Both words still appear on packages, so check which one is printed.
