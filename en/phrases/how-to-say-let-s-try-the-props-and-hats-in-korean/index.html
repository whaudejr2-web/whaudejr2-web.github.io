How to say "Let's try the props and hats" in Korean (소품이랑 모자 써 봐요)

You can say this

소품이랑 모자 써 봐요.

sopumirang moja sseo bwayo

Meaning: Let's try the props and hats.

Good to know

Booth shops keep a free rack of headbands, sunglasses and hats by the door, and many have a mirror and hair tools for a quick touch-up. Put everything back for the next group.
