How to say "Where do I put my shoes?" in Korean (신발은 어디에 넣어요?)

You can say this

신발은 어디에 넣어요?

sinbareun eodie neoeoyo

Meaning: Where do I put my shoes?

Good to know

Shoes come off right at the entrance and go into a small numbered locker. At many places you hand that shoe locker key to the front desk and get the key for a clothing locker in return.
