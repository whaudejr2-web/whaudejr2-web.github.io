How to say "It's okay" in Korean (괜찮아요)

You can say this

괜찮아요.

gwaenchanayo

Meaning: It's okay.

Good to know

This one phrase covers it's okay, no thanks, and I'm fine. Koreans use it to decline politely, to answer are you hurt, and to reassure someone, so you will hear it constantly.
