What does "양식은 킬로에 사만 원이에요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

양식은 킬로에 사만 원이에요.

yangsigeun killoe saman wonieyo

Meaning: Farmed is forty thousand won a kilo.

Good to know

Market fish is sold live and priced by weight. The vendor weighs it in front of you, and wild-caught fish of the same kind can cost double the farmed price.
