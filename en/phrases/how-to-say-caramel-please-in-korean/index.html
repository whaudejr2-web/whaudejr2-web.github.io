How to say "Caramel, please" in Korean (카라멜 맛으로 주세요)

You can say this

카라멜 맛으로 주세요.

karamel maseuro juseyo

Meaning: Caramel, please.

Good to know

Caramel, onion and cheese are the standard popcorn flavors, and many counters let you mix two. Since 2008 Korean theaters are not allowed to ban outside snacks either.
