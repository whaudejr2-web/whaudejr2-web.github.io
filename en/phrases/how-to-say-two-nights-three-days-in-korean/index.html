How to say "Two nights, three days" in Korean (이박 삼일이요)

You can say this

이박 삼일이요.

ibak samiriyo

Meaning: Two nights, three days.

Good to know

Koreans describe a stay as nights plus days, so two nights is two nights, three days. Booking sites and tour packages in Korea use the same format.
