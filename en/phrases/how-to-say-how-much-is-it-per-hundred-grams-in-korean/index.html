How to say "How much is it per hundred grams?" in Korean (백 그램에 얼마예요?)

You can say this

백 그램에 얼마예요?

baek geuraeme eolmayeyo

Meaning: How much is it per hundred grams?

Good to know

Meat and fish are priced per hundred grams, not per kilo. A tag that says two thousand won looks cheap until you realize that is for a hundred grams.
