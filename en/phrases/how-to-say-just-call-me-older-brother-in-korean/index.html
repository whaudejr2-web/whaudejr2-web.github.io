What does "그냥 오빠라고 불러." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

그냥 오빠라고 불러.

geunyang opparago bulleo

Meaning: Just call me older brother.

Good to know

The word for older brother depends on who is speaking: women use this one, men use another. The same goes for older sister. Close friends use these titles too, not only family.
