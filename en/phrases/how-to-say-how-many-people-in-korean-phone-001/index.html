What does "몇 분이세요?" mean in Korean?

You will hear this

몇 분이세요?

myeot buniseyo

Meaning: How many people?

Good to know

Staff count customers with an honorific counter for people. When you answer about your own group, use the plain counter, as in the next sentence.
