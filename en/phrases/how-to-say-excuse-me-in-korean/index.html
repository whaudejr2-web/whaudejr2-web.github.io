How to say "Excuse me" in Korean (여기요)

You can say this

여기요!

yeogiyo

Meaning: Excuse me!

Good to know

Calling out to staff is normal and not rude in Korea. Many restaurants also have a call button on the table; press it and a server comes, so there is no need to wave or wait for eye contact.
