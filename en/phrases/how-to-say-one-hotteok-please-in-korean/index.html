How to say "One hotteok, please" in Korean (호떡 하나 주세요)

You can say this

호떡 하나 주세요.

hotteok hana juseyo

Meaning: One hotteok, please.

Good to know

A fried pancake filled with brown sugar, cinnamon and nuts. The filling comes out as hot liquid sugar, so wait a moment before you bite. Busan has a famous version stuffed with seeds.
