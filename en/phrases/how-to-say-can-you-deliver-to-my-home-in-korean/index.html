How to say "Can you deliver to my home?" in Korean (집으로 배송돼요?)

You can say this

집으로 배송돼요?

jibeuro baesongdwaeyo

Meaning: Can you deliver to my home?

Good to know

Big marts deliver your in-store purchase to your door the same day, usually free above a minimum spend. You book a time slot at the delivery desk near the exit.
