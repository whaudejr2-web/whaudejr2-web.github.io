How to say "I want to do a couple look" in Korean (커플룩 하고 싶어요)

You can say this

커플룩 하고 싶어요.

keopeulluk hago sipeoyo

Meaning: I want to do a couple look.

Good to know

Matching couple outfits are a common sight in Korea, from identical T-shirts to matching sneakers. Couples often wear them for photos or to mark their hundredth day together.
