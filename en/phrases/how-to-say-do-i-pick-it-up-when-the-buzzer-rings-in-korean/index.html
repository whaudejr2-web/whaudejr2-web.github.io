How to say "Do I pick it up when the buzzer rings?" in Korean (진동벨 울리면 가져가면 돼요?)

You can say this

진동벨 울리면 가져가면 돼요?

jindongbel ullimyeon gajyeogamyeon dwaeyo

Meaning: Do I pick it up when the buzzer rings?

Good to know

Most cafés hand you a vibrating pager. When it buzzes, collect your drink at the pick-up counter.
