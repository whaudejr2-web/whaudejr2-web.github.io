How to say "I'm freezing to death" in Korean (얼어 죽겠어요)

You can say this

얼어 죽겠어요.

eoreo jukgesseoyo

Meaning: I'm freezing to death.

Good to know

Seoul winters regularly drop below minus 10 degrees Celsius, and the weather service issues cold wave alerts on the coldest days. Saying you are freezing to death is a very common everyday expression.
