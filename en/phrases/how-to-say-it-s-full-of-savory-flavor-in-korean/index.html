How to say "It's full of savory flavor" in Korean (감칠맛이 있어요)

You can say this

감칠맛이 있어요.

gamchilmasi isseoyo

Meaning: It's full of savory flavor.

Good to know

This is the Korean word for umami. Korean cooking gets it from fermented soybean paste, fish sauce and anchovy broth, which is why a simple stew can taste so deep.
