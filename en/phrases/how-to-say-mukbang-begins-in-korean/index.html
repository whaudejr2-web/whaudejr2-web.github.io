How to say "Mukbang begins" in Korean (먹방 시작)

You can say this

먹방 시작!

meokbang sijak

Meaning: Mukbang begins!

Good to know

Short for eating broadcast. It started on Korean live streams around 2010, spread through variety shows, and in 2021 the Oxford English Dictionary added the word along with twenty-five other Korean terms.
