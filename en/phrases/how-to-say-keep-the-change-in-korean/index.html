How to say "Keep the change" in Korean (잔돈은 괜찮아요)

You can say this

잔돈은 괜찮아요.

jandoneun gwaenchanayo

Meaning: Keep the change.

Good to know

Tipping is not expected in Korea, and drivers hand back every coin. Saying this over a few hundred won is fine, but nobody will think badly of you for taking your change.
