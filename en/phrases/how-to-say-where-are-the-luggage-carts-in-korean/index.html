How to say "Where are the luggage carts?" in Korean (카트는 어디 있어요?)

You can say this

카트는 어디 있어요?

kateuneun eodi isseoyo

Meaning: Where are the luggage carts?

Good to know

Luggage carts at Incheon Airport are free. You will find them lined up next to the baggage carousels, so there is no need to carry everything by hand.
