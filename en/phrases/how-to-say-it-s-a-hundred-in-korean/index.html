How to say "It's a hundred" in Korean (백 점이에요)

You can say this

백 점이에요!

baek jeomieyo

Meaning: It's a hundred!

Good to know

The machine scores every song, and the number is famously random. Some places reward a perfect hundred with a few free extra minutes, so people cheer when it appears.
