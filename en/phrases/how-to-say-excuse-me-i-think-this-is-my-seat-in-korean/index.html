How to say "Excuse me, I think this is my seat" in Korean (저기, 여기 제 자리인 것 같은데요)

You can say this

저기, 여기 제 자리인 것 같은데요.

jeogi yeogi je jariin geot gateundeyo

Meaning: Excuse me, I think this is my seat.

Listen and repeat
