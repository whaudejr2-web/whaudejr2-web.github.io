How to say "It's already late. I should head home" in Korean (벌써 늦었네요. 저 이제 집에 가야 돼요)

You can say this

벌써 늦었네요. 저 이제 집에 가야 돼요.

beolsseo neujeonneyo. jeo ije jibe gaya dwaeyo

Meaning: It's already late. I should head home.
