How to say "I didn't book ahead, are there seats left?" in Korean (예매 안 했는데 자리 있어요?)

You can say this

예매 안 했는데 자리 있어요?

yemae an haenneunde jari isseoyo

Meaning: I didn't book ahead, are there seats left?
