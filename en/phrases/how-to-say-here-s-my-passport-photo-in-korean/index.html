How to say "Here's my passport photo" in Korean (여권 사진 여기 있어요)

You can say this

여권 사진 여기 있어요.

yeogwon sajin yeogi isseoyo

Meaning: Here's my passport photo.

Good to know

The photo must be 3.5 by 4.5 centimeters, in color, on a white background and taken within the last six months. Most offices have a photo booth near the entrance.
