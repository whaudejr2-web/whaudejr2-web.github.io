How to say "A month's supply of daily contact lenses, please" in Korean (일회용 렌즈 한 달 치 주세요)

You can say this

일회용 렌즈 한 달 치 주세요.

ilhoeyong renjeu han dal chi juseyo

Meaning: A month's supply of daily contact lenses, please.

Good to know

Korean law bans selling contact lenses online, so you buy them in person at an optical shop or eye clinic. Bring your current box; staff will match the brand and power or run a quick test.
