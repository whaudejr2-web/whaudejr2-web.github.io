How to say "I don't eat pork" in Korean (돼지고기 안 먹어요)

You can say this

돼지고기 안 먹어요.

dwaejigogi an meogeoyo

Meaning: I don't eat pork.

Good to know

Pork is the most common meat in Korean cooking, and small pieces go into many stews, dumplings and fried rice without being listed. Say this clearly before you order.
