How to say "Do I have to order on the kiosk?" in Korean (키오스크로 주문해야 돼요?)

You can say this

키오스크로 주문해야 돼요?

kioseukeuro jumunhaeya dwaeyo

Meaning: Do I have to order on the kiosk?

Good to know

Touch-screen kiosks have spread to even tiny snack bars in Korea. Most have a language button in a corner of the screen, and many take cards only, no cash.
