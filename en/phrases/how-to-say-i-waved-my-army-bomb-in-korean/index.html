How to say "I waved my ARMY Bomb" in Korean (아미밤 흔들었어요)

You can say this

아미밤 흔들었어요.

amibam heundeureosseoyo

Meaning: I waved my ARMY Bomb.

Good to know

The official BTS light stick is called the ARMY Bomb. At concerts it can be synced so the whole stadium changes color at the same time.
