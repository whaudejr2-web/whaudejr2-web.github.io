How to say "Once you see a big church, you're almost there" in Korean (큰 교회가 보이면 다 온 거예요)

You can say this

큰 교회가 보이면 다 온 거예요.

keun gyohoega boimyeon da on geoyeyo

Meaning: Once you see a big church, you're almost there.

Good to know

Many Koreans give directions by landmarks such as convenience stores, banks or churches. Many churches have a red neon cross on the roof that is easy to spot at night.
