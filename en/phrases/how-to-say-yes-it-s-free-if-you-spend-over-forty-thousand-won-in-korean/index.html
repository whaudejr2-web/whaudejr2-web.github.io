How to say "Yes, it's free if you spend over forty thousand won" in Korean (네, 사만 원 이상이면 무료예요)

You can say this

네, 사만 원 이상이면 무료예요.

ne saman won isangimyeon muryoyeyo

Meaning: Yes, it's free if you spend over forty thousand won.
