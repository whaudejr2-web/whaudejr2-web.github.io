How to say "I'm sorry" in Korean (죄송합니다)

You can say this

죄송합니다.

joesonghamnida

Meaning: I'm sorry.

Good to know

This is the standard apology for strangers, staff and anyone older than you. Koreans usually lower their head a little as they say it.
