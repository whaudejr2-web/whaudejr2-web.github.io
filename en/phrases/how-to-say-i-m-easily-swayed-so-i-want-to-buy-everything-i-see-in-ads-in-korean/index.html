How to say "I'm easily swayed, so I want to buy everything I see in ads. Literally, my ears are thin" in Korean (저는 귀가 얇아서 광고를 보면 다 사고 싶어요)

You can say this

저는 귀가 얇아서 광고를 보면 다 사고 싶어요.

jeoneun gwiga yalbaseo gwanggoreul bomyeon da sago sipeoyo

Meaning: I'm easily swayed, so I want to buy everything I see in ads. Literally, my ears are thin.
