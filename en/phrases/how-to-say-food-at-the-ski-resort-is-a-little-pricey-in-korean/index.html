How to say "Food at the ski resort is a little pricey" in Korean (스키장 음식은 좀 비싸요)

You can say this

스키장 음식은 좀 비싸요.

seukijang eumsigeun jom bissayo

Meaning: Food at the ski resort is a little pricey.
