How to say "Let's meet at Gangnam Station, exit two" in Korean (강남역 2번 출구에서 봐요)

You can say this

강남역 2번 출구에서 봐요.

gangnamyeok ibeon chulgueseo bwayo

Meaning: Let's meet at Gangnam Station, exit two.

Good to know

First dates in Seoul usually start at a numbered subway exit, so people name the station and the exit number. The numbers are posted above every stairway.
