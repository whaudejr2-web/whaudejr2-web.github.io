What does "통신사 할인 있으세요?" mean in Korean?

You will hear this

통신사 할인 있으세요?

tongsinsa harin isseuseyo

Meaning: Do you have a phone carrier discount?

Good to know

Korean phone carriers such as SKT and KT have membership apps with cinema discounts, so staff ask about it at every sale. Visitors without a Korean plan can simply say no.
