How to say "Could you call a taxi for me?" in Korean (택시 좀 불러 주시겠어요?)

You can say this

택시 좀 불러 주시겠어요?

taeksi jom bulleo jusigesseoyo

Meaning: Could you call a taxi for me?

Good to know

Hotel staff usually order a taxi through the Kakao T app, which most Koreans use. In Seoul, regular taxis are orange or silver; black ones are deluxe taxis and cost more.
