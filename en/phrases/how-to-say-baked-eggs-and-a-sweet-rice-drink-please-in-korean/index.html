How to say "Baked eggs and a sweet rice drink, please" in Korean (맥반석 계란하고 식혜 주세요)

You can say this

맥반석 계란하고 식혜 주세요.

maekbanseok gyeranhago sikye juseyo

Meaning: Baked eggs and a sweet rice drink, please.

Good to know

Eggs slow baked on heated stone until the whites turn brown, washed down with a cold sweet rice drink, are the classic sauna snack. Locals crack the egg on their forehead, or on a friend's.
