How to say "Please top up my T-money card" in Korean (티머니 충전해 주세요)

You can say this

티머니 충전해 주세요.

timeoni chungjeonhae juseyo

Meaning: Please top up my T-money card.

Good to know

You can buy and top up a T-money transit card at most convenience stores, but the top-up is cash only. Credit cards are not accepted for loading money.
