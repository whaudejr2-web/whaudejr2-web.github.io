How to say "I'm swamped these days. Literally, no time to open my eyes or nose" in Korean (요즘 눈코 뜰 새 없이 바빠요)

You can say this

요즘 눈코 뜰 새 없이 바빠요.

yojeum nunko tteul sae eopsi bappayo

Meaning: I'm swamped these days. Literally, no time to open my eyes or nose.
