How to say "What's the date today?" in Korean (오늘 며칠이에요?)

You can say this

오늘 며칠이에요?

oneul myeochirieyo

Meaning: What's the date today?

Good to know

Dates use Sino-Korean numbers, and Koreans say the year first, then the month, then the day. Written dates follow the same order, from the biggest unit to the smallest.
