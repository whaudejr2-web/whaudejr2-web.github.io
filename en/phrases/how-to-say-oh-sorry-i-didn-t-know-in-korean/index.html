How to say "Oh, sorry, I didn't know" in Korean (아, 죄송해요. 몰랐어요)

You can say this

아, 죄송해요. 몰랐어요.

a joesonghaeyo mollasseoyo

Meaning: Oh, sorry, I didn't know.
