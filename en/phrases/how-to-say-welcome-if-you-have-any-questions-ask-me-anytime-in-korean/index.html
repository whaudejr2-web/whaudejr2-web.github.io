How to say "Welcome. If you have any questions, ask me anytime" in Korean (환영해요. 궁금한 거 있으면 언제든지 물어보세요)

You can say this

환영해요. 궁금한 거 있으면 언제든지 물어보세요.

hwanyeonghaeyo. gunggeumhan geo isseumyeon eonjedeunji mureoboseyo

Meaning: Welcome. If you have any questions, ask me anytime.
