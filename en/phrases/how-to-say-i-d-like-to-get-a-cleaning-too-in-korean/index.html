How to say "I'd like to get a cleaning too" in Korean (스케일링도 받고 싶어요)

You can say this

스케일링도 받고 싶어요.

seukeillingdo batgo sipeoyo

Meaning: I'd like to get a cleaning too.

Good to know

Since 2017, national insurance covers one cleaning per year for everyone nineteen and older, and the patient's share is only around fifteen thousand won. The benefit resets every first of January.
