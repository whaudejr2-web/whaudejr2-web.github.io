How to say "Spring is cherry blossom season" in Korean (봄은 벚꽃 시즌이에요)

You can say this

봄은 벚꽃 시즌이에요.

bomeun beotkkot sijeunieyo

Meaning: Spring is cherry blossom season.

Good to know

Cherry blossoms usually peak in Seoul in early April and last only about a week. Jinhae, in the south, hosts the country's largest blossom festival, drawing millions of visitors.
