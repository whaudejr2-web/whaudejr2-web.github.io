How to say "Send me the photo. Casual" in Korean (사진 보내 줘)

You can say this

사진 보내 줘.

sajin bonae jwo

Meaning: Send me the photo. Casual.

Good to know

Requests change too: the polite please-give ending becomes a short casual one. Shop staff and public announcements never use the casual form with customers.
