How to say "How much is a basket of tangerines?" in Korean (귤 한 바구니에 얼마예요?)

You can say this

귤 한 바구니에 얼마예요?

gyul han bagunie eolmayeyo

Meaning: How much is a basket of tangerines?

Good to know

Nearly all of Korea's tangerines grow on Jeju. Harvest runs from November to January, and many farms let you pick your own and eat as many as you like for a small fee.
