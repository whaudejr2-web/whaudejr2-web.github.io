How to say "Where is the police station?" in Korean (경찰서가 어디예요?)

You can say this

경찰서가 어디예요?

gyeongchalseoga eodiyeyo

Meaning: Where is the police station?

Good to know

You do not need a main station. Small neighborhood police posts are open 24 hours and take theft and loss reports, and the tourist police keep booths in the main tourist districts of Seoul.
