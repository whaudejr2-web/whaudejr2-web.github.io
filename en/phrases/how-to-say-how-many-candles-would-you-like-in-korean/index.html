What does "초는 몇 개 드릴까요?" mean in Korean?

You will hear this

초는 몇 개 드릴까요?

choneun myeot gae deurilkkayo

Meaning: How many candles would you like?

Good to know

Candles come free with the cake. Long candles count as ten years each and short ones as one year, so for a 25th birthday you ask for two long and five short.
