How to say "I'm going to study Korean hard" in Korean (한국어 공부 열심히 할 거예요)

You can say this

한국어 공부 열심히 할 거예요.

hangugeo gongbu yeolsimhi hal geoyeyo

Meaning: I'm going to study Korean hard.
