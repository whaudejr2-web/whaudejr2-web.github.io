How to say "How do I use the convenience store ramyeon cooker?" in Korean (편의점 라면 기계는 어떻게 써요?)

You can say this

편의점 라면 기계는 어떻게 써요?

pyeonuijeom ramyeon gigyeneun eotteoke sseoyo

Meaning: How do I use the convenience store ramyeon cooker?

Good to know

Many convenience stores have a self-serve noodle cooker. Buy a packet, empty it into the paper bowl provided, set it on the machine and press start; it adds water and boils it in about four minutes.
