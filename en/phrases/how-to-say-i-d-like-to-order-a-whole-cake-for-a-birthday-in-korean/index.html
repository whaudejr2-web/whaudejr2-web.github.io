How to say "I'd like to order a whole cake for a birthday" in Korean (생일 케이크로 홀케이크 하나 주문할게요)

You can say this

생일 케이크로 홀케이크 하나 주문할게요.

saengil keikeuro holkeikeu hana jumunhalgeyo

Meaning: I'd like to order a whole cake for a birthday.
