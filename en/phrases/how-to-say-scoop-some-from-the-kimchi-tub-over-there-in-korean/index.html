How to say "Scoop some from the kimchi tub over there" in Korean (저기 김치통에서 덜어 가세요)

You can say this

저기 김치통에서 덜어 가세요.

jeogi gimchitongeseo deoreo gaseyo

Meaning: Scoop some from the kimchi tub over there.
