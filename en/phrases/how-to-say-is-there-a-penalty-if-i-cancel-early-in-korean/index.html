How to say "Is there a penalty if I cancel early?" in Korean (중간에 해지하면 위약금 있어요?)

You can say this

중간에 해지하면 위약금 있어요?

junggane haejihamyeon wiyakgeum isseoyo

Meaning: Is there a penalty if I cancel early?
