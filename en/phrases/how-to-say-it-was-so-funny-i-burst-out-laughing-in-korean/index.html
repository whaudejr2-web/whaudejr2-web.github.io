How to say "It was so funny I burst out laughing" in Korean (너무 웃겨서 깔깔 웃었어요)

You can say this

너무 웃겨서 깔깔 웃었어요.

neomu utgyeoseo kkalkkal useosseoyo

Meaning: It was so funny I burst out laughing.
