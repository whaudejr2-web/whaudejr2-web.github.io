How to say "A senior is treating us to dinner" in Korean (선배가 밥 사 준대요)

You can say this

선배가 밥 사 준대요.

seonbaega bap sa jundaeyo

Meaning: A senior is treating us to dinner.

Good to know

Seniors are expected to pick up the bill when they eat with juniors. Juniors pay it forward by buying for the students who enter after them.
