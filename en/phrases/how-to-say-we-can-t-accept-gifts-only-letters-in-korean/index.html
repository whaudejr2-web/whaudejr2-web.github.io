What does "선물은 못 받아요. 편지만 받아요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

선물은 못 받아요. 편지만 받아요.

seonmureun mot badayo pyeonjiman badayo

Meaning: We can't accept gifts, only letters.

Good to know

Several big agencies, including HYBE and JYP, no longer accept fan gifts for their artists. Letters are still collected in a box at the venue, so the letter is often the one thing you can really hand over.
