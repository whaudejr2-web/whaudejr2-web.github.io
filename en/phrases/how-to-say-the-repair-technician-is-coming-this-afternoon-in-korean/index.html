How to say "The repair technician is coming this afternoon" in Korean (오늘 오후에 수리 기사가 와요)

You can say this

오늘 오후에 수리 기사가 와요.

oneul ohue suri gisaga wayo

Meaning: The repair technician is coming this afternoon.
