How to say "Where is the hot kiln room?" in Korean (불가마는 어디예요?)

You can say this

불가마는 어디예요?

bulgamaneun eodiyeyo

Meaning: Where is the hot kiln room?

Good to know

The hottest room is a dome of clay and stone heated like a kiln, and the air inside can pass eighty degrees Celsius. Regulars go in for a few minutes at a time, then cool off and drink water.
