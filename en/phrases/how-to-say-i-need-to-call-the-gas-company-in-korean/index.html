How to say "I need to call the gas company" in Korean (가스 회사에 전화해야 돼요)

You can say this

가스 회사에 전화해야 돼요.

gaseu hoesae jeonhwahaeya dwaeyo

Meaning: I need to call the gas company.

Good to know

When you move in, the city gas company sends a technician to connect the stove and boiler and run a safety check. You book the visit by phone or online, and someone must be home.
