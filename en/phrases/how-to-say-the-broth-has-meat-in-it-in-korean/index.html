What does "육수에 고기가 들어가요." mean in Korean?

You will hear this

육수에 고기가 들어가요.

yuksue gogiga deureogayo

Meaning: The broth has meat in it.

Good to know

Many Korean soups and noodle dishes start with anchovy and kelp stock or beef bone broth, so a dish that looks like only vegetables and noodles usually is not vegetarian. The broth is the key question to ask.
