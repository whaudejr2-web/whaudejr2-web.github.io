How to say "We lock it at midnight, so let me know in advance if you'll be late" in Korean (자정에 잠그니까 늦으시면 미리 말씀해 주세요)

You can say this

자정에 잠그니까 늦으시면 미리 말씀해 주세요.

jajeonge jamgeunikka neujeusimyeon miri malsseumhae juseyo

Meaning: We lock it at midnight, so let me know in advance if you'll be late.
