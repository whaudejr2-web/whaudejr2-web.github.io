How to say "Where is the lost and found?" in Korean (유실물 센터가 어디예요?)

You can say this

유실물 센터가 어디예요?

yusilmul senteoga eodiyeyo

Meaning: Where is the lost and found?

Good to know

Seoul Metro keeps lost and found centers at a few major stations, and anything handed in to the police anywhere in Korea is listed on the national LOST112 website, which has an English version.
