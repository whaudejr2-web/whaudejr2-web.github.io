Asking Questions in Korean

Ask any question in Korean

42 phrases

  • 저기요, 뭐 좀 물어봐도 돼요?Excuse me, may I ask you something?
  • 네, 말씀하세요.Sure, go ahead.
  • 궁금한 게 있어요.There's something I'm curious about.
  • 이름이 뭐예요?What's your name?
  • 어디에 살아요?Where do you live?
  • 이게 뭐예요?What is this?
  • 이건 떡이에요.This is rice cake.
  • 이거 누가 만들었어요?Who made this?
  • 저 사람은 누구예요?Who is that person?
  • 제 친구예요.That's my friend.
  • 이거 누구 거예요?Whose is this?
  • 언제 시작해요?When does it start?
  • 몇 시에 끝나요?What time does it end?
  • 얼마나 걸려요?How long does it take?
  • 장소가 어디예요?Where is the venue?
  • 얼마나 멀어요?How far is it?
  • 어느 쪽이에요?Which way is it?
  • 왜요?Why?
  • 왜 안 돼요?Why not?
  • 왜 그래요?What's wrong?
  • 정말이에요?Really? Is that true?
  • 무슨 뜻이에요?What does that mean?
  • 무슨 말이에요?What do you mean?
  • 어떻게 생각해요?What do you think?
  • 어떻게 알았어요?How did you know?
  • 어느 게 더 좋아요?Which one is better?
  • 이거요.This one.
  • 하나에 얼마예요?How much for one?
  • 몇 개 있어요?How many are there?
  • 모두 몇 명이에요?How many people in total?
  • 펜 있어요?Do you have a pen?
  • 네, 여기요.Yes, here you go.
  • 들어가도 돼요?Can I come in?
  • 이렇게 해도 괜찮아요?Is it okay if I do it like this?
  • 네, 괜찮아요.Yes, that's fine.
  • 좀 도와주실 수 있어요?Could you help me a little?
  • 그럼요, 뭐 도와드릴까요?Of course, what can I help you with?
  • 음, 저도 잘 몰라요.Hmm, I don't know either.
  • 하나만 더 물어볼게요.Just one more question.
  • 질문 있어요?Any questions?
  • 아니요, 이제 됐어요.No, I'm all set now.
  • 대답해 주셔서 감사해요.Thank you for answering.
