  • 사장님이 추천해 주셨어요.The owner recommended it.
  • 냄새 너무 좋아요.It smells so good.
  • 냄새부터 맛있어요.Even the smell is delicious.
  • 한번 먹어 볼게요.Let me try it.
  • 한입 크게 먹을게요.I'm taking a big bite.
  • 소리 들리세요?Can you hear that sound?
  • 미쳤어요, 너무 맛있어요.This is insane, it's so good.
  • 겉은 바삭해요.The outside is crispy.
  • 속은 촉촉해요.The inside is moist.
  • 쫄깃쫄깃해요.It's nice and chewy.
  • 육즙이 팡팡 터져요.The juices burst right out.
  • 입에서 살살 녹아요.It melts in your mouth.
  • 고소해요.It's nutty and rich.
  • 감칠맛이 있어요.It's full of savory flavor.
  • 달콤해요.It's sweet.
  • 새콤해요.It's pleasantly sour.
  • 단짠단짠이에요.It's sweet and salty, sweet and salty.
  • 좀 짜요.It's a bit salty.
  • 좀 싱거워요.It's a bit bland.
  • 느끼해요.It's greasy.
  • 뜨거워요, 조심하세요.It's hot, be careful.
  • 매운데 자꾸 손이 가요.It's spicy, but I keep reaching for more.

  • 깔끔해요.It's clean and light.

  • 멈출 수가 없어요.I can't stop.
  • 십 점 만점에 십 점이에요.Ten out of ten.
  • 인생 맛집이에요.This is the best restaurant of my life.
  • 여러분, 이거 꼭 드셔 보세요.Everyone, you have to try this.
