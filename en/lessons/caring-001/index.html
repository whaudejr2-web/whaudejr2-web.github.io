Taking Care of Someone Who Is Sick

Care for a sick friend in Korean

Free PDF: 40 phrases for your first day in KoreaNew Korean listening practice every day on YouTube

40 phrases

  • 괜찮아요? 어디 아파요?Are you okay? Are you feeling sick?
  • 얼굴이 많이 안 좋아 보여요.You look really pale.
  • 몸살 났나 봐요. 온몸이 쑤셔요.I think I'm coming down with something. My whole body aches.
  • 몸이 으슬으슬 추워요.I've got the chills.
  • 식은땀도 나요.I'm breaking out in a cold sweat too.
  • 열 있어요? 이마 좀 만져 볼게요.Do you have a fever? Let me feel your forehead.
  • 이마가 너무 뜨거워요.Your forehead is really hot.
  • 약은 먹었어요?Did you take any medicine?
  • 아직이요. 집에 약이 없어요.Not yet. I don't have any at home.
  • 제가 편의점에서 해열제 사 올게요.I'll go get you some fever medicine at the convenience store.
  • 병원에 가 보는 게 좋겠어요.I think you should see a doctor.
  • 괜찮아요. 하룻밤 자면 나을 거예요.I'm fine. I'll be better after a good night's sleep.
  • 안 돼요. 제가 같이 가 줄게요.No way. I'll go with you.
  • 근처 내과 아직 문 열었을 거예요.The internal medicine clinic nearby should still be open.
  • 독감이래요. 며칠 푹 쉬래요.It's the flu. The doctor says to rest for a few days.
  • 집에 가서 푹 쉬어요.Go home and get plenty of rest.
  • 따뜻한 물 많이 마셔요.Drink lots of warm water.
  • 이따가 죽 사 갈게요.I'll bring you some porridge later.
  • 죽 사 왔어요. 따뜻할 때 먹어요.I brought you porridge. Eat it while it's warm.
  • 입맛이 하나도 없어요.I have no appetite at all.
  • 그래도 몇 숟가락만 먹어요.Still, have just a few spoonfuls.
  • 먹고 나서 약 꼭 먹어요.Make sure you take your medicine after you eat.
  • 유자차 타 줄까요? 목에 좋아요.Want me to make you some citron tea? It's good for your throat.
  • 내일 출근해야 하는데요.But I have to go to work tomorrow.
  • 아플 때는 쉬어도 괜찮아요.It's okay to rest when you're sick.
  • 팀장님한테는 제가 말해 둘게요.I'll let the team leader know.
  • 일 걱정은 하지 말고 무리하지 마요.Don't worry about work, and don't push yourself.
  • 오늘은 아무것도 하지 말고 쉬어요.Take it easy today and don't do anything.
  • 필요한 거 있으면 언제든지 전화해요.Call me anytime if you need anything.
  • 저 진짜 걱정돼요.I'm really worried about you.
  • 챙겨 줘서 정말 고마워요.Thank you so much for looking after me.
  • 얼른 나아요.Get well soon.
  • 오늘은 못 가서 죽 배달시켰어요.I can't come over today, so I had porridge delivered to you.
  • 방금 죽 받았어요. 진짜 감동이에요.The porridge just arrived. I'm so touched.
  • 좀 어때요? 열은 내렸어요?How are you feeling? Did your fever go down?
  • 덕분에 많이 나았어요.I feel a lot better, thanks to you.
  • 아직 기침은 좀 나요.I still have a bit of a cough.
  • 다 나을 때까지 조심해요.Take care of yourself until you're fully better.
  • 다 나으면 제가 밥 살게요.Once I'm better, I'll treat you to a meal.
  • 좋아요. 약속했어요!Deal. You promised!
