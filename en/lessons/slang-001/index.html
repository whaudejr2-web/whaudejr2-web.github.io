40 phrases

  • 요즘 젊은 사람들 말이 너무 어려워요.Young people's slang is so hard these days.
  • 하나 가르쳐 주세요.Teach me one.
  • 불금인데 뭐 해요?It's party Friday, what are you up to?
  • 그게 무슨 뜻이에요?What does that mean?
  • 줄임말이에요.It's an abbreviation.
  • 오늘은 혼밥 할래요.I'll eat solo today.
  • 이 식당 가성비 최고예요.This restaurant is the best value for money.
  • 저는 얼죽아예요.I'm iced americano even if I freeze to death.
  • 이게 제 소확행이에요.This is my small but certain happiness.
  • 그 친구 완전 인싸예요.That friend is a total insider, the popular type.
  • 저는 그냥 아싸예요.I'm more of an outsider.
  • 엠비티아이가 뭐예요?What's your MBTI type?
  • 우리는 찐친이에요.We're real true friends.
  • 눈치 좀 보세요.Read the room.
  • 저 요즘 썸 타는 사람 있어요.There's someone I've got a something going with.
  • 헐, 진짜요?Whoa, seriously?
  • 대박, 누구예요?No way, who is it?
  • 그건 티엠아이예요.That's TMI.
  • 그 옷 진짜 간지 나요.That outfit has real swag.
  • 꾸안꾸 스타일이에요.It's the dressed-up-but-not-dressed-up look.
  • 어제 플렉스 했어요.I flexed yesterday, I splurged.
  • 그 영화 완전 꿀잼이에요.That movie is honey-level fun.
  • 그건 진짜 노잼이에요.That one is a total no-fun.
  • 와, 이거 쩔어요!Wow, this is sick!
  • 이건 친구끼리만 써요.This one is only for friends.
  • 회사에서는 쓰지 마세요.Don't use it at work.
  • 그건 케바케예요.That's case by case.
  • 갑분싸 됐어요.The mood suddenly went cold.
  • 멘붕 왔어요.I'm having a mental breakdown.
  • 갑자기 현타 왔어요.Reality just hit me.
  • 요즘 엔잡 하는 사람 많아요.Lots of people juggle N jobs these days.
  • 워라밸이 중요해요.Work-life balance matters.
  • 올해는 갓생 살 거예요.This year I'm going to live a god-tier life.
  • 제 좌우명은 중꺾마예요.My motto is: what matters is an unbreakable heart.
  • 완전 럭키비키잖아요.Lucky Vicky, everything works out for me.
  • 그 말 요즘 많이 써요?Is that word used a lot these days?
  • 그거 이미 유행 지났어요.That one's already out of style.
  • 저도 한번 써 볼게요.I'll try using it too.
  • 오, 완전 한국 사람 같아요.Oh, you sound totally Korean.
  • 요즘은 진짜 별다줄이에요.These days they really shorten every little thing.
