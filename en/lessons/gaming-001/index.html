Korean Gaming Terms and Chat

41 phrases

  • 피시방 자리 있어요?Do you have a free seat? Asked at the PC room counter.
  • 두 시간 충전해 주세요.Two hours, please. Said at the counter.
  • 자리에서 라면 주문할 수 있어요?Can I order ramen from my seat?
  • 무슨 게임 해요?What game do you play?
  • 저는 롤 해요.I play League of Legends.
  • 랭크 돌릴래요?Want to play some ranked?
  • 티어가 어떻게 돼요?What's your tier?
  • 저 초보예요.I'm a beginner.
  • 튜토리얼부터 할게요.I'll start with the tutorial.
  • 이 캐릭터 처음 해 봐요.This is my first time playing this character.
  • 저는 서포터 할게요.I'll play support.
  • 잘 부탁드려요.Nice to meet you, let's play well together. Typed at the start of a match.
  • 고고!Let's go!
  • 저 좀 캐리해 주세요.Carry me, please.
  • 모여요.Group up.
  • 빠져요!Fall back!
  • 힐 좀 주세요.Heal me, please.
  • 저 죽었어요.I'm dead.
  • 부활 기다리고 있어요.I'm waiting to respawn.
  • 킬 땄어요!I got a kill!
  • 나이스!Nice!
  • 렉 걸려요.I'm lagging.
  • 핑이 너무 높아요.My ping is too high.
  • 미안해요, 제가 던졌어요.Sorry, I threw that.
  • 탓하지 마세요.Don't blame people.
  • 팀워크가 중요해요.Teamwork matters.
  • 매너 좀 지켜요.Mind your manners.
  • 저 사람 신고할게요.I'm reporting that player.
  • 잠깐 자리 비울게요.I'll be away from the keyboard for a second.
  • 마이크 켜도 돼요?Can I turn on my mic?
  • 소리가 안 들려요.I can't hear any sound.
  • 레벨 업 했어요!I leveled up!
  • 좋은 게임이었어요.Good game.
  • 플레이 진짜 좋았어요.That was great play.
  • 한 판 더 해요.One more game.
  • 이게 진짜 마지막 판이에요.This is really the last game.
  • 시간 추가할게요.I'll add more time. Said at the PC room counter.
  • 재밌게 했어요. 고마워요.That was fun, thanks.
  • 친구 추가해도 돼요?Can I add you as a friend?
  • 내일 같은 시간에 해요.Same time tomorrow.
  • 저 먼저 갈게요.I'm heading out first.
