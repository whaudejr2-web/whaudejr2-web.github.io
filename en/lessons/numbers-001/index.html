Numbers and Prices in Korean

Korean numbers that actually matter

Free PDF: 40 phrases for your first day in KoreaNew Korean listening practice every day on YouTube

40 phrases

  • 하나, 둘, 셋.One, two, three. These are the numbers for counting things and people.
  • 넷, 다섯, 여섯.Four, five, six.
  • 일곱, 여덟, 아홉, 열.Seven, eight, nine, ten.
  • 몇 명이세요?How many people?
  • 두 명이에요.There are two of us.
  • 맥주 한 병 주세요.One bottle of beer, please.
  • 커피 세 잔 주세요.Three coffees, please.
  • 이거 네 개 주세요.Four of these, please.
  • 하나 더 주세요.One more, please.
  • 스물다섯 살이에요.I'm twenty five years old.
  • 일, 이, 삼.One, two, three. These are the numbers for prices and phone numbers.
  • 사, 오, 육.Four, five, six.
  • 칠, 팔, 구, 십.Seven, eight, nine, ten.
  • 백, 천, 만.One hundred, one thousand, ten thousand.
  • 이거 얼마예요?How much is this?
  • 천 원이에요.It's one thousand won.
  • 오천 원이에요.It's five thousand won.
  • 만 원이에요.It's ten thousand won.
  • 만 오천 원이에요.It's fifteen thousand won.
  • 오만 원이에요.It's fifty thousand won.
  • 십만 원이에요.It's one hundred thousand won.
  • 두 개에 만 원이에요.It's two for ten thousand won.
  • 너무 비싸요.That's too expensive.
  • 좀 깎아 주세요.Can you make it a little cheaper?
  • 천 원만 깎아 주세요.Just take one thousand won off, please.
  • 할인 돼요?Is there a discount?
  • 이십 퍼센트 할인이에요.It's twenty percent off.
  • 현금으로 하면 싸요?Is it cheaper if I pay cash?
  • 다 해서 얼마예요?How much is it all together?
  • 전부 삼만 원이에요.It's thirty thousand won in total.
  • 거스름돈 받으세요.Here's your change.
  • 반만 주세요.Just half, please.
  • 가격이 두 배예요.The price is double.
  • 전화번호가 어떻게 되세요?What's your phone number?
  • 제 전화번호는 공일공, 일이삼사, 오육칠팔이에요.My phone number is zero one zero, one two three four, five six seven eight.
  • 삼백이 호예요.It's room three oh two.
  • 백사십삼 번 버스 타세요.Take bus number one forty three.
  • 육 번 출구로 나오세요.Come out through exit six.
  • 몇 층이에요?What floor is it on?
  • 삼 층이에요.It's on the third floor.
