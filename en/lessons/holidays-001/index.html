Korean Holidays: Seollal and Chuseok

42 phrases

  • 새해 복 많이 받으세요.Happy New Year.
  • 새해에도 건강하세요.Stay healthy in the new year.
  • 설날은 음력 일월 일일이에요.Seollal is the first day of the first lunar month.
  • 설 연휴에 뭐 해요?What are you doing over the Seollal holiday?
  • 고향에 내려가요.I'm going down to my hometown.
  • 명절에는 차가 많이 막혀요.Traffic is terrible during the holidays.
  • 기차표 구했어요?Did you get a train ticket?
  • 기차표가 벌써 매진됐어요.The train tickets are already sold out.
  • 버스로 가야겠어요.I guess I'll take the bus.
  • 저는 그냥 서울에 있을 거예요.I'm just staying in Seoul.
  • 연휴에 서울은 조용해요.Seoul is quiet during the holiday.
  • 가게가 다 문 닫았어요.All the shops are closed.
  • 편의점은 열었어요.The convenience store is open.
  • 설 선물 세트 샀어요?Did you buy a Seollal gift set?
  • 부모님께 드릴 거예요.It's for my parents.
  • 온 가족이 모였어요.The whole family got together.
  • 한복 입으니까 예쁘네요.You look lovely in hanbok.
  • 세배 드릴게요.Let me do the New Year's bow.
  • 올해도 건강하시고 복 많이 받으세요.Wishing you health and lots of luck this year too. Said while bowing.
  • 세뱃돈 받았어요!I got New Year's money!
  • 얼마 받았어요?How much did you get?
  • 떡국 먹었어요?Did you have rice cake soup?
  • 떡국 한 그릇 더 주세요.One more bowl of rice cake soup, please.
  • 차례 지냈어요.We held the ancestral rite.
  • 음식 준비하느라 힘들었어요.Preparing all the food was exhausting.
  • 전 부치는 거 도와드릴게요.I'll help with frying the pancakes.
  • 즐거운 한가위 보내세요.Have a happy Chuseok.
  • 풍성한 한가위 되세요.Have an abundant Chuseok.
  • 송편 같이 만들어요.Let's make songpyeon together.
  • 보름달 보면서 소원 빌어요.Make a wish while looking at the full moon.
  • 달이 정말 크네요.The moon is really big.
  • 명절 잘 보내세요.Have a good holiday.
  • 명절 잘 보내셨어요?Did you have a good holiday?
  • 연휴 동안 뭐 했어요?What did you do over the holiday?
  • 집에서 푹 쉬었어요.I just rested at home.
  • 친척들이 결혼은 언제 하냐고 물어봤어요.My relatives asked when I'm getting married.
  • 명절 인사 문자 보냈어요.I sent holiday greeting texts.
  • 행복한 설 보내세요.Have a happy Seollal. A typical text message greeting.
  • 내일부터 다시 출근이에요.Back to work from tomorrow.
  • 연휴가 너무 짧았어요.The holiday was way too short.
  • 명절에 살이 좀 쪘어요.I gained a little weight over the holiday.
  • 다음 명절에 또 봐요.See you next holiday.
