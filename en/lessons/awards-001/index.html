Award Shows and Acceptance Speeches

What idols say when they win an award

Free PDF: 40 phrases for your first day in KoreaNew Korean listening practice every day on YouTube

42 phrases

  • 오늘 투표 꼭 해 주세요.Please make sure you vote today.
  • 저는 벌써 투표했어요.I already voted.
  • 시상식 같이 볼래요?Do you want to watch the award show together?
  • 레드카펫 시작했어요!The red carpet has started!
  • 우리 애들 후보에 올랐어요.Our kids got nominated.
  • 너무 떨려요. 상 받았으면 좋겠어요.I'm so nervous. I really hope they win.
  • 수상자를 발표하겠습니다.I will now announce the winner, says the host.
  • 축하드립니다!Congratulations, says the host.
  • 무대 위로 올라와 주세요.Please come up on stage, says the host.
  • 이름 불렀어요! 진짜 받았어요!They called the name! They really won!
  • 먼저 이렇게 큰 상 주셔서 정말 감사합니다.First of all, thank you so much for such a big award.
  • 정말 예상 못 했어요.I really didn't expect this.
  • 이 상은 팬분들 덕분이에요.This award is all thanks to our fans.
  • 우리 팬분들 정말 사랑합니다.We really love our fans.
  • 대표님께 감사드립니다.I want to thank our CEO.
  • 회사 식구분들 감사합니다.Thank you to everyone at our company.
  • 고생해 주신 스태프분들께도 감사드립니다.I also want to thank all the staff who worked so hard.
  • 우리 멤버들 정말 고마워요.Thank you so much, my members.
  • 엄마, 아빠, 사랑해요.Mom, Dad, I love you.
  • 울지 않으려고 했는데 눈물이 나네요.I tried not to cry, but here come the tears.
  • 죄송해요, 말이 잘 안 나와요.I'm sorry, I can't get the words out.
  • 힘든 시간도 있었는데요.There were hard times too.
  • 포기하지 않길 잘했어요.I'm so glad we didn't give up.
  • 데뷔했을 때가 생각나요.It reminds me of the day we debuted.
  • 첫 대상이라 더 의미가 있어요.It's our first grand prize, so it means even more.
  • 앞으로 더 좋은 음악으로 보답하겠습니다.We will repay you with even better music.
  • 팬분들도 항상 건강하세요.Fans, please always stay healthy too.
  • 전 세계 팬분들 감사합니다.Thank you to our fans all over the world.
  • 영어로 한마디 할게요.Let me say a few words in English.
  • 오래오래 함께해요.Let's stay together for a long, long time.
  • 다시 한번 감사합니다.Thank you once again.
  • 너무 자랑스러워요.I'm so proud of them.
  • 저도 같이 울었어요.I cried along with them.
  • 소감 진짜 감동이었어요.The speech was really touching.
  • 소감 영어 자막 있어요?Are there English subtitles for the speech?
  • 받을 만했어요.They totally deserved it.
  • 무대도 레전드였어요.The performance was legendary too.
  • 직캠 봤어요?Did you see the fancam?
  • 영상 저장해 놨어요.I saved the video.
  • 대상 축하해요!Congrats on the grand prize!
  • 내년에도 같이 시상식 봐요.Let's watch the award show together next year too.
  • 아직도 여운이 남아요.I still can't get over it.
