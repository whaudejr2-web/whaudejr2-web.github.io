Birthdays and Parties

Celebrate a birthday like a local

Free PDF: 40 phrases for your first day in KoreaNew Korean listening practice every day on YouTube

43 phrases

  • 생일이 며칠이에요?What date is your birthday?
  • 이번 주 금요일이에요.It's this Friday.
  • 다음 주가 제 생일이에요.My birthday is next week.
  • 생일 축하드려요.Happy birthday. Polite, for someone older than you.
  • 생일 축하해!Happy birthday! Casual, to a close friend.
  • 아침에 미역국 먹었어요?Did you have seaweed soup this morning?
  • 엄마가 끓여 주셨어요.My mom made it for me.
  • 생일에 뭐 하고 싶어요?What do you want to do on your birthday?
  • 선물로 뭐 받고 싶어요?What do you want as a gift?
  • 마음만으로도 충분해요.Just the thought is enough.
  • 토요일에 제 생일 파티 할 건데 올래요?I'm having my birthday party on Saturday. Want to come?
  • 네, 꼭 갈게요.Yes, I'll definitely be there.
  • 친구 데려와도 돼요?Can I bring a friend?
  • 네, 많이 데려오세요.Sure, bring lots of people.
  • 파티룸 빌렸어요.I rented a party room.
  • 몇 시까지 가면 돼요?What time should I be there?
  • 일곱 시까지 오세요.Come by seven.
  • 깜짝 파티 준비했어요.We prepared a surprise party.
  • 비밀이에요. 말하면 안 돼요.It's a secret. Don't tell.
  • 초대해 줘서 고마워요.Thanks for inviting me.
  • 카톡으로 기프티콘 보냈어요.I sent you a gift voucher on KakaoTalk.
  • 생일 선물이에요. 열어 보세요.It's your birthday present. Open it.
  • 선물 고마워요. 정말 마음에 들어요.Thanks for the gift. I really love it.
  • 케이크 가져왔어요.I brought a cake.
  • 초 몇 개 꽂을까요?How many candles should I put on?
  • 생일 축하합니다.Happy birthday to you. The words of the Korean birthday song.
  • 불 켰어요. 소원 빌어요!The candles are lit. Make a wish!
  • 소원 빌었어요?Did you make a wish?
  • 이제 촛불 끄세요.Now blow out the candles.
  • 셋 세면 불어요. 하나, 둘, 셋!Blow on three. One, two, three!
  • 같이 사진 찍어요!Let's take a picture together!
  • 케이크 자를게요.I'll cut the cake.
  • 자, 건배해요!Come on, let's toast!
  • 오늘의 주인공이에요.You're the star of the day.
  • 이제 몇 살이에요?How old are you now?
  • 한 살 더 먹었네요.You're a year older now.
  • 스물다섯 살 됐어요.I just turned twenty-five.
  • 이차 가요!On to round two!
  • 이따가 노래방 가요!Let's go to karaoke after this!
  • 오늘 너무 즐거웠어요.I had so much fun today.
  • 최고의 생일이었어요.It was the best birthday ever.
  • 다들 와 줘서 정말 고마워요.Thank you all so much for coming.
  • 내년 생일도 같이 보내요.Let's spend next year's birthday together too.
