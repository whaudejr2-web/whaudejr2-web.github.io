40 phrases before you land in Korea

40 phrases

  • 안녕하세요.Hello.
  • 반갑습니다.Nice to meet you.
  • 제 이름은 안나예요.My name is Anna.
  • 저는 관광객이에요.I'm a tourist.
  • 한국은 처음이에요.It's my first time in Korea.
  • 감사합니다.Thank you.
  • 고맙습니다.Thank you. This is another common way to say it.
  • 죄송합니다.I'm sorry.
  • 잠시만요.Excuse me, just a second.
  • 저기요.Excuse me, to get someone's attention.
  • 네.Yes.
  • 아니요.No.
  • 이거 주세요.This one, please.
  • 괜찮아요.It's okay.
  • 잘 모르겠어요.I don't understand.
  • 한국어 잘 못해요.I don't speak Korean well.
  • 천천히 말해 주세요.Please speak slowly.
  • 다시 한번 말해 주세요.Please say that again.
  • 영어 할 줄 아세요?Do you speak English?
  • 이거 뭐예요?What is this?
  • 여기에 써 주세요.Please write it here.
  • 도와주세요.Please help me.
  • 하나, 둘, 셋.One, two, three.
  • 이거 하나 주세요.One of these, please.
  • 두 개 주세요.Two, please.
  • 세 명이에요.There are three of us.
  • 얼마예요?How much is it?
  • 천 원이에요.It's one thousand won.
  • 만 원이에요.It's ten thousand won.
  • 너무 비싸요.That's too expensive.
  • 카드 돼요?Do you take cards?
  • 화장실 어디예요?Where is the restroom?
  • 여기 어떻게 가요?How do I get to this place?
  • 잘 먹겠습니다.Thank you for the meal, said before eating.
  • 맛있어요.It's delicious.
  • 건배!Cheers!
  • 한국이 좋아요.I like Korea.
  • 안녕히 계세요.Goodbye, said to someone who is staying.
  • 안녕히 가세요.Goodbye, said to someone who is leaving.
  • 다음에 또 봐요.See you next time.
