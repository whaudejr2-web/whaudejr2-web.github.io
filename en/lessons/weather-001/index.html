Weather and Seasons

Weather small talk in Korean

Free PDF: 40 phrases for your first day in KoreaNew Korean listening practice every day on YouTube

42 phrases

  • 오늘 날씨 어때요?How's the weather today?
  • 날씨가 좋아요.The weather is nice.
  • 날씨가 안 좋아요.The weather is bad.
  • 따뜻해요.It's warm.
  • 시원해요.It's nice and cool.
  • 너무 더워요.It's so hot.
  • 너무 습해요.It's so humid.
  • 땀이 많이 나요.I'm sweating a lot.
  • 밤에도 더워요.It's hot even at night.
  • 햇빛이 강해요.The sun is strong.
  • 선크림 바르세요.Put on sunscreen.
  • 비가 와요.It's raining.
  • 우산 있어요?Do you have an umbrella?
  • 우산을 안 가져왔어요.I didn't bring an umbrella.
  • 칠월은 장마철이에요.July is the rainy season.
  • 비가 그쳤어요.The rain stopped.
  • 내일 비 온대요.They say it'll rain tomorrow.
  • 비 맞았어요.I got caught in the rain.
  • 추워요.It's cold.
  • 얼어 죽겠어요.I'm freezing to death.
  • 쌀쌀해요.It's chilly.
  • 눈이 와요.It's snowing.
  • 첫눈이 왔어요!The first snow fell!
  • 길이 미끄러워요.The road is slippery.
  • 따뜻하게 입으세요.Dress warmly.
  • 겉옷 챙기세요.Bring a jacket.
  • 바람이 많이 불어요.It's really windy.
  • 오늘 미세먼지가 심해요.The fine dust is bad today.
  • 마스크 쓰세요.Wear a mask.
  • 오늘은 공기가 맑아요.The air is clear today.
  • 어느 계절을 좋아해요?What season do you like?
  • 저는 봄을 제일 좋아해요.I like spring the most.
  • 봄은 벚꽃 시즌이에요.Spring is cherry blossom season.
  • 한국은 사계절이 있어요.Korea has four seasons.
  • 가을에는 단풍이 예뻐요.The autumn leaves are beautiful.
  • 여름은 덥고 겨울은 추워요.Summer is hot and winter is cold.
  • 겨울에는 눈이 와요.It snows in winter.
  • 날씨 좋은데 나가요.The weather's nice, let's go out.
  • 너무 더워서 나가기 싫어요.It's too hot, I don't want to go out.
  • 일기예보 봤어요?Did you check the forecast?
  • 내일은 추워진대요.They say it'll get cold tomorrow.
  • 날씨가 자주 바뀌어요.The weather changes a lot.
