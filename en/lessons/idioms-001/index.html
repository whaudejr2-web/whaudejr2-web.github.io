Korean Idioms You Hear Every Day

Korean idioms you hear every day

41 phrases

  • 발등에 불 떨어졌어요.I'm in a real crunch now. Literally, fire dropped on the top of my foot.
  • 내일까지 보고서를 내야 돼요.I have to turn in the report by tomorrow.
  • 요즘 눈코 뜰 새 없이 바빠요.I'm swamped these days. Literally, no time to open my eyes or nose.
  • 점심 먹을 시간도 없어요.I don't even have time for lunch.
  • 우리 팀은 손발이 잘 맞아요.Our team works well together. Literally, our hands and feet match.
  • 시작이 반이에요.Getting started is half the job. Literally, the start is half.
  • 운동하기로 마음먹었어요.I've made up my mind to exercise. Literally, I ate my mind.
  • 내일부터 매일 아침에 뛸 거예요.Starting tomorrow I'm running every morning.
  • 민수 씨는 발이 정말 넓어요.Minsu knows everyone. Literally, his feet are wide.
  • 아는 사람이 진짜 많아요.He really knows a lot of people.
  • 제 친구는 입이 무거워요.My friend can keep a secret. Literally, her mouth is heavy.
  • 비밀 얘기해도 괜찮아요.It's fine to tell her secrets.
  • 저는 귀가 얇아서 광고를 보면 다 사고 싶어요.I'm easily swayed, so I want to buy everything I see in ads. Literally, my ears are thin.
  • 저희 엄마는 손이 커요.My mom is generous. Literally, her hands are big.
  • 항상 음식을 너무 많이 해요.She always makes way too much food.
  • 언니는 눈이 높아요.My older sister has high standards. Literally, her eyes are high.
  • 그래서 아직 남자 친구가 없어요.That's why she still doesn't have a boyfriend.
  • 국수는 언제 먹여 줄 거예요?When are you getting married? Literally, when will you feed us noodles?
  • 아직 결혼 생각 없어요.I'm not thinking about marriage yet.
  • 어제 소개팅에서 바람맞았어요.I got stood up on a blind date yesterday. Literally, I was hit by the wind.
  • 한 시간이나 기다렸어요.I waited a whole hour.
  • 그래서 마음이 무거워요.So I feel down. Literally, my heart is heavy.
  • 걱정 마세요. 마음 놓으세요.Don't worry, you can relax. Literally, put your heart down.
  • 시험에서 미역국 먹었어요.I failed the exam. Literally, I ate seaweed soup.
  • 김칫국부터 마시지 마세요.Don't count your chickens before they hatch. Literally, don't drink the kimchi soup first.
  • 아직 합격한 거 아니에요.You haven't passed yet.
  • 그 회사 들어가기는 하늘의 별 따기예요.Getting into that company is nearly impossible. Literally, picking a star from the sky.
  • 그 일은 식은 죽 먹기예요.That job is a piece of cake. Literally, eating cold porridge.
  • 저도 이제 나이를 많이 먹었어요.I've gotten pretty old too. Literally, I've eaten a lot of age.
  • 요즘 감기 걸린 사람이 많아요.A lot of people have caught colds lately. Literally, people the cold has caught.
  • 감기 걸리지 않게 조심하세요.Be careful not to catch a cold.
  • 택배를 목이 빠지게 기다렸어요.I waited forever for my package. Literally, until my neck fell out.
  • 드디어 오늘 왔어요.It finally came today.
  • 시장에서 바가지 썼어요.I got ripped off at the market. Literally, I wore a gourd bowl.
  • 같은 거를 다른 데서는 반값에 팔아요.They sell the same thing for half the price elsewhere.
  • 그 사람은 가면을 쓴 것 같아요.I think he's hiding his real self. Literally, he seems to be wearing a mask.
  • 진짜 속마음을 모르겠어요.I can't tell what he really feels.
  • 친구들이 다 등을 돌렸어요.All his friends turned their backs on him.
  • 거짓말을 너무 많이 했거든요.He told way too many lies, you see.
  • 사촌이 땅을 사면 배가 아프대요.People get jealous of others' success. Literally, when a cousin buys land, your stomach hurts.
  • 친구가 승진했는데 솔직히 배가 좀 아파요.My friend got promoted and honestly I'm a bit jealous. Literally, my stomach hurts a little.
