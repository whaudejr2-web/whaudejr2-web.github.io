Korean Etiquette Phrases

Be polite like a Korean

41 phrases

  • 앗, 죄송합니다. 못 봤어요.Oops, I'm sorry, I didn't see you.
  • 괜찮아요. 저도 죄송해요.It's okay, sorry on my part too.
  • 여기 앉으세요.Please, take this seat. Offering your seat to an elder.
  • 처음 뵙겠습니다. 잘 부탁드립니다.Nice to meet you, I look forward to getting to know you.
  • 인사가 참 바르네요.What nice manners you have.
  • 실례지만 몇 년생이세요?Excuse me, what year were you born?
  • 저는 구십팔 년생이에요.I was born in ninety-eight.
  • 그럼 저보다 두 살 위시네요.Then you're two years older than me.
  • 언니라고 불러도 돼요?Can I call you unni?
  • 형이라고 부를게요.I'll call you hyung.
  • 민수 씨, 이쪽으로 오세요.Minsu, please come this way.
  • 신발은 신발장에 넣어 주세요.Please put your shoes in the shoe cabinet.
  • 신발은 여기서 벗을게요.I'll take my shoes off here.
  • 저희 나라에서 가져온 선물이에요.It's a gift from my country.
  • 이거 별거 아닌데 받아 주세요.This is nothing much, but please accept it.
  • 아이고, 뭘 이런 걸 다.Oh my, you didn't have to.
  • 두 손으로 드릴게요.I'll hand it to you with both hands.
  • 어른한테는 두 손으로 드려야 돼요.You should give things to elders with both hands. A tip from a friend.
  • 어른이 먼저 드시면 시작해요.We start after the elders begin eating.
  • 먼저 드세요.Please, go ahead and eat.
  • 잘 먹겠습니다.Thank you for the meal, I'll enjoy it. Said before eating.
  • 제가 따라 드릴게요.Let me pour that for you.
  • 잔 좀 들어 주세요.Please hold up your glass.
  • 어른 앞에서는 고개 돌리고 마셔요.In front of elders, turn your head away when you drink. A tip from a friend.
  • 젓가락을 밥에 꽂으면 안 돼요.Don't stick your chopsticks upright in the rice.
  • 식탁에서는 코 풀면 안 돼요.You shouldn't blow your nose at the table.
  • 잠깐 화장실 좀 다녀올게요.Excuse me, I'll be right back from the restroom.
  • 사장님, 여기 좀 봐 주세요.Excuse me, over here please. Calling the owner.
  • 이모, 여기 김치 좀 더 주세요.Auntie, could we get more kimchi here?
  • 많이 드셨어요? 더 드세요.Did you eat enough? Have some more.
  • 아니요, 배불러요. 정말 잘 먹었어요.No, I'm full, it was really wonderful.
  • 잘 먹었습니다.Thank you, that was a great meal. Said after eating.
  • 오늘 사 주셔서 감사합니다.Thank you for treating me today.
  • 먼저 가세요.After you.
  • 먼저 하세요.Please, go ahead.
  • 아, 감사합니다.Oh, thank you.
  • 초대해 주셔서 감사합니다.Thank you for inviting me.
  • 먼저 가 보겠습니다.Excuse me, I'll head out first.
  • 먼저 들어가 볼게요. 안녕히 계세요.I'll get going first, goodbye.
  • 덕분에 잘 먹고 갑니다.Thanks to you I had a lovely meal.
  • 네, 조심히 가세요.Okay, get home safe.
