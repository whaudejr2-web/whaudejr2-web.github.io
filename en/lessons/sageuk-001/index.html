Lines from Historical K-Dramas

Sound like a Joseon royal

43 phrases

  • 게 누구냐?Who goes there?
  • 이리 오너라.Come here.
  • 서두르거라!Hurry!
  • 주상 전하 납시오!His Majesty the King is coming!
  • 전하.Your Majesty.
  • 중전마마.Your Majesty the Queen.
  • 전하, 부르셨사옵니까?Your Majesty, you called for me?
  • 과인이 그대를 믿소.I trust you.
  • 그러하냐?Is that so?
  • 전하, 아뢰옵니다.Your Majesty, I have something to report.
  • 그리하거라.Do so.
  • 명을 받들겠사옵니다.I will obey your command.
  • 예, 전하.Yes, Your Majesty.
  • 알겠느냐?Do you understand?
  • 예, 명심하겠사옵니다.Yes, I will keep it in mind.
  • 성은이 망극하옵니다.Your grace is boundless. The way to say it is an honor.
  • 황공하옵니다.I am humbled, Your Majesty.
  • 송구하옵니다.I am deeply sorry, Your Majesty.
  • 아니 되옵니다!You must not, Your Majesty!
  • 통촉하여 주시옵소서.I beg you to reconsider, Your Majesty.
  • 어명이다!It is the king's command!
  • 네 이놈, 무엄하다!How dare you, you insolent man!
  • 감히!How dare you! Literally: so boldly.
  • 어찌 그런 말씀을 하시옵니까?How can you say such a thing?
  • 마마, 고정하시옵소서.Please calm yourself, Your Majesty.
  • 참으로 다행이옵니다.Truly, what a relief.
  • 이는 역모이옵니다!This is treason, Your Majesty!
  • 여봐라, 저놈을 끌어내라!Guards, drag that man out!
  • 오라를 받아라!Take the rope! Said when arresting someone.
  • 네 죄를 네가 알렸다!You know your own crime!
  • 억울하옵니다!I am falsely accused!
  • 소인은 정말 몰랐사옵니다.I truly did not know, my lord.
  • 용서하여 주시옵소서.Please forgive me.
  • 부디 살려 주시옵소서.Please spare my life.
  • 전하, 죽여 주시옵소서.Kill me, Your Majesty. A dramatic way of begging forgiveness.
  • 저자를 당장 참하라!Execute that man at once!
  • 사약을 내리노라.I grant you the poison. The king's death sentence.
  • 나리.My lord. Said to an official or nobleman.
  • 대감, 큰일 났사옵니다.My lord, something terrible has happened.
  • 그대를 연모하오.I love you, in old court speech.
  • 가자꾸나.Let us go.
  • 물러가거라.You may leave.
  • 부디 몸 성히 계시옵소서.Farewell, and stay well.
