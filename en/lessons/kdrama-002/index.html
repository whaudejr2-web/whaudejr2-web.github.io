20 phrases

  • 저 사람 누구예요?Who is that person?
  • 왜 이제 왔어요?Why are you only here now?
  • 저만 믿어요.Just trust me.
  • 저랑 결혼해 줄래요?Will you marry me?
  • 그 손 놔요.Let go of that hand.
  • 이 사람 제 여자친구예요.She's my girlfriend.
  • 제 눈 봐요.Look me in the eyes.
  • 다시 시작해요.Let's start over.
  • 운명인가 봐요.It must be fate.
  • 첫눈에 반했어요.I fell for you at first sight.
  • 왜 그렇게 쳐다봐요?Why are you staring at me like that?
  • 질투해요?Are you jealous?
  • 심장이 너무 뛰어요.My heart is racing.
  • 제발요.Please, I'm begging you.
  • 저 버리지 마세요.Don't leave me.
  • 아무것도 아니에요.It's nothing.
  • 왜 울어요?Why are you crying?
  • 다 제 잘못이에요.It's all my fault.
  • 행복해야 해요.You have to be happy.
  • 꿈 아니죠?This isn't a dream, right?
