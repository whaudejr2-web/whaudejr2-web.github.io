42 phrases

  • 저 어제 지갑을 잃어버렸어요.I lost my wallet yesterday.
  • 네? 진짜요?What? Really?
  • 어머, 어떡해요.Oh no, what are you going to do?
  • 저런, 안됐네요.Oh dear, that's too bad.
  • 힘들었겠어요.That must have been stressful.
  • 그래서요?And then what?
  • 근데 경찰서에서 찾았어요.But I got it back at the police station.
  • 휴, 다행이네요.Phew, what a relief.
  • 아, 그렇구나.Oh, I see.
  • 잘됐네요.That worked out well.
  • 저 다음 주에 제주도 가요.I'm going to Jeju next week.
  • 우와, 대박!Wow, awesome!
  • 좋겠어요.Lucky you.
  • 부러워요.I'm jealous.
  • 재밌겠네요.Sounds fun.
  • 아, 그래요?Oh, is that so?
  • 네, 네.Uh-huh, go on.
  • 아, 네.Oh, okay.
  • 요즘 비행기표가 너무 비싸요.Plane tickets are so expensive these days.
  • 맞아요.Right.
  • 그러니까요.Exactly, I know.
  • 그렇죠.Yeah, that's how it is.
  • 저도요.Me too.
  • 저도 같은 생각이에요.I agree.
  • 말이 되네요.That makes sense.
  • 아, 그렇군요.I see.
  • 무슨 말인지 알겠어요.I understand what you mean.
  • 그럼요.Of course.
  • 당연하죠.Naturally, of course.
  • 그리고 저 회사 그만뒀어요.And I quit my job.
  • 네? 농담이죠?What? You're kidding, right?
  • 에이, 말도 안 돼요.Come on, no way.
  • 헐, 진짜예요?Whoa, seriously?
  • 와, 완전 미쳤네요.Wow, that's totally crazy.
  • 장난 아니네요.That's no joke.
  • 전혀 몰랐어요.I had no idea.
  • 처음 들었어요.That's the first I've heard of it.
  • 세상에.Oh my goodness.
  • 잘한 거 같아요?Do you think I did the right thing?
  • 음, 생각 좀 해 볼게요.Hmm, let me think about it.
  • 글쎄요.Well, I'm not sure.
  • 그래도 응원할게요.Still, I'm rooting for you.
