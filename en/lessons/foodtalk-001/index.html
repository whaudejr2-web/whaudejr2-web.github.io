Talking About Food You Like

Talk about food like a Korean

Free PDF: 40 phrases for your first day in KoreaNew Korean listening practice every day on YouTube

42 phrases

  • 제일 좋아하는 음식이 뭐예요?What's your favorite food?
  • 저는 떡볶이를 제일 좋아해요.I like tteokbokki the most.
  • 저는 매운 음식 좋아해요.I like spicy food.
  • 매운 거 잘 드세요?Can you handle spicy food?
  • 저는 매운 거 잘 못 먹어요.I can't really eat spicy food.
  • 저는 편식이 심해요.I'm a really picky eater.
  • 저는 아무거나 다 잘 먹어요.I eat everything.
  • 못 먹는 거 있어요?Is there anything you can't eat?
  • 저는 다 괜찮아요.I'm fine with everything.
  • 한국 음식 중에 뭐 먹어 봤어요?What Korean food have you tried?
  • 한국 음식 완전 좋아해요.I totally love Korean food.
  • 순대 먹어 봤어요?Have you tried sundae?
  • 아직 못 먹어 봤어요.I haven't tried it yet.
  • 네, 먹어 봤는데 맛있었어요.Yes, I tried it and it was good.
  • 무슨 맛이에요?What does it taste like?
  • 달아요.It's sweet.
  • 짜요.It's salty.
  • 셔요.It's sour.
  • 써요.It's bitter.
  • 느끼해요.It's greasy.
  • 담백해요.It's light and clean-tasting.
  • 고소해요.It's nutty and savory.
  • 시원해요.It's refreshing. Koreans say it even about hot soup.
  • 중독성 있어요.It's addictive.
  • 집밥 맛이에요.It tastes like home cooking.
  • 엄마가 해 준 것 같아요.It's like my mom made it.
  • 쫄깃해요.It's chewy.
  • 바삭해요.It's crunchy.
  • 부드러워요.It's soft and tender.
  • 이거 한번 먹어 보고 싶어요.I want to try this.
  • 맛집 하나만 추천해 주세요.Recommend me one good restaurant.
  • 이 동네는 뭐가 유명해요?What's this neighborhood famous for?
  • 여기는 칼국수가 유명해요.This place is famous for knife-cut noodles.
  • 맛있어요?Is it good?
  • 입에 딱 맞아요.It's exactly to my taste.
  • 그냥 그래요.It's okay.
  • 제 입맛에는 좀 안 맞아요.It doesn't quite suit my taste.
  • 너무 짜요.It's too salty.
  • 양이 너무 많아요.The portion is too big.
  • 더 못 먹겠어요.I can't eat any more.
  • 또 먹고 싶어요.I want to eat it again.
  • 다음에 같이 먹으러 가요.Let's go eat it together next time.
